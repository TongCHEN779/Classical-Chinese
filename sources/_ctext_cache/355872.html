《醫要類 Essentials of Medicine》

《醫要類·概幾 The General Incipience》

1
○岐伯曰：奇恆之府。運氣五六周甲，實二燥濕而一也；髒腑經絡概應十二，恒也；互根亢承之變，奇也。脾為後天本，腎為先天本，而腎具真水火也，交濟則火即元氣矣。三部十二動脈，分診也；寸口，巧診也；八奇，寄診也。李士材闢王叔和而遵內經。潛老夫曰：兩端代錯，而悟在其中矣。內經上下應，難經六表裏，蓋關不動，而腎命、大小腸有亙幾焉，胎交手之圓圖可徵。一氣而周三隧宗氣、榮、衛，行二道者也嗌有兩管，二陰出道。四診望、聞、問、切而八切浮、沉、遲、數、滑、澀、虛、實，而二十四脈可推也，內外寒熱虛實辨尤要矣。人迎，喉旁胃脈；氣口，兩手肺脈也。亦可分征兩手人迎、氣口，論內外，則止是外感于風，故肝木見症而人迎盛；內傷於食，故脾胃見症而氣口盛。寸關尺共九分，關前一分仍是肝脾之首耳。且論感則不拘六表裏矣。如傷寒論胸腹小腹，即內經上候上、下候下之診也。如論胃脈，則六脈之和平皆胃也，豈定執右關論胃哉？對治易曉，從治難明，況隔二三乎？天時有變，地有異候，人稟殊而養又殊，寧可執哉？要當深究三才之故，類應通幾，互格物則，而歷症以征常變，乃中節也。運氣、經絡、脈理、病症、藥性、醫方，各有常變，明其理，歷症多，自能會通易簡。若一言而終，不過調水火、順元氣耳。
○ Qibo spoke of the extraordinary and constant bowels. The five and six of the circulation of qi go round the sexagenary cycle, being in reality two dryness and damp and yet one; the viscera, bowels, channels and collaterals all correspond to twelve — this is the constant; the changes of mutual rooting, excess and control are the extraordinary. The spleen is the root of the Posterior-to-Heaven and the kidneys the root of the Prior-to-Heaven, and the kidneys contain true water and fire; when they assist each other, fire is primal qi. The twelve pulsing vessels of the three regions are diagnosis by division; the inch-mouth is skilful diagnosis; the eight extraordinary vessels are lodged diagnosis. Li Shicai attacked Wang Shuhe and followed the Inner Classic. The Old Man of Qian said: The two ends alternate, and understanding lies within them. The correspondence of above and below in the Inner Classic and the six exterior–interior pairs of the Classic of Difficulties: the bar does not move, while the kidney, Gate of Life and large and small intestines have their enduring incipience; the round diagram of the embryo's crossed hands can verify it. One qi circles through the three tunnels ancestral, nutritive and defensive qi and moves along the two paths the throat has two pipes, and the two lower orifices are the exits. With the four examinations looking, listening, asking and feeling and the eight pulse qualities floating, sinking, slow, rapid, slippery, rough, empty and full, from which the twenty-four pulses can be inferred, the distinction of inner and outer, cold and hot, empty and full is especially important. The ren ying is the stomach pulse beside the throat; the qi-mouth is the lung pulse at the two hands. One may also distinguish the ren ying and qi-mouth at the two hands; speaking of inner and outer, it is only that external contraction is from wind, so liver-wood symptoms appear and the ren ying is strong, and internal injury is from food, so spleen and stomach symptoms appear and the qi-mouth is strong. The inch, bar and cubit are nine fen in all, and the one fen before the bar is still the head of the liver and spleen. Moreover, in discussing contraction one need not be bound by the six exterior–interior pairs. For instance, the Treatise on Cold Damage on the chest, abdomen and lower abdomen is the Inner Classic's diagnosis of reading the upper above and the lower below. In discussing the stomach pulse, the harmony and balance of all six pulses is the stomach — why must one hold to the right bar for the stomach? Treatment by opposition is easy to grasp and treatment by compliance hard to understand — how much more when two or three removes apart? The seasons of Heaven change, the land has different signs, people's endowments differ and their nourishment differs too — how can one hold to one rule? One must deeply investigate the reasons of the three powers, comprehend the incipient through categorical correspondence, investigate the patterns of things mutually, and verify the constant and the changing through experience of cases; that is keeping the measure. The circulation of qi, channels and collaterals, pulse theory, illnesses, drug natures and prescriptions each have their constants and changes; understand their principles and see many cases, and one will naturally comprehend them simply and easily. To sum it up in a word, it is no more than regulating water and fire and following the primal qi.

《醫要類·中風 Wind-Stroke》

1
○冬至起坎，候八方風，從鄉來者長養，不則名為虛賊風，害萬物，虛者多從背俞中，而有中藏、中府、中血脈之分。其以火、以虛、以濕、以寒、以暑、以氣、以食、以惡而中者，類中風也。凡中風昏倒，口抉不開，以牙皂、半夏、細辛末吹鼻取嚏，無嚏則死。切辨者閉與脫：如牙關緊閉，兩手握固，是閉症也蘇合丸、三生飲開之；若口開心絕，手撒脾絕，眼合肝絕，遺尿腎絕，聲如鼾肺絕，吐沬直視，髮直搖頭，上攛面赤，汗出，皆脫症也大灌理中湯，及炙臍下，或救十一。中藏者，滯九竅，唇緩，二便閉，不能言，耳聾，鼻塞，目瞀；其中府者，肢節廢，脈浮惡風，拘急不仁，外有六經形證；中血脈者，半表裏，但見口眼喎斜，半身作痛，惟當養血順氣，大秦芄湯、羌活愈風湯和之。兼證則隨察之。
○ From the winter solstice, starting at kan, one watches the winds of the eight directions: those that come from their proper quarter nourish growth, otherwise they are called empty thief-winds, which harm all things; those who are depleted are mostly struck through the transport points of the back, and there are distinctions of striking the viscera, the bowels, or the blood vessels. Those struck by fire, depletion, damp, cold, summer heat, qi, food or malign influences have quasi-wind-stroke. Whenever someone falls unconscious from wind-stroke with jaws locked, blow powdered soap-pod, pinellia and asarum into the nose to make him sneeze; if he does not sneeze he will die. The vital distinction is between blockage and collapse: if the jaws are clenched and both hands gripped tight, it is a blockage pattern open it with Storax Pills or the Three-Raw Decoction; if the mouth is open the heart is exhausted, if the hands are limp the spleen is exhausted, if the eyes are closed the liver is exhausted, if urine is lost the kidneys are exhausted, if the voice is like snoring the lungs are exhausted, and spitting foam with a fixed stare, hair standing up and shaking head, upturned eyes, red face and sweating are all collapse patterns pour in large doses of Middle-Regulating Decoction and moxa below the navel, which may save one in ten. When the viscera are struck, the nine orifices are blocked, the lips slack, urine and stool retained, the patient cannot speak, is deaf, the nose stuffed and the eyes dim; when the bowels are struck, the limbs and joints are useless, the pulse floating with aversion to wind, cramps and numbness, with the outward signs of the six channels; when the blood vessels are struck, it is half exterior and half interior, with only a twisted mouth and eye and pain on one side of the body, and one should simply nourish the blood and smooth the qi, harmonizing with the Major Large Gentian Decoction and the Notopterygium Wind-Curing Decoction. Accompanying symptoms are examined as they arise.

《醫要類·厥 Reversal》

1
○常病陰厥，益火之原；陽厥，壯水之主：與傷寒異傷寒陽厥用推陳致新，陰厥用附子理中，冰炭殊途。陽衰陰湊，五指至膝皆寒，曰寒厥宜六物附子湯；陰衰陽湊，令人足下熱，甚則循三陰而上逆，曰熱厥宜六味地黃丸；怒火起肝，令血菀於上，曰薄厥宜蒲黃湯；煩勞擾陽，火炎水乾，至夏益亢，孤陽厥逆如煎，曰煎厥人參固本丸；五尸之氣亂人陰陽，暴厥如死，曰尸厥宜流氣飲、蘇合丸；寒痰迷悶，四肢逆冷，曰痰厥宜姜附湯；胃寒吐蛔，曰蛔厥宜烏梅丸、理中湯；中風身溫而痰涎多，中氣身冷而涎亦少，總曰氣厥宜順氣散；醉飽感傷，著惱食填，厥逆，宜探吐之。經曰：上部有脈，下部無脈，法當吐。又有氣暴逆者，氣複自愈。丹溪治內虛而僕者，炙氣海而愈。人生命門胞糸在臍，故丹田氣海與腎脈相通。
○ For ordinary yin reversal, augment the source of fire; for yang reversal, strengthen the master of water: it differs from cold damage in cold damage, yang reversal uses "pushing out the old to bring in the new" and yin reversal Aconite Middle-Regulating — as different as ice and charcoal. When yang is weak and yin gathers, the five fingers up to the knees are all cold, called cold reversal Six-Ingredient Aconite Decoction is suitable; when yin is weak and yang gathers, the soles of the feet are hot, and in severe cases it rises in counterflow along the three yin, called heat reversal Six-Ingredient Rehmannia Pills are suitable; when the fire of anger rises from the liver and makes the blood stagnate above, it is called sudden reversal Typha Pollen Decoction is suitable; when vexation and toil disturb the yang, fire blazes and water dries up, growing worse in summer, the solitary yang reversing as if boiling, it is called boiling reversal Ginseng Root-Securing Pills; when the qi of the five corpses disorders a person's yin and yang and he suddenly collapses as if dead, it is called corpse reversal Qi-Flowing Drink and Storax Pills are suitable; when cold phlegm confuses and oppresses and the four limbs are counterflow-cold, it is called phlegm reversal Ginger and Aconite Decoction is suitable; when a cold stomach vomits roundworms, it is called roundworm reversal Mume Pills and Middle-Regulating Decoction are suitable; when in wind-stroke the body is warm with much phlegm and in qi-stroke the body is cold with little phlegm, both are called qi reversal Qi-Smoothing Powder is suitable; when after drinking and eating one's fill there is contraction and injury, vexation and food stuffing, with reversal counterflow, one should induce vomiting. The Classic says: When there is a pulse in the upper region and none in the lower, the rule is to induce vomiting. There are also cases of sudden counterflow of qi which recover of themselves when the qi returns. Danxi treated someone who collapsed from inner depletion by moxa at Qihai, and he recovered. The placental cord of the Gate of Life of human life is at the navel, so the cinnabar field and sea of qi communicate with the kidney vessel.

《醫要類·傷寒 Cold Damage》

1
○仲景書殘矣，得陶節庵六書、吳綬蘊要而明。當分兩門：傷寒由皮毛入髒腑，初雖惡寒發熱，而終為熱症，其人必素有火者；中寒直入髒腑，始終惡寒，並無發熱等症，其人必無火者。一則發表攻裏，一則溫中散寒，甚判然也。讀傷寒書而不讀東垣書，則內傷不明；讀東垣書而不讀丹溪書，則陰虛不明；讀丹溪書而不讀薛立齋書，則真陰真陽不明。補天要論其得源矣。溫病不惡寒而渴，乃火鬱也。虞天民曰：傳經傷寒是鬱病，岐伯所謂寒氣外凝，內鬱轉而為熱，汗之則愈，是也。六鬱用越鞠丸，要當用逍遙散。陶五法曰：發散、解肌、和解、攻裡、救里。醫宗曰：汗、吐、下、溫、清、補。類傷寒者，或痰［發熱自汗，胸滿，但頭不痛，項不強。或食積發熱頭痛，但身不痛，氣口緊盛，或虛煩煩躁發熱，但身與頭不痛，不惡寒，不浮緊，或腳氣足受寒濕，頭痛身熱，肢節痛，便閉嘔逆，但腳痛，或腫滿，或枯細，或內癰。脈浮數，若有痛處，飲食如常，畜膿也。胸痛而咳，咽乾不渴，濁唾腥臭，肺癰也。小腹重，按之痛，便數如淋，汗出惡寒，身皮甲錯，腹皮腫急，脈滑而數，腸癰也。胃脘痛，手不可近，胃脈細，人迎盛者，胃脘癰也。立齋曰：癰疽亦與內科同理。其傷風者，脈浮緩。發熱鼻塞，有汗惡風。若素有痰熱，而風邪外來，則風乘火勢，火借風威，互相鼓煽也。實則汗散，虛則固其衛氣，兼解風邪。其治風火之法，辛涼外發，甘苦內和，勿與苦寒。
○ Zhongjing's book is incomplete, and it was made clear by Tao Jie'an's Six Books and Wu Shou's Essentials. It must be divided into two categories: cold damage enters from the skin and hair into the viscera and bowels, and though at first there is aversion to cold and fever, it ends as a heat pattern, and the patient must be one who always had fire; cold-stroke goes straight into the viscera and bowels, with aversion to cold from start to finish and no fever or such symptoms, and the patient must be one without fire. The one calls for releasing the exterior and attacking the interior, the other for warming the middle and dispersing cold — they are clearly distinct. If one reads the books on cold damage but not Dongyuan's books, internal injury will not be understood; if one reads Dongyuan's books but not Danxi's, yin depletion will not be understood; if one reads Danxi's books but not Xue Lizhai's, true yin and true yang will not be understood. The Essential Discourses on Mending Heaven gets to the source. Warm disease without aversion to cold but with thirst is constrained fire. Yu Tianmin said: Channel-transmitted cold damage is a constraint illness, what Qibo called cold qi congealing outside and constraint within turning into heat, cured by sweating. For the six constraints the Escape-Restraint Pills are used, but one should use the Free and Easy Powder. Tao's five methods are: dispersing, releasing the muscles, harmonizing, attacking the interior and rescuing the interior. The Medical Tradition says: sweating, vomiting, purging, warming, clearing and tonifying. What resembles cold damage is either phlegm [fever with spontaneous sweating and fullness of the chest, but no headache and no stiffness of the neck. — or food accumulation fever and headache, but no body pain, with a tight, strong qi-mouth, or depletion vexation agitation and fever, but no pain of body or head, no aversion to cold, and a pulse not floating and tight, or leg qi the feet affected by cold and damp, headache and body heat, pain in the limbs and joints, constipation and retching, but with pain in the legs, swollen and full or withered and thin, or internal abscess. If the pulse is floating and rapid and there is a painful spot while eating is normal, pus has collected. Chest pain with cough, dry throat without thirst, and turbid, fishy-smelling spittle is lung abscess. Heaviness of the lower abdomen, painful on pressure, frequent urination like strangury, sweating with aversion to cold, scaly skin, swollen tight abdominal skin and a slippery, rapid pulse is intestinal abscess. Pain in the stomach duct that cannot bear the hand, with a thin stomach pulse and strong ren ying, is stomach-duct abscess. Lizhai said: Abscesses follow the same principles as internal medicine. In wind damage the pulse is floating and moderate. Fever, stuffed nose, sweating and aversion to wind. If there was phlegm-heat already and wind evil comes from outside, the wind takes advantage of the fire's force and the fire borrows the wind's might, fanning each other. If replete, sweat and disperse; if depleted, secure the defensive qi while also releasing the wind evil. The method of treating wind-fire is to disperse outward with pungent-cool and harmonize within with sweet-bitter; do not give bitter-cold.

《醫要類·霍亂 Sudden Turmoil》

1
○內經分濕、熱、風、暑、虛、實。其症忽心腹痛，吐泄，甚者轉筋，以陽明養宗筋，屬胃與大腸，吐下亡液，故攣縮也。大抵陰陽反戾，故以陰陽水飲之浸之或陳稷湯，吐瀉不止可飲米醋。乾霍亂不吐瀉，名攪腸沙，以土鬱致火擾，不可過攻、過熱、過寒，古方鹽熬童便，降火行血也。轉筋厥逆，當細察色脈，隨經標本施治。
○ The Inner Classic distinguishes damp, heat, wind, summer heat, depletion and repletion. Its symptoms are sudden pain in the heart and abdomen, vomiting and diarrhoea, and in severe cases cramps; because yangming nourishes the ancestral sinew and belongs to the stomach and large intestine, vomiting and diarrhoea lose fluids, so there is contraction. In general yin and yang are turned against each other, so one gives yin-yang water to drink and to soak in or Aged Millet Decoction; if vomiting and diarrhoea do not stop, rice vinegar may be drunk. Dry sudden turmoil without vomiting or diarrhoea is called "gut-churning sand"; because constrained earth causes fire to stir, one must not attack too much, heat too much or cool too much; the old prescription of salt boiled with boys' urine is to bring down fire and move the blood. For cramps and reversal one should examine colour and pulse carefully and treat according to the channel, branch and root.

《醫要類·火 Fire》

1
○六腑移熱於髒，煎熬不已，感變熱症，最難治也。陽明積熱內爍，謂之實熱，宜疏利之。七情鬱發，一髒受傷，則有偏勝之火，傳克不行，宜虛補其毋，實瀉其子，以勝復之。又有勞役致損真陰，陰虛火動，宜滋陰以降火。其胃虛過食生冷，抑遏陽氣於脾，謂之火鬱，每已午時肌熱燎如瘧狀，宜升陽散火。火有陰陽，日燈爐龍之異。若五志過極之火，驚動血者火起心，怒動起肝，憂動起肺，思動起脾，勞動起腎。河間暑火立論，欲與仲景傷寒對講，非通論種種也。潛草曰：百病皆火，養生者火。兩腎間動氣為元氣本，而胃中氣續之，運動即火也。勿縱慾以竭火，亦勿服寒以滅火，陰平陽秘，少火生氣，此當深參。
○ When the six bowels transfer heat to the viscera and it boils on without cease, turning into a heat pattern, it is the hardest to treat. When accumulated heat in yangming consumes within, it is called repletion heat, and one should clear and disperse it. When the seven emotions are pent up and burst out and one viscus is injured, there is a one-sidedly prevailing fire that cannot be transmitted and overcome; one should tonify the mother of the depleted and drain the child of the replete, overcoming it by return. There are also cases where toil has damaged the true yin, and with yin depleted the fire stirs; one should enrich yin to bring down fire. When a depleted stomach has eaten too much raw and cold food, suppressing the yang qi in the spleen, it is called constrained fire: every day at the si and wu hours the flesh burns hot like malaria, and one should raise the yang and disperse the fire. Fire has yin and yang and the differences of sun, lamp, stove and dragon. As for the fire of the five emotions in excess, when alarm moves the blood the fire arises from the heart, when anger moves it, from the liver, when sorrow moves it, from the lungs, when thought moves it, from the spleen, and when toil moves it, from the kidneys. Hejian's theory of summer-heat fire was meant to be discussed alongside Zhongjing's cold damage, and is not a general theory for all cases. The Qian Drafts say: All illnesses are fire, and what nourishes life is fire. The moving qi between the two kidneys is the root of primal qi, and the qi in the stomach continues it; its movement is fire. Do not indulge desires and exhaust the fire, nor take cold [drugs] to extinguish the fire; with yin calm and yang sealed, the lesser fire produces qi — this should be deeply pondered.

《醫要類·血 Blood》

1
○外感六氣，俱使人血溢，不獨火也。寒氣致病者，寒傷榮也，果寒鬱血，須散之，不必滋陰降火之藥。向分咳血、嗽血出肺，咯血、唾血出腎，腎實統之：腎脈入肺，循喉，挾舌本，其支從肺出，絡心，注胸中。夫痰、汗、血，皆水也，血從痰出，乃腎水挾相火炎上也，寒藥多死，溲溺可救，然久用亦損胃，須六味湯。真陰失守，虛陽上泛，吐血，須八味湯。內傷暴吐，急用獨參。金匱方用柏葉、黑姜、艾、馬通煮之，不可類推乎？衄血出經，衄行清道；吐血出胃，吐行濁道。犀角地黃治衄也，無犀角，代以升麻，此為陽明鬱火也。衄亦有陰虛火衰者，其血必點滴不成流也。似症者，火不歸元，而嗽喘面紅，吐痰血者，以八味地黃冷飲之。辯訣曰：實熱舌胎焦，甚則黑；假熱有胎必滑，渴而不能飲水，飲亦少而吐出，面赤而嬌。陰虛者，大小便俱利，吐痰必多；傷寒者，大小便閉也。陰虛發熱，有似承氣症，似白虎症，須加減八味冷飲之，翌日畏寒脈脫，乃補其陽。若大失血後大發熱，須獨參湯。辯訣曰：陰虛面必赤，其渴者，腎枯引救也，雖渴而舌滑，脈數而尺無力，或尺洪數而按之必不鼓也；若問其服過涼藥，則脈亦有力而鼓指矣。
○ All the six qi contracted from outside make people's blood overflow, not only fire.
