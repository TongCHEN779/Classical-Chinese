《醫藥類 Medicines》

《醫藥類·醫藥通類約幾 The General Incipience of the Categories of Medicines》

1
○潛草曰：至理不測，因物則以征之，醫固一大物理之橐龠也。喙者，跂者，核者，柯者，皆可以任督榮衛觀之，皆可以好惡制化窮之。形色精神，臭味燥潤，無非元氣陰陽之升降出入也，無非五六十二之運氣經絡也。用在中節，調其亢承，類應之綱，符幾不二，盡其性者，秩序變化在其中矣，豈愁約則荒而博則混哉？雌雄子母，藥之倫也。水土之抽為草木，其水母而土父乎？動物之榮衛脂膏，其水母而火父乎？究論之，六淫亦水火也，七情亦水火也。其病也，亢火與元氣不兩立，停水亦與元氣不兩立；其交濟也，水火即元氣也。氣為體，水為相，火為用，不相離也。是皆可以參兩而通類研幾焉。氣味最切，而形色應之如香走氣，味入血，花實蔓幹各具形色；輕重各分陰陽，而上下遂專升降金石玉墜，而硫以質陽，沉香、神砂兼統升降；凡有頭尾，三停以分耆、歸、防、桔類；皮骨筋節，皆應表裡竹茹走膜，諸皮走皮，藤則象筋，藕杉用節；腐蘗溲通，同主于化曲豉皆因腐變，畜糞概謂之通，寒號食柏實而遺，已複自食其遺，故通血分；物體全具，取其專長瓠藤含水，萆麻吸物，秦艽以糾象絡，石菖以清入心，荎具五味而功在酸收，葈耳同豨薟而專入巔腦：其通類固犁然矣。永叔曰：船上驚病者，刮舵牙汗末和丹砂、茯神飲之而愈；蜈蚣螫者，唾墨畫雞咮啄之，止痛。此亦以其神情而通類也。
○ The Qian Drafts say: The ultimate principle cannot be fathomed, and is verified through the patterns of things; medicine is certainly a great bellows of the principles of things. Beaked and footed creatures, kernels and stalks can all be viewed in terms of the Conception and Governing vessels and the nutritive and defensive qi, and all can be fathomed in terms of their likes and dislikes, control and transformation. Form and colour, essence and spirit, smell, taste, dryness and moisture are all nothing but the rising, falling, going out and coming in of the yin and yang of primal qi, and all nothing but the circulation of qi and the channels of five, six and twelve. Their use lies in keeping the measure and regulating excess and control; the guiding threads of categorical correspondence tally with the incipient without division, and for one who exhausts their natures, order and transformation are within — why worry that brevity leads to neglect and breadth to confusion? Male and female, child and mother are the relationships of medicines. Plants are drawn up out of water and earth — is water their mother and earth their father? The nutritive and defensive qi and the fat and grease of animals — is water their mother and fire their father? Considered thoroughly, the six excesses too are water and fire, and the seven emotions too are water and fire. In illness, excess fire cannot coexist with primal qi, and stagnant water cannot coexist with primal qi either; when they assist each other, water and fire are primal qi. Qi is the substance, water the minister, and fire the function, and they are inseparable. All this can be studied by threes and twos, comprehending categories and examining the incipient. Qi and flavour are the most pressing, and form and colour correspond to them as fragrance travels in the qi and flavour enters the blood, and flowers, fruits, vines and trunks each have their form and colour; light and heavy each divide yin and yang, and upper and lower then govern rising and falling metal, stone and jade sink, while sulphur is yang by substance; aloeswood and cinnabar govern both rising and falling; whatever has a head and tail is divided into three sections astragalus, angelica, ledebouriella, platycodon and the like; skin, bone, sinew and joint all correspond to exterior and interior bamboo shavings go to the membranes, the various peels go to the skin, vines resemble sinews, and lotus root and fir are used for the joints; rotting, sprouting, urine and passing all govern transformation ferment and fermented beans both come from rotting, and animal dung is generally said to open; the cold-crying bird eats cypress seeds and excretes them, then eats its own droppings again, so it opens the blood aspect; where the whole body of the thing is present, one takes its special strength the gourd vine holds water, castor draws things, gentian knots like the collaterals, sweet flag enters the heart by its clarity, schisandra has all five flavours but its merit is sour astringency, and cocklebur is like siegesbeckia but goes especially to the crown and brain: their categories are clearly apparent. Yongshu said: Someone who fell ill from fright on a boat recovered after drinking scrapings of the sweat on the rudder mixed with cinnabar and poria-heart; for a centipede sting, spitting on ink, drawing a cock's beak and making it peck stops the pain. This too is comprehending categories through their spirit and disposition.

《醫藥類·何往非藥 What Is Not Medicine?》

1
○藥知其故，乃能用之，反因約類，盡變不難。先曾祖本庵公知醫，具三才之故，廷尉公、中丞公皆留心紀驗，不肖以智有窮理極物之癖，閒嘗約之：補瀉也，對治從治也，寒熱溫涼而平統之，五味歸於甘苦而淡統之，皆概也；而好惡最微，因地因時，相制而變，久習而轉，皆有其端。聖人舉一二以通類，特常人信不及耳。日用如醬能制殺，醋能斂入，面豉發散，酒醴活血，燒酒行陽，寒食飯能化瘢，鍋焦健脾，柴霜收火下行，土能和物化物，金石下鎮，水火其統用也，矢溺皆妙，垢浴養身，椒熱下行，姜獨發散，鹽湯能吐，貧人得糯為補，飫後得茶即清，何待他求哉？人信不及，反多一疑，疑則又成一病。古人驗症既確，直與之藥，不言其故，而且先為之說，以平其神，故效也。學者明人身矣，知藥性矣，驗症之寒熱虛實為要，初中後三法，和取從折，屬之五治，不可不知。中德曰：先高祖封御史，諱學漸，號本庵，高景逸總憲序其書，門人謚明善先生，精醫。中通曰：公因一也，反因二也，此方氏之易學，真破天荒，一切皆然，即醫可以取證。
○ Only by knowing the reasons of medicines can one use them; with reverse causation and summarized categories, exhausting their changes is not difficult. My late great-great-grandfather Lord Ben'an knew medicine and the reasons of the three powers, and Lord Tingwei and His Excellency the Grand Coordinator both paid attention to recording their experience; I, the unworthy Yizhi, with my addiction to exhausting principles and investigating things to the end, have tried in leisure to summarize them: tonifying and draining, treating by opposition and by compliance; cold, hot, warm and cool, governed by the neutral; the five flavours returning to sweet and bitter, governed by the bland — all are outlines; but likes and dislikes are the subtlest, varying with place and time, changing by mutual restraint and turning by long habit, and all have their beginnings. The sages raised one or two cases to comprehend the categories; it is only that ordinary people do not believe enough. In daily use: soy paste can counteract killing, vinegar can astringe and draw in, fermented beans disperse, wine and sweet liquor quicken the blood, distilled liquor moves the yang, Cold Food rice can dissolve scars, the crust of the pot strengthens the spleen, firewood frost gathers fire and moves it downward, earth can harmonize and transform things, metal and stone settle downward, and water and fire are their general use; dung and urine are both marvellous, washing off dirt nourishes the body, pepper's heat moves downward, ginger alone disperses, salt water can induce vomiting, for the poor glutinous rice is a tonic, and after a surfeit tea clears at once — why seek elsewhere? People do not believe enough and add a doubt, and the doubt becomes another illness. When the ancients had verified a pattern with certainty, they simply gave the medicine without explaining the reason, and moreover first gave an explanation to calm the patient's spirit — that is why it worked. When the student understands the human body and knows the natures of medicines, the essential thing is to verify the cold, heat, depletion and repletion of the pattern; the three methods of beginning, middle and end, harmonizing and taking, following and breaking, belonging to the five treatments, must be known. Zhongde says: My great-great-great-grandfather, honoured as Censor, named Xuejian, styled Ben'an, whose book Censor-in-Chief Gao Jingyi prefaced, and whose disciples gave him the private posthumous name Master Mingshan, was expert in medicine. Zhongtong says: Gongyin is one and fanyin is two — this learning of the Changes of the Fang family truly breaks new ground; everything is so, and medicine can serve as proof.

《醫藥類·上下分餘氣 Sharing the Surplus Qi Above and Below》

1
○兔絲、寄生在木上，分餘氣者兔絲同女蘿而色近赤，蘿則青也；寄生不一，而取桑寄生；芝則隨地寄生，不假種識；茯苓、豕零、雷丸、桓檀皆在下而分餘氣者也茯苓為松餘，豕零為楓餘，雷丸為竹余，桓檀為檗餘。
○ Dodder and mistletoe, which live on trees, share the surplus qi dodder is like usnea but nearly red in colour, while usnea is green; there are many mistletoes, and mulberry mistletoe is the one taken; lingzhi lodges wherever it is, without needing to be planted; poria, zhuling, leiwan and huantan all share the surplus qi below poria is the surplus of the pine, zhuling of the sweetgum, leiwan of the bamboo, and huantan of the phellodendron.

《醫藥類·物皆有任督關節 All Things Have Conception and Governing Vessels and Joints》

1
○蠐螬背行，先循乎督；蝮蜟之用，專依乎任；化為玄蟬，復循任會督，如環無端：可以悟蒸變矣。龜運任會督于顛，鹿運督會任于尾閭。海螵蛸上表堅薄如介，裡理輕脆而通，蓋維持者督，闔闢者任，口以腹息以胎矣。草木之根皮節花，亦任督也，節則帶絡之關會也。
○ The grub moves on its back, first following the Governing vessel; the use of the cicada nymph relies especially on the Conception vessel; when it turns into the black cicada, it again follows the Conception vessel to meet the Governing, like a ring without end — from this one can grasp steaming transformation. The tortoise moves the Conception vessel to meet the Governing at the crown; the deer moves the Governing vessel to meet the Conception at the coccyx. The cuttlebone's upper surface is hard and thin like a shell, and its inner grain light, brittle and open: for what maintains is the Governing, and what opens and closes is the Conception; the mouth breathes through the belly as in the womb. The roots, bark, joints and flowers of plants are also Conception and Governing vessels, and the joints are the meeting points of the Girdle and collaterals.

《醫藥類·用心骨核 Using Hearts, Bones and Kernels》

1
○石棗核澀精，而今何去核？沈括之說也。麥冬連心用，方合土德全體，王紹隆之說也。枸杞根實，神農通用，而後分之，根乃去骨，何所本乎？蓮實去薏，失聖始種矣，此盧頤之說也。潛草曰：去骨心核者，取其味粹；存之者，象形取證也。蘇恭曰：枳實宜全核穰。連理亭曰：梔子連殼治上焦。若今連翹去仁用殼，則不知何所本也。連翹入手足少陽陽明，又肖心，入心治耳聾。陸麗京曰：人心虛，草木心多實，故天、麥冬、蓮子去心者，以入心堅而不化也。
○ The kernels of cornelian cherries astringe the essence, so why are they now removed? This is Shen Kuo's view. Ophiopogon is used with its heart, so as to accord with the whole substance of the virtue of earth — this is Wang Shaolong's view. Shennong used both the root and fruit of lycium; later they were separated, and the root has its core removed — on what basis? When the plumule is removed from lotus seeds, the sage's original planting is lost — this is Lu Yi's view. The Qian Drafts say: Removing the bones, hearts and kernels is for the purity of the flavour; keeping them is for taking evidence from the resemblance of form. Su Gong said: Immature bitter orange should keep its kernels and pulp. Lian Liting said: Gardenia with its husk treats the upper burner. As for forsythia today, removing the seeds and using the husk, I do not know on what basis. Forsythia enters the hand and foot shaoyang and yangming; it also resembles the heart, enters the heart and treats deafness. Lu Lijing said: The human heart is empty, while the hearts of plants are mostly solid; so asparagus root, ophiopogon and lotus seeds have their hearts removed because, entering the heart, they would be hard and not dissolve.

《醫藥類·草之沙者屬金，屬降 Sandy Herbs Belong to Metal and to Descending》

1
○蒲黃如沙，如黃金之不變，是其專精在黃舌腫滲之即消。石園荽之花上如沙，曰海金沙，主治石淋，因其如沙而消瘀通下也。
○ Cattail pollen is like sand, like gold that does not change, so its special essence is in its yellow a swollen tongue soaked with it subsides at once. The flowers of stone coriander have something like sand on them, called "sea gold sand," which mainly treats stone strangury, because, being like sand, it dissolves stasis and opens downward.

《醫藥類·赤箭芝 The Red Arrow Lingzhi》

1
○根為天麻，自內及外；苗為赤箭，達上反下。沉括惜世但以治風，豈知其為補益上品哉？實如楝，六棱，至秋不落，卻透空入莖中，還筒而下，潛生土內。抱朴稱獨搖芝，根有十二子環之。乘雅附六芝中，將謂別一物耶？芝以雨霽雲徹而生，不假種識，故益五髒，有中之無，赤黑者，交心腎之引藥也。番豆花透空入土結豆，當通潤臧府，是一類推法也，皆貫無於有之藥。
○ Its root is tianma, going from inside out; its shoot is the red arrow, reaching up and returning down. Shen Kuo regretted that the world uses it only to treat wind — how could they know that it is a superior tonic? Its fruit is like that of the chinaberry, six-angled, not falling in autumn, but piercing through the hollow into the stem, going back down the tube and growing hidden in the soil. The Baopuzi calls it the "solitary swaying lingzhi," whose root is ringed by twelve offshoots. The Chengya includes it among the six lingzhi — does it take it to be a different thing? Lingzhi grows when the rain clears and the clouds part, without needing to be planted, so it benefits the five viscera, being nothing within something; the red and black ones are guiding drugs for joining the heart and kidneys. The foreign bean flowers, piercing through the hollow, enter the soil and form beans, so they should open and moisten the viscera and bowels; this is one method of inference by category — all are drugs that run nothingness through somethingness.

《醫藥類·蓍蒿 Milfoil and Wormwood》

1
○茵蔯、艾、薷皆蒿類。蓍以耆老，末大於本，故神明幽贊歸之。一莖直上，百歲滿百，長丈，羲、孔墓生。神農本經云：益氣充肌明目。潘去然云：孔林蓍六棱，今藥不用。著以用茵陳即蓍也。
○ Capillary wormwood, mugwort and elsholtzia are all of the wormwood kind. Milfoil (shi) is so called from being old (qi), its top larger than its base, so the gods and spirits secretly assist it. A single stem goes straight up; at a hundred years it has a hundred stems and is a zhang tall, growing on the graves of Fu Xi and Confucius. Shennong's Basic Classic says it augments qi, fills out the flesh and brightens the eyes. Pan Quran says the milfoil of the Confucian forest is six-angled, but is not used in medicine today. It is written that using capillary wormwood is the same as milfoil.

《醫藥類·反時生 Growing Against the Seasons》

1
○秋冬生長，夏至死，是升出降入倒用者，芥麥例也，羊踶、千金子、夏枯草皆然羊踶主禿瘡陰蠋，千金子、夏枯皆行水，而夏枯入目珠，治瘰癆。智在粵見有十字草，冬青夏槁，葉尖長，四布如十字，春于葉中抽四枝結果，三棱，斷莖有白汁，似澤漆、貓兒睛，綠花，而本草言五枝抽，謂四分而兼中抽為五乎？最治蠱毒，記以俟考。嶺北亦處處有之。
○ Those that grow in autumn and winter and die at the summer solstice, whose rising out and descending in are reversed, follow the example of mustard and wheat; yangti, qianjinzi and self-heal are all so yangti treats bald sores and genital worms; qianjinzi and self-heal both move water, and self-heal enters the pupil of the eye and treats scrofula. In Yue I, Zhi, saw a "cross plant," green in winter and withered in summer, with long pointed leaves spread in four directions like a cross; in spring it puts out four branches from among the leaves and bears three-angled fruit, and its broken stem has white sap; it resembles sun spurge and cat's-eye, with green flowers, but the Materia Medica says it puts out five branches — does that mean four divisions plus the central shoot make five? It is the best for treating gu poison; I record it for further examination. It is also found everywhere north of the Ridges.

《醫藥類·黃連燥，梔子潤 Coptis Dries, Gardenia Moistens》

1
○皆苦寒，而口乾渴宜炒梔，濕痢宜黃連。
○ Both are bitter and cold, but for dry mouth and thirst fried gardenia is suitable, and for damp dysentery coptis.

《醫藥類·參 Ginseng》

1
○參似人形，力大，白者切之，須有金井玉欄。土木參明潤不白，蓋上黨紫團之類乎？近以泡參膠接小參，殊自難辨。市上節參似小菖蒲而曲，乃參蘆也蘆可湧吐，虛人用以發散，亦可同黃茋、萎蕤用之。江楚嶺北大山亦有三椏五葉生樹陰者，其味淡。直莖紫而葉如青葙兩開者，沙參也。乘雅曰：人參天地，故以名之音驂。衛氣日行陽道則寤，夜入五藏則寐，凡病張偟不能假寐者，人參入口便得安寢；急走銜參不喘，此亦視其人耳。能回元氣於垂亡，能理一切虛證，氣虛者固宜，血虛亦不可缺，無陽則陰無以生，李言聞、薛立齋詳矣。醫宗曰：肺脈洪實，火氣方逆，血熱妄行，不可驟用；痧疹初發，身雖熱而斑點未形；傷寒始作，症未定而邪熱方熾：勿誤投也。有時多用則宣通，少用反壅滯。貧者聞貴藥則吝，小醫利在行其所便，故王海藏、王節齋之說適借口耳。中履曰：出參之土木，在古北口外，非向宣府之土木也。
○ Ginseng resembles a human shape and is powerful; when white ones are cut they must show a "golden well and jade railing." Tumu ginseng is bright and glossy but not white — is it of the kind of Shangdang and Zituan? Recently small roots have been glued together with soaked ginseng, which is very hard to detect. The jointed ginseng on the market, like a small curved sweet flag, is the ginseng rhizome-head the head can induce vomiting, and for depleted people is used to disperse; it can also be used with astragalus and Solomon's seal. In the great mountains of Jiang and Chu and north of the Ridges there are also kinds with three branches and five leaves growing in the shade of trees, whose taste is bland. That with a straight purple stem and leaves like celosia opening in pairs is shashen. The Chengya says: Ginseng (renshen) forms a triad (can) with Heaven and Earth, hence its name pronounced can. The defensive qi travels by day in the yang paths and one is awake, and at night enters the five viscera and one sleeps; whenever someone ill and agitated cannot even doze, as soon as ginseng enters the mouth he sleeps soundly; holding ginseng in the mouth while running fast prevents breathlessness — but this too depends on the person. It can restore primal qi when it is about to perish and can manage all depletion patterns; it is certainly suitable for qi depletion, and indispensable for blood depletion too, for without yang yin cannot be produced; Li Yanwen and Xue Lizhai have treated this in detail. The Medical Tradition says: When the lung pulse is flooding and full, the fire qi is just in counterflow and the hot blood runs wild, it must not be used suddenly; when measles first appear and, though the body is hot, the spots have not yet formed; when cold damage first begins and the pattern is not yet fixed while the evil heat is just raging: do not give it by mistake. Sometimes used in large amounts it opens and penetrates, while in small amounts it causes blockage instead. The poor balk on hearing of expensive drugs, and petty physicians profit by doing what is convenient, so the views of Wang Haizang and Wang Jiezhai serve as their excuse. Zhonglü says: The Tumu where ginseng comes from is beyond the Gubeikou, not the Tumu formerly of Xuanfu.

《醫藥類·甘草 Licorice》

1
○存中辨景純蘦大苦非甘草，是也。今重秦粉草，正以西邊風勁，其根直深，故其力厚。劉客生言：西寧、鎮番甘草最多，葉似天竹，但冬凋耳，根鬚下數丈，外域成樹。
○ Cunzhong argued that the ling, "great bitter," of Jingchun is not licorice — that is right. Today the powdery licorice of Qin is prized, precisely because the winds of the western borderland are strong and its roots go straight and deep, so its power is great. Liu Kesheng said that licorice is most plentiful at Xining and Zhenfan, its leaves like nandina but withering in winter, its roots and rootlets going down several zhang, and beyond the borders it grows into trees.

《醫藥類·黃連 Coptis》

1
○本草貴宣連，今黃山所產是也。時重川連，而南楚辰播皆是。愚在城步辰陽見人取之，是雞爪類。高守恒曰：邛州黃連長尺，粗如箸，或如小指，雅閬巴皆小雞爪。愚按：雲貴連皆無毛，諸深山連皆有毛，生溪石上，葉似小芹而冬不凋，但其味更苦耳。瀉心生用，餘可吳萸、姜製。
○ The Materia Medica values Xuan coptis, which is what is now produced at Huangshan. At present Sichuan coptis is valued, and that of Chen and Bo in southern Chu is all of it. At Chengbu and Chenyang I saw people gathering it; it is of the chicken-claw kind. Gao Shouheng said: The coptis of Qiongzhou is a chi long and as thick as a chopstick or a little finger, while that of Ya, Lang and Ba is all small chicken-claw. I note: the coptis of Yunnan and Guizhou is all hairless, while that of the deep mountains is all hairy, growing on rocks in streams, with leaves like small celery that do not wither in winter, only it tastes even more bitter. For draining the heart it is used raw; otherwise it may be prepared with evodia or ginger.

《醫藥類·大黃 Rhubarb》

1
○川雅州大黃最大，如碗，錦紋而香。陝西平涼大黃有大如瓜者。得此酒製上巔，九蒸曬補，硫製化積如神。
○ The rhubarb of Yazhou in Sichuan is the largest, as big as a bowl, with a brocade pattern and fragrant. The rhubarb of Pingliang in Shaanxi can be as big as a melon. Prepared with wine it reaches the crown; steamed and dried nine times it tonifies; prepared with sulphur it dissolves accumulations miraculously.

《醫藥類·黃精偏精 Solomon's Seal and Pianjing》

1
○黃精似姜而一干對分，葉長尺許，本草謂如竹葉，不肖也。初苗名筆管菜。其偏精即萎蕤，華陀告樊阿之青黏也。瀕湖極稱萎蕤功並人參。神隱有萎蕤餌法：二月九月採根，形比黃精，葉狹而長，表白裏青，莖直如箭幹，有節，三月開青花，結圓實。
○ Huangjing resembles ginger with a single stem dividing in pairs and leaves a chi or so long; the Materia Medica's saying that they are like bamboo leaves is not accurate. The first shoots are called "brush-holder vegetable." Its pianjing is weirui, the "green glue" that Hua Tuo told Fan A of. Binhu greatly praises weirui, putting its merit on a par with ginseng. The Shenyin has a method for eating weirui: gather the root in the second and ninth months; its form is like huangjing, the leaves narrow and long, white on top and green beneath, the stem straight like an arrow-shaft with joints; it opens green flowers in the third month and bears round fruit.

《醫藥類·附子 Aconite》

1
○出彰明赤水，苖三四尺，葉似石龍芮及艾，花紫碧色，冬至沃田，豕糞種之，明秋始茂，其根生子如芋，宋楊天惠記詳矣。近年各處種之，隔年發葉似菊，三年始生子，蓋烏頭沃種久之而大也，灌以金汁。其曰奚毒，即雀芋，非此種也。雀芋之最大者，海芋，即觀音蓮。博物志：交州俚子藥箭，即曝雀芋根，射人沸爛，飲月水及糞汁，時有瘥者，射豬狗無恙，以食糞也。潛山中以射虎。中德曰：乙酉張獻忠亂蜀時，附子至三四十金一枚。中通曰：底平尖正者少，皆刻木盒，以鹽浸耎，而安木盒中印造之。彭飛雲曰：真彰明色灰，秦產色白。葉蔭生曰：鞏昌皆種烏頭，多年成附子。平涼一帶四月八、臘八寺煮烏頭飯，眾蓋煮一晝夜，極熟則自無毒，揭蓋加水則不可食。河北咂酒皆以烏頭為曲。
○ It comes from Chishui in Zhangming; the shoots are three or four chi tall, the leaves like celery-leaved buttercup and mugwort, the flowers purple-blue; at the winter solstice it is planted in rich fields with pig dung, and flourishes only in the next autumn; its root produces offshoots like taro — Yang Tianhui of the Song recorded it in detail. In recent years it has been planted everywhere; in the second year it puts out leaves like chrysanthemum, and only in the third year produces offshoots, because wutou planted in rich soil grows large over time; it is watered with "golden juice." What is called xidu is the sparrow-taro, not this kind. The largest of the sparrow-taros is the giant taro, that is, Guanyin lotus. The Treatise on Broad Matters: the poisoned arrows of the Li people of Jiaozhou are made with dried sparrow-taro root; a person shot by them boils and rots, and those who drink menstrual blood or dung-juice sometimes recover; pigs and dogs shot are unharmed because they eat dung. In the mountains of Qian they use it to shoot tigers. Zhongde says: In the yiyou year, when Zhang Xianzhong ravaged Shu, aconite cost thirty or forty taels apiece. Zhongtong says: Few have flat bottoms and true points; all are made by carving wooden moulds, soaking [the roots] soft in salt and pressing them into the moulds. Peng Feiyun says: True Zhangming aconite is ash-coloured, and that produced in Qin is white. Ye Yinsheng says: Gongchang plants wutou everywhere, which after many years becomes aconite. Around Pingliang, on the eighth of the fourth month and on Laba, the monasteries cook wutou rice; the crowd covers it and cooks it for a whole day and night, and when thoroughly cooked it is naturally non-poisonous, but if the lid is lifted and water added it cannot be eaten. The zajiu of Hebei all uses wutou as its ferment.

《醫藥類·鐘乳 Stalactite》

1
○鐘乳、孔公孽皆石精所凝，而相謂補陽，愚嘗疑之。兩粵最多。黎美周曰：性澀而凝精，合陽藥走下部，故方家用之。服乳忌參術，謂木能動鐘乳也，存中所載夏英公與其小吏利害相反，然哉？江鄰幾曰：高敏之以鐘乳飼牛而飲其乳，後患血痢卒，以鐘乳太澀也。中履曰：黎美周遂球，廣州孝廉，丙戌與楊機部、郭雲機、萬吉人、姚有僕同虔難，有集行世。
○ Stalactite and kong gong nie are both congealed from the essence of stone, and they are said to tonify yang, but I have doubted this. They are most plentiful in the two Yue. Li Meizhou says: Its nature is astringent and congeals the essence; combined with yang drugs it goes to the lower region, which is why practitioners use it. Taking stalactite one must avoid ginseng and atractylodes, because wood can stir stalactite; is it so that, as Cunzhong records, it had opposite effects for Duke Xiaying and his junior clerk? Jiang Linji said: Gao Minzhi fed stalactite to an ox and drank its milk, and later died of bloody dysentery, because stalactite is too astringent. Zhonglü says: Li Meizhou (Suiqiu) was a provincial graduate of Guangzhou, who in the bingxu year died in the Qian disaster together with Yang Jibu, Guo Yunji, Wan Jiren and Yao Youpu; his collected works circulate.

《醫藥類·雄黃 Realgar》

1
○楊億筆記：楊嵎依鄭玄黃堥五毒藥而愈頰瘍。虞雍公以甘草服雄黃蒸餅丸而愈痢。黃堥，瓦合有蓋者，范汪改為飛黃散是也：瓦盆中安雌黃，南丹砂，北慈石，東曾青，西白石英，上為礜石，膏次之，鐘乳居下，雄黃覆之，云母布於底，以盆蓋之，羊毛固濟，以陳葦燒一日，取其飛黃用之。夫雄黃，治瘡殺蠱毒之上品也，而入肝經氣分，故能定癇涎，治瘧痢，又能化血為水。凡赤者為雄，有臭黃，以醋洗其臭者，當辨之。武都階州有水窟雄黃，可充丹砂，但研細色黃耳。老父在蜀得蒜汁煉雄精成佩方，置床室，卻病，辟蛇鬼。若以化物，當與硝石同用。中德曰：先祖中丞公筮仕在蜀。
○ Yang Yi's Jottings: Yang Yu cured an ulcer of the cheek following Zheng Xuan's yellow-jar five-poison medicine. Duke Yu Yong cured dysentery by taking realgar steamed-cake pills with licorice. The yellow jar is an earthenware box with a lid, which Fan Wang changed into the Flying Yellow Powder: in an earthenware basin place orpiment, cinnabar to the south, magnetite to the north, azurite to the east and white quartz to the west, arsenolite on top, gypsum next, stalactite below, cover with realgar, spread mica on the bottom, cover with a basin, seal tight with wool, burn with old reeds for a day, and take the "flying yellow" to use. Realgar is a superior drug for treating sores and killing gu poison, and it enters the qi aspect of the liver channel, so it can settle epileptic drool and treat malaria and dysentery, and it can also turn blood into water. All red ones are realgar; there is also "stinking yellow," which is washed with vinegar for its smell, and must be distinguished. At Jiezhou in Wudu there is water-cave realgar which can pass for cinnabar, except that when ground fine it is yellow. In Shu my late father obtained a recipe for refining realgar essence with garlic juice into a pendant, which placed in the bedroom wards off illness and repels snakes and ghosts. If used to transform things, it should be used together with saltpetre. Zhongde says: My late grandfather, His Excellency the Grand Coordinator, began his official career in Shu.

《醫藥類·硫 Sulphur》

1
○淮南曰：夏至硫黃澤。蓋陽入地遇陰而成者。舶硫如蜜，黃中有金紅處，擊開如水晶，有光，今青硫不佳也。陸麗京云：硫暖而利，其性下行；焰硝煖而散，其性上行；礞石寒而下；消石煖而上。劉薦叔曰：今以消石化硫為水，以竹筒盛之，埋馬糞中一月，而合鉛霜十之一，制度甚妙，最化積聚。專欲扶陽，則埋一甕豆漿，而以小罌盛礬硫，松毛塞之，令口向甕，塗泥固之，金粟火煆之，滴下者精，乃以豬髒同煮。孫公談圃曰：三伏日餌硫黃百粒，去髒腑穢滯，以煖而速下也。七寶丹治久患瀉痢、宿食氣痛，最驗。白樂天曰：退之服硫終一病，訖不痊。王讜唐語林曰：韓愈病卒，召群僚曰：「吾不藥，今將病死矣。汝詳視吾手肢體，無誑人云韓愈癩死也。」老人原可服硫，死自天命，翛然而去，與硫何與而謗之？黃山谷在宜州服紫霞丹，自云得力。曾紆勸其勿服，山谷曰：「公等疽根在旁，乃不可服；如僕服之，殆是晴雲之在川谷也。」中德曰：麗京乃陸鯤庭年伯之兄，善醫。劉薦叔年伯名中藻，福安人。中通曰：劉安期醫學主子和，而卻服硫。揭子暄曰：暘氣入地遇水則死為硫，升雲則爆為雷，乃生養萬物之原，藥當以此為第一種，但須善製耳。遇硫毒，研釜底煤炮湯飲，蓋煤為火之宅，猶以油餅收油也。
○ The Huainanzi says: At the summer solstice sulphur is moist. For it is formed when yang enters the earth and meets yin. Shipborne sulphur is like honey, with gold-red spots in the yellow, and when broken open is like rock crystal with a lustre; the present green sulphur is not good. Lu Lijing says: Sulphur is warm and promoting, and its nature moves downward; saltpetre is warm and dispersing, and its nature moves upward; chlorite is cold and descending; nitre is warm and rising. Liu Jianshu says: Today saltpetre is used to dissolve sulphur into a liquid, which is put in a bamboo tube and buried in horse dung for a month, then combined with a tenth part of lead frost — the preparation is excellent and best for dissolving accumulations. If one wants solely to support yang, bury a jar of soy milk, put alum and sulphur in a small jar stopped with pine needles, turn its mouth toward the [larger] jar, seal it with mud, and calcine it with a millet-grain fire; what drips down is the essence, which is then boiled with pig's viscera. Sun Gong's Garden of Talks says: On the dog days take a hundred grains of sulphur to remove filth and stagnation from the viscera and bowels, because it is warm and descends quickly. The Seven Treasures Elixir is most effective for treating chronic diarrhoea and dysentery, retained food and qi pain. Bai Letian said: Tuizhi took sulphur, and ended with an illness from which he never recovered. Wang Dang's Forest of Tang Talk says: When Han Yu was dying of illness he summoned his colleagues and said: "I do not take medicine, and now I am about to die of illness. Look carefully at my hands, limbs and body, so that no one can falsely say that Han Yu died of leprosy." Old men may well take sulphur; death comes from Heaven's decree, and when one departs serenely, what has sulphur to do with it that it should be slandered? Huang Shangu took the Purple Mist Elixir at Yizhou and said he benefited from it. Zeng Yu advised him not to take it, and Shangu said: "You gentlemen have the roots of carbuncles beside you, so you must not take it; when I take it, it is like clear clouds in the mountain valleys." Zhongde says: Lijing is the elder brother of Uncle Lu Kunting, and is skilled in medicine. Uncle Liu Jianshu was named Zhongzao and was a man of Fu'an. Zhongtong says: Liu Anqi's medicine followed Zihe, yet he took sulphur. Master Jie Xuan says: When yang qi enters the earth and meets water it dies and becomes sulphur, and when it rises into the clouds it explodes as thunder; it is the source of the generation and nourishment of all things, and among drugs should be counted the very first, only it must be well prepared. For sulphur poisoning, grind pot-bottom soot, steep it in hot water and drink it, for soot is the dwelling of fire, just as an oil cake absorbs oil.

《醫藥類·龍腦 Borneol》

1
○婆律木如杉，而葉圓背白，皮有甲錯。葉廷珪則謂千年杉鋸版取腦，則與取樟腦法同矣。唐天寶交趾貢龍腦，如蠶蟬形。宋史：熙寧九年，英州雷震，一山樟樹盡枯，中皆龍腦。可信樹汁降香亦有取煉者，不必執一也。今以梅花片食咽鎖喉者真，韶州升樟腦似之：切新樟片，井水浸三日，鍋煎，柳攪，汁減半，柳有白霜，即沁入瓦盂，經宿自成，再合二皿，濕紙封之，文武火熁之半時，取冷，如此三四次，即冰片也。或以杉木鋪之，合諸藥封二碗，入水半盞煨之，水乾取開，腦自升於上。贊寧曰：其赤色者曰父婆律。凡諸力厚有汁之老樹，遇大凍枯者，皆有香，皆可作片取腦升用。松潘有娑羅樹化龍腦之事。中通曰：篩壁土與樟腦拌勻，攤碗內，捩薄荷汁灑土上，又以一碗合定，濕紙條固其縫而蒸之，少時樟腦飛上碗底，皆成冰片。
○ The borneol tree is like a fir, with round leaves white beneath and scaly bark. Ye Tinggui says that the "brain" is obtained by sawing boards from thousand-year firs, which is the same as the method of obtaining camphor. In the Tianbao period of the Tang, Jiaozhi presented borneol shaped like silkworms and cicadas. The History of the Song: in the ninth year of Xining, lightning struck a mountain at Yingzhou, all the camphor trees withered, and inside them all was borneol. One may believe that some refine it from tree sap and rosewood too; one need not cling to one source. Today the plum-blossom flakes that lock the throat when swallowed are genuine; the sublimed camphor of Shaozhou resembles it: cut fresh camphor-wood into slices, soak them in well water for three days, boil them in a pot stirring with a willow stick until the liquid is halved and white frost appears on the willow, then pour it into an earthenware basin, where it sets overnight; then put two dishes together, seal them with wet paper and heat them over a gentle and strong fire for half an hour, take off and cool, and after three or four times it is "ice flakes." Or spread fir wood, combine it with the other drugs sealed in two bowls, add half a cup of water and roast it; when the water has dried, open it, and the camphor has sublimed to the top. Zanning says: The red kind is called "father borneol." All old trees of strong force and much sap that wither in a great frost are fragrant, and can all be made into flakes, the camphor being sublimed for use. At Songpan there is the story of a sal tree turning into borneol. Zhongtong says: Sift earth from a wall and mix it evenly with camphor, spread it in a bowl, wring out peppermint juice and sprinkle it on the earth, cover it with another bowl, seal the joint with strips of wet paper and steam it; after a while the camphor flies up to the bottom of the [upper] bowl and all becomes ice flakes.

《醫藥類·巴豆 Croton》

1
○有三種，而起線者為雄，不去膜傷胃，不去心則嘔，沉香浸則能升降，同大黃則瀉反緩。蓋巴豆惡大黃，而仲景備急丸同用之。王好古曰：可以通腸，可以止瀉，世不知也。吳鑑在曰：巴豆樹葉有刺，本草未載。蠟匱丸治久溏，妙甚。退齋雅聞云：趙傳霖傳方：枳殼去穰，納巴豆一，以麻皮合之，水煮爛，即去巴豆，焙乾枳末待用，治一切痞。中履曰：吳鑒在德操為子遠公侄，已丑為桂林巡按，履隨老父依之一年。暄曰：巴豆作瀉傷人者，濃煎黃連汁服。

Wu Jianzai said: The leaves of the croton tree have prickles, which the Materia Medica does not record. The Wax-Casket Pills treat chronic loose stools very well. 《醫藥類·錦地羅、三七、豬腰子、山羊血 Jindiluo, Sanqi, Pig's Kidney and Wild Goat's Blood》

1
○廣西四種妙藥，瞿稼軒年伯取以濟人，極驗。錦地羅，蔓結子，根似括蔞、萆薢，會典貢錦地羅二斤，言其貴也，諸瘡立潰，散血最神。三七，乃山漆也，根似乾地黃而短，止血散血定痛。豬腰子，六瓣，形似之，土人乾之以為縣鑰牌，磨治腫毒。山羊血止心痛，跌死者酒服三錢即活。中通曰：近見金道隱年伯記韶州丹霞崖上產錦地羅，則廣東亦有之，生石上，有藤，藤生葉如錢大，葉聚五，相間生，生不過五六寸，常枯，遇雨復活。
○ These four marvellous drugs of Guangxi Uncle Qu Jiaxuan used to help people, with great effect. Jindiluo is a vine bearing seeds, its root like trichosanthes and dioscorea; the Collected Statutes lists two jin of jindiluo as tribute, which shows how precious it is; all sores burst at once, and it is most miraculous for dispersing blood. Sanqi is mountain lacquer; its root is like dried rehmannia but shorter, and it stops bleeding, disperses blood and settles pain. "Pig's kidney" has six lobes and is shaped like one; the natives dry it to use as county key-tags, and ground it treats swellings and poisons. Wild goat's blood stops heart pain, and those who have fallen and died revive at once if given three qian in wine. Zhongtong says: Recently I saw that Uncle Jin Daoyin recorded jindiluo growing on the Danxia cliffs at Shaozhou, so Guangdong has it too; it grows on rocks, with a vine whose leaves are the size of a coin, gathered in fives, growing alternately, no more than five or six inches long, often withering and reviving when it rains.

《醫藥類·吳茱萸 Evodia》

1
○柯古云：椒下行，吳茱萸上行。而常子正中丞痰飲以吳茱萸愈，小便皆有茱萸氣。梅楊卿用酒浸吳茱萸三宿，以茯苓末拌之，酒下百粒，將謂茯苓引之下耶？又云：咽舌生瘡，以醋調吳茱萸末貼兩足心，移夜便愈，與附子同功，則何嘗不引熱下行耶？川椒功在閉精，吳茱乃溫中下氣者也。椒子不如漆光者，多食則閉氣。胡椒暖胃助陽，乾姜守而不走，生姜發散，茴香入小腸，砂仁、豆蔻香而開胃，肉蔻能澀，常食料之煖者，用各有宜。
