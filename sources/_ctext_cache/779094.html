《人身類 The Human Body》

《人身類·鼻祖 The Nose as Ancestor》

1
○朱隱老曰：人寐則目不用，次耳不用，鼻息則晝夜寤寐不息者也。浮山愚者曰：始生，鼻與命門為小環，漸長而成任督之環，故鼻獨直而有二孔，自為代錯也。十二經、十五絡、三隧、八奇，總應呼吸，如草木之根葉，時時上下，歸塘昆頂，貫輸環輪，而無一息或間者也。平旦寸口，百脈會焉，習莫察矣。聖人以亥巳之晝夜知子午之晝夜，而與百性同用卯酉之晝夜。帝出乎震，吾得夏時，誰並悟乎？息者，消息、休息，自心生死之幾也。心息相依，以入深深，通晝夜而知，則遊即大息也。黃石齋先生疑萬三千五百息與易策不合，老父十五年得之，蓋九洛之度符也，六十四日大周者也，概也，中有盈虛。
○ Zhu Yinlao said: When a person sleeps, first the eyes cease to function, then the ears, but the breath of the nose never stops, day or night, waking or sleeping. The Foolish One of Fushan says: At the beginning of life, the nose and the Gate of Life form a small circle, which as one grows becomes the circle of the Conception and Governing vessels; so the nose alone is straight and has two nostrils, alternating of themselves. The twelve channels, fifteen collaterals, three tunnels and eight extraordinary vessels all respond to breathing, like the roots and leaves of plants, constantly going up and down, returning to the pool and the summit, passing through and circling without a single breath's interruption. At dawn all the vessels meet at the inch-mouth, yet from habit no one notices. The sages knew the day and night of zi and wu by the day and night of hai and si, and used with the common people the day and night of mao and you. "The Lord comes forth in zhen"; "I have obtained the seasons of Xia" — who has understood them together? Breath (xi) means waxing and waning (xiaoxi) and rest (xiuxi); it is the incipience of life and death arising from the mind. When mind and breath depend on each other, entering deeper and deeper, knowing through day and night, then roaming is the great breath. Master Huang Shizhai doubted that the thirteen thousand five hundred breaths agreed with the milfoil numbers of the Changes; my late father worked it out after fifteen years: it tallies with the measures of the nine and the Luo [diagram], a great cycle of sixty-four days — this is the outline, with waxing and waning within.

《人身類·三隧 The Three Tunnels》

1
○任督旋者，宗氣也；陰行脈者，榮也；陽行分肉之際者，衛也。一呼行三寸，吸如之，五為常，六為閏，行陽周諸腑，行陰周諸髒，各二十五度，與五十度周脈相合，而會於寅，行八百十丈，循環無息者也。馬蒔謂：呼三吸一，八會之氣會於膻中，行于上焦，謂之宗氣；榮言運也，由中焦生，太陰主內；衛言維也，由下焦生，太陽主外；呼出心肺，吸入腎肝，總言之也。中通曰：言出氣海，有出無入，而日受飲食，胃氣亦長者也。鼻吸上腦，而胸中應之，三隧之脈自應之，記此以問神明者。
○ What circles through the Conception and Governing vessels is the ancestral qi; what moves yin in the vessels is the nutritive qi; what moves yang between the divisions of the flesh is the defensive qi. With each breath out it moves three inches, and the same with each breath in; five is the norm and six the intercalary; moving through the yang it circles the bowels, and moving through the yin it circles the viscera, twenty-five times each, combining with the fifty circuits of the vessels, meeting at yin and travelling eight hundred and ten zhang, circling without rest. Ma Shi said: Three out and one in; the qi of the eight meetings meets at the chest centre and moves in the upper burner, and is called the ancestral qi; "nutritive" speaks of transport, being produced from the middle burner, with taiyin governing the interior; "defensive" speaks of maintaining, being produced from the lower burner, with taiyang governing the exterior; breathing out from the heart and lungs and breathing in to the kidneys and liver is a general way of speaking. Zhongtong says: This speaks of [qi] issuing from the sea of qi, going out without coming in, yet receiving food and drink daily, so that the stomach qi also grows. When the nose breathes in up to the brain, the chest responds, and the vessels of the three tunnels respond of themselves; I record this to ask those of spiritual insight.

《人身類·二路 The Two Paths》

1
○上下通止二路：前則通于髒而藏精，後則通于腑而輸泄。口二竅：前曰喉，肺管也，會厭司開以言，司閉以食，肺下連心，心系腎肝肺，此五髒滿而不實者也；後曰咽，食管也，胃脘為賁門，入胃，從幽門循小腸，闌門接大腸及直腸，出魄門，此六腑實而不滿者也。小腸泌別清濁，闌門旁為膀胱，達前陰，上管其下管即腎系也。愚者曰：口二竅，上有鼻孔，下二竅，前後溲，而前復有二竅也。精屬腎，而曰五髒藏精，髒不洩，而獨腎泄，蓋小心焦火泄之也。故曰：口之合髒腑者，脾胃也，味養氣而言吐納也；宗筋之合臟腑者，腎精小心焦火藏用者也；目之合髒腑者，神于明也，色通內外而心直受之幾也；耳之合髒腑者，神於暗也，聲通內外而心旁受之幾也。口出耳入，言以風力；鼻獨識氣，上腦為近也。唇以表脾，而四肢合肝，皮毛合肺，骨則腎主之矣。髒與髒交者，心肺以在上也；腑與腑合者，胃、大小腸在中傳受也；髒腑相合者，肝膽、脾胃之司左右也；腎與膀胱，則形不相通而氣相屬者也。此不可以不知。中德曰：膀胱無上竅，似綿紙之透汞，焉有下竅而聽氣之收放？經曰：氣化則能出矣。故肺氣無病，則闌門泌別依時。中履曰：鼻之于目，山澤通氣；口之于耳，雷風相薄。
○ There are two paths for passing through and stopping, above and below: the front connects with the viscera and stores the essence, and the back connects with the bowels and conveys and drains. The mouth has two openings: the front one is called the throat, the windpipe, with the epiglottis opening for speech and closing for eating; the lungs connect below with the heart, and the heart is linked with the kidneys, liver and lungs; these five viscera are full but not solid; the back one is called the gullet, the food pipe, with the stomach duct as the cardia, entering the stomach, going through the pylorus along the small intestine, through the ileocaecal gate connecting with the large intestine and rectum, and out at the anus; these six bowels are solid but not full. The small intestine separates the clear and the turbid, and beside the ileocaecal gate is the bladder, reaching the front orifice; above it governs the lower pipe, which is the kidney system. The Foolish One says: The mouth has two openings, above there are the nostrils, and below two openings, front and rear excretion, and in front there are again two openings. The essence belongs to the kidneys, and it is said that the five viscera store essence; the viscera do not drain, and only the kidneys drain, because the burner-fire of the little heart drains it. So it is said: what connects the mouth with the viscera and bowels is the spleen and stomach, flavour nourishing qi, and speaking of taking in and giving out; what connects the ancestral sinew with the viscera and bowels is the storing and use of the kidney essence and the burner-fire of the little heart; what connects the eyes with the viscera and bowels is spirit in brightness, colour passing between inside and outside and the heart receiving it directly — this is its incipience; what connects the ears with the viscera and bowels is spirit in darkness, sound passing between inside and outside and the heart receiving it from the side — this is its incipience. The mouth puts out and the ears take in, speech using the force of wind; the nose alone recognizes qi, being close to the brain above. The lips express the spleen, the four limbs correspond to the liver, the skin and hair to the lungs, and the bones are governed by the kidneys. Viscus connects with viscus in the heart and lungs because they are above; bowel joins bowel in the stomach and the large and small intestines because they transmit and receive in the middle; viscera and bowels join in the liver and gall bladder and the spleen and stomach, which govern left and right; the kidneys and bladder are not connected in form but belong together in qi. This must be known. Zhongde says: The bladder has no upper opening, like cotton paper through which mercury seeps; how could it have a lower opening that lets qi be gathered and released? The Classic says: When qi transforms, it can come out. So when the lung qi is healthy, the ileocaecal gate separates [clear and turbid] at the proper times. Zhonglü says: The nose relates to the eyes as mountain and marsh exchange qi; the mouth relates to the ears as thunder and wind press on each other.

《人身類·三部九候 The Three Regions and Nine Pulses》

1
○兩額動脈，太陽所行，以候頭角氣，上部天也；兩頰動脈，鼻孔兩邊近巨髎，陽明所行，以候口齒，上部地也；耳前動脈陷中，少陽所行，以候耳目之氣，上部人也。手太陽經渠以候肺，中部天也；手陽明大腸合骨以候胸中之氣，中部地也；手少陰神門以候心，中部人也。毛際外，羊矢下寸半陷中，五里分，臥取，以候肝，下部天也；太溪以候腎，下部地也；魚腹上直五里下箕門分，沉取以候脾，跗上衝陽之分穴以候胃，下部人也。三而三之，合則為九，九分九野，為九藏，神藏五，形藏四也。內經順三才以表，舉其實，人皆在中。
○ The pulsing vessels on both temples, along which taiyang runs, are used to read the qi of the head and corners of the forehead — the heaven of the upper region; the pulsing vessels on both cheeks, beside the nostrils near Juliao, along which yangming runs, are used to read the mouth and teeth — the earth of the upper region; the pulsing vessel in the hollow in front of the ear, along which shaoyang runs, is used to read the qi of the ears and eyes — the man of the upper region. Jingqu of the hand taiyang is used to read the lungs — the heaven of the middle region; Hegu of the hand yangming large intestine is used to read the qi in the chest — the earth of the middle region; Shenmen of the hand shaoyin is used to read the heart — the man of the middle region. In the hollow an inch and a half below Yangshi, outside the pubic hair, at the Wuli division, taken lying down, the liver is read — the heaven of the lower region; Taixi is used to read the kidneys — the earth of the lower region; at the division of Jimen below Wuli, straight above the fish-belly, taken deep, the spleen is read, and at the Chongyang point on the instep the stomach is read — the man of the lower region. Three times three makes nine in all; the nine divisions correspond to the nine fields and are the nine stores, five stores of spirit and four stores of form. The Inner Classic follows the three powers to express them, raising their reality, with man always in the middle.

《人身類·十二動脈 The Twelve Pulsing Vessels》

1
○太淵在寸，手太陰肺也；陽谿在手合骨上，手陽明大腸也；衝陽在跗，足陽明胃也；衝門在腹下前股溝縫，足太陰脾也；陰卻在神門內，手少陰心也；天窗在喉旁，手太陽小腸也；委中在膕腕，足太陽膀胱也；大溪在踝裏旁，足少陰腎也；勞宮在掌心，手厥陰心包絡也；和髎在耳與目之間，手少陽三焦也；懸鐘在外臁踝之上，足少陽膽也；太衝在足大指上趺，足厥陰肝也。此以十二經循環言。兩太陰肺脾、兩陽明大腸胃，是濕土燥金相代錯以化用也，子隨母居，土旺金盛，故太陰為髒位，與陽明隔而氣相合為腑也。兩少陰心腎、兩太陽小腸膀胱，是君火寒水代錯以化用也，火從水化，水隨腎至，故少陰為髒位，與太陽隔而氣相合為腑也。兩厥陰心包肝、兩少陽三焦膽，是風木相火化錯以化用也，相火寄于肝腎，膽者肝之腑，心包者腎之配，故厥陰為髒位，與少陽隔而氣相合為腑也。一少陽，二陽明，三太陽；一厥陰，二少陰，三太陰：故少陽與厥陰、陽明與少陰、太陽與太陰相衝合也。少陽中見厥陰，合矣；陽明與太陰合，太陽與少陰合，則峙合也。陽明中見太陽，言病生于濕土也；厥陰中見少陽，言病乃相火為之，是從中也。心腎、小腸膀胱，水火互根，各隨見證而治，是從本從標也。少陽之病治相火，太陰之病治溼土，是從本也。風從火斷，則汗之；燥與濕兼，則下之；燥通大腑，濕利小水，皆下也。肝膽三包心小皆火，脾胃腎肺大膀皆濕，十二經有約幾焉。張璣診手而復診趺陽、太谿，正謂三陽以胃為主，三陰以腎為主也。中履曰：手足十二經循環六氣表裏，別詳一圖，是老父獨悟者也。
○ Taiyuan, at the inch, is the hand taiyin lung; Yangxi, above Hegu on the hand, the hand yangming large intestine; Chongyang, on the instep, the foot yangming stomach; Chongmen, in the groove of the front thigh below the abdomen, the foot taiyin spleen; Yinxi, inside Shenmen, the hand shaoyin heart; Tianchuang, beside the throat, the hand taiyang small intestine; Weizhong, at the back of the knee, the foot taiyang bladder; Taixi, beside the inner ankle, the foot shaoyin kidney; Laogong, in the palm, the hand jueyin pericardium; Heliao, between the ear and eye, the hand shaoyang Triple Burner; Xuanzhong, above the outer ankle, the foot shaoyang gall bladder; Taichong, on the instep above the big toe, the foot jueyin liver. This speaks in terms of the circulation of the twelve channels. The two taiyin, lungs and spleen, and the two yangming, large intestine and stomach, are damp earth and dry metal alternating to transform and function; the child dwells with the mother, earth flourishing and metal abundant, so taiyin is the position of the viscera, separated from yangming but joined in qi as bowels. The two shaoyin, heart and kidneys, and the two taiyang, small intestine and bladder, are sovereign fire and cold water alternating to transform and function; fire transforms following water, and water follows the kidneys, so shaoyin is the position of the viscera, separated from taiyang but joined in qi as bowels. The two jueyin, pericardium and liver, and the two shaoyang, Triple Burner and gall bladder, are wind-wood and ministerial fire alternating to transform and function; the ministerial fire lodges in the liver and kidneys, the gall bladder is the bowel of the liver and the pericardium the partner of the kidneys, so jueyin is the position of the viscera, separated from shaoyang but joined in qi as bowels. One is shaoyang, two yangming, three taiyang; one jueyin, two shaoyin, three taiyin: so shaoyang and jueyin, yangming and shaoyin, taiyang and taiyin oppose and join each other. Shaoyang sees jueyin in the middle — they are joined; yangming joins taiyin and taiyang joins shaoyin — these are opposite joinings. Yangming seeing taiyang in the middle means that the illness arises from damp earth; jueyin seeing shaoyang in the middle means that the illness is caused by the ministerial fire — this is following the middle. The heart and kidneys, small intestine and bladder, water and fire, are mutually rooted, and each is treated according to the symptoms seen — this is following the root and following the branch. For illnesses of shaoyang treat the ministerial fire, and for illnesses of taiyin treat the damp earth — this is following the root. When wind is judged to follow fire, induce sweating; when dryness and damp are combined, purge; for dryness open the large bowel and for damp promote urination — both are purging. The liver, gall bladder, Triple Burner, pericardium, heart and small intestine are all fire; the spleen, stomach, kidneys, lungs, large intestine and bladder are all damp; the twelve channels have this summary incipience. Zhang Ji, after taking the pulse at the hand, took it again at Fuyang and Taixi, meaning precisely that the three yang take the stomach as master and the three yin take the kidneys as master. Zhonglü says: The circulation of the twelve channels of hands and feet and the exterior–interior pairs of the six qi are detailed separately in a diagram, which my father worked out on his own.

《人身類·內經尺寸脈 The Inner Classic's Pulses at the Cubit and Inch》

1
○尺外以候腎，尺里以候腹中。胕上左，外以候肝，內以候鬲；右，外以候胃，內以候脾。上胕上右，外以候肺，內以候胸中；左，外以候心，內以候膻中。前以候前，後以候後。上竟上者，胸喉中事也；下竟下者，少腹腰股膝脛足中事也。微論曰：外以候陽，內以候陰。腎肝胃肺心皆近背之陽，故從外取；腹鬲脾胸與膻中皆近腹之陰，故從內取也。大小腸皆在下焦腹中，故候之尺裏。樞要亦以左尺主小腸、膀胱、前陰之病，右尺主大腸、後陰之病。今脈訣乃以大小腸候之上部，限右尺候三焦，謬矣。然愚者又有說。
○ The outer side of the cubit reads the kidneys, the inner side of the cubit reads the abdomen. Above it on the left, the outer side reads the liver and the inner the diaphragm; on the right, the outer reads the stomach and the inner the spleen. Above that, on the right, the outer reads the lungs and the inner the chest; on the left, the outer reads the heart and the inner the chest centre. The front reads the front and the back reads the back. The uppermost reads matters in the chest and throat; the lowermost reads matters in the lower abdomen, loins, thighs, knees, shins and feet. The Subtle Discussion says: The outer reads the yang and the inner the yin. The kidneys, liver, stomach, lungs and heart are all near the yang of the back, so they are taken from the outer side; the abdomen, diaphragm, spleen, chest and chest centre are all near the yin of the belly, so they are taken from the inner side. The large and small intestines are both in the lower burner in the abdomen, so they are read at the inner side of the cubit. The Essentials of the Pivot also has the left cubit govern illnesses of the small intestine, bladder and front orifice, and the right cubit illnesses of the large intestine and rear orifice. The present Pulse Formulas read the large and small intestines in the upper region and restrict the right cubit to reading the Triple Burner — a mistake. But the Foolish One has another view.

《人身類·六表裏脈法 The Method of the Six Exterior–Interior Pulses》

1
○尺寸六表裏，出于難經，故王叔和專于人迎寸口，分三部九候，倘經所謂引而伸之，可千可萬者乎？寸難曰：假令心脈急甚者，肝邪干心也；心脈微急者，膽邪干小腸也。豈非岐伯從其位，越人脈經從其絡，互徵巧取，亦何悖焉？
○ The six exterior–interior pairs at the cubit and inch come from the Classic of Difficulties, so Wang Shuhe concentrated on the ren ying and the inch-mouth and divided the three regions and nine pulses — is this not what the Classic means by "draw it out and extend it, and it can be a thousand or ten thousand"? The [Classic of] Difficulties on the inch says: Suppose the heart pulse is very tight: it is liver evil invading the heart; if the heart pulse is slightly tight, it is gall-bladder evil invading the small intestine. Is it not that Qibo followed the positions and Yueren's Pulse Classic followed the collaterals, verifying each other and taking them skilfully — what contradiction is there?

《人身類·八奇經九道法 The Method of the Eight Extraordinary Channels and Nine Paths》

1
○二維主一身之表裏，以乾坤言也：陽由外踝上行衛分，陰由內踝上行榮分。二蹺起跟中，主一身左右之陰陽，以東西言也：足少陰太陽之別脈，分內外踝，會目內眥者也。督循背，任循腹，衝夾臍，以南北言也，皆起于會陰，而三焦命門與督任衝相通。帶脈橫束腰，以六合言也，起季脅足厥陰章門穴，足少陰當十四椎屬帶。陽維病寒熱，陰維病心痛，陽蹺之病陰緩陽，陰蹺之病陽緩陰急，督病脊強而厥，任病結疝瘕聚，衝病逆氣裏急，帶病腹滿腰寒。傷寒傳以足經，連奇經也。李瀕湖以九道診尺寸，其理微矣。李士材曰：足六經已盡周身上下之脈絡，而手經已在其內。
○ The two linking vessels govern the exterior and interior of the whole body, speaking in terms of qian and kun: the yang rises from the outer ankle and moves in the defensive division, and the yin rises from the inner ankle and moves in the nutritive division. The two heel vessels start in the heel and govern the yin and yang of the left and right of the body, speaking in terms of east and west: they are the branch vessels of foot shaoyin and taiyang, dividing at the inner and outer ankles and meeting at the inner corner of the eye. The Governing vessel follows the back, the Conception vessel the belly, and the Penetrating vessel flanks the navel, speaking in terms of south and north; all start at the perineum, and the Triple Burner and Gate of Life communicate with the Governing, Conception and Penetrating vessels. The Girdle vessel binds the waist horizontally, speaking in terms of the six directions; it starts at the free ribs at the Zhangmen point of foot jueyin, and foot shaoyin at the fourteenth vertebra belongs to the girdle. Illness of the yang linking vessel is chills and fever, of the yin linking vessel heart pain; illness of the yang heel vessel is slackness of yin and [tension of] yang, of the yin heel vessel slackness of yang and tension of yin; illness of the Governing vessel is stiffness of the spine with reversal, of the Conception vessel knotted hernias and accumulations, of the Penetrating vessel counterflow qi and inner urgency, and of the Girdle vessel abdominal fullness and cold loins. Cold damage is transmitted through the foot channels, which connect with the extraordinary vessels. Li Binhu diagnosed the nine paths at the cubit and inch — subtle is his principle. Li Shicai says: The six foot channels already cover all the vessels and collaterals of the body above and below, and the hand channels are already within them.

《人身類·左右分司之徵 Evidence for the Division of Functions between Left and Right》

1

○蒸浴發散最捷，然陽弱之人傷氣。內經曰：煙垢落，賊風入深。此一理也。臍以下宜椒浴，足宜夜浴，最養人，面則日盥爽神。指甲內垢，以白梅與皂角洗之。漆在身手洗不去，以油洗之。桐油黏手，稻草末擦之。
○ Steam baths are the quickest for dispersing, but in those with weak yang they harm the qi. The Inner Classic says: When the sooty dirt falls off, the thief-wind enters deep. This is one principle. Below the navel a pepper bath is good, and the feet should be bathed at night, which is most nourishing; the face washed daily refreshes the spirit. Dirt under the fingernails is washed with smoked plums and soap-pods. Lacquer on the body that cannot be washed off is washed with oil. When tung oil sticks to the hands, rub with powdered rice straw.

《人身類·緩筋法 Methods of Relaxing the Sinews》

1
○古云：書楙緩筋，木瓜能治腳氣。楙，木瓜也。今俗人見足麻，則一草貼眉上。凡人身上結筋，以木瓢三擊之自散。中履曰：一心試法，久而筋舒。然心定則氣血和，此理不可忽也。
○ The ancients said: Writing [the character] mao relaxes the sinews, and the papaya can cure leg qi. Mao is the papaya. Today when common people find their feet numb, they stick a blade of grass on their eyebrow. For knotted sinews anywhere on the body, strike them three times with a wooden ladle and they disperse of themselves. Zhonglü says: Try the method with a single mind, and in time the sinews relax. But when the mind is settled the qi and blood are harmonious; this principle must not be neglected.

A strong double-brewed liquor of Jila, brewed with safflower, pepper and walnuts in the ferment, is effective for frequent urination.

1

Recently there have been people skilled at treating illness by moxibustion of people's shadows. Those who needle and moxa people's shadows nowadays first hide poison on their fingers and press it on the painful spot, and then moxa the shadow, so that the person's skin hurts.

1
○人骨節中有涎，所以轉動滑利。中風則涎上潮，咽喉裏響，以藥壓下，俾歸骨節可也。若吐其涎，時間快意，枯人手足，縱活亦為廢人。小兒驚風亦不可吐涎。
○ There is a fluid in the joints of the human bones, which makes their movement smooth. If one makes the person vomit the fluid, it feels pleasant for the moment, but it dries up the hands and feet, and even if he lives he will be crippled. In children's fright-wind too one must not make them vomit the fluid.

1
○皇甫牧玉匣記：李處厚言驗傷，以糟或灰湯薄之，都無傷跡；若以新赤油傘日中覆之，以水沃尸，其跡必見。無骸尸，于舊停處布熱灰作人形，祀而散生豆其上，有傷處豆自爆。
○ The Jade Casket Record of Huangfu Mu: Li Chuhou said that in examining injuries, if one applies wine lees or ash-water, no trace of injury appears; but if one covers [the body] with a new red oiled umbrella in the sun and pours water over the corpse, the traces will certainly appear. If there is no skeleton, spread hot ash in the shape of a person at the place where the corpse once lay, make offerings, and scatter raw beans on it; where there are injuries, the beans pop by themselves.

《人身類·留屍法 Methods of Preserving Corpses》

1
○李預服玉，其尸暑中不殠。黃金塞竅亦不腐，灌澒更效，或以茶嚼澒納鼻，亦可暑中不殠。漢書言污辱呂雉尸，以水銀斂也。孛露國有一拔爾撒摩樹，脂塗尸，千年不朽。確辨錄曰：有徒以坐脫販其師者，礦合婆律也，乾澒納鼻，而耨香熏為肉身者也。寡欲恬澹，氣盡而逝，心不係戀，煖從頂脫，尸為遺蛻，久之自干，小乘專致，常然豈足怪乎？骨剛者不壞，志凝精結，有成塊燒不壞者，此舍利子之所由來也。物理所曰：童男髮根可黏起舍利，乳香久留能生舍利。
○ Li Yu ate jade, and his corpse did not stink in the heat. Stopping the orifices with gold also prevents decay, and pouring in mercury is even more effective; or chewing mercury with tea and putting it into the nose also keeps it from stinking in the heat. The History of the Han says that the corpse of Lü Zhi, when defiled, had been laid in mercury. In Peru there is a balsam tree whose resin smeared on a corpse keeps it from decaying for a thousand years. The Record of Sure Distinctions says: There are disciples who peddle their masters as having died sitting in meditation, mixing ore with borneol, putting dried mercury in the nose and fumigating with musk and incense to make a "flesh body." When someone of few desires and calm detachment passes away as his qi is exhausted, his mind attached to nothing, the warmth leaves through the crown of the head and the corpse becomes a sloughed shell, which in time dries of itself; as with the single-minded devotion of the Lesser Vehicle, this is a common thing — what is there to marvel at? Those with hard bones do not decay, and when the will congeals and the essence knots there are lumps that do not burn, which is the origin of sarira. The Treatise on the Principles of Things says: The hair roots of a virgin boy can pick up sarira, and frankincense kept long can produce sarira.

1

《人身類·人異 Strange People》

1
○向稱穿胸、飛首、大人、小人。神異經言北海人長二千里，殆如稱修羅乎？若王莽時巨無霸，則見漢書者也。山海經：小人國有犁靈之尸。神異經言：鵠國人七寸，嘗畏鵠吞；西北荒人長一分。異苑載張仲師長尺二寸。東方朔知巨靈之類是也。王世懋二酉委譚言：徐方伯時在甘州，張大參在涼州，破土得小棺二三尺，啟視，須鬢老人，多紗帽紅袍者，眾喧然，遂止，以祭文掩之。二君皆目擊可信人也。存而不論，寧獨六合外哉？馬觀曰：占城國有女人目無瞳，夜寢飛頭，食人家小兒。則飛首之說可類推也。外紀言：一種鬚眉畢具，特手指相連如鳧爪。又唱蘭達地曾于海中獲一女人，與食亦食，亦肯為人役使，但不能言，其一身有肉皮下垂至地，如衣袍服者。中通曰：至于雌雄異類、有情無情之變，則綱目人□一篇悉矣。自顯而言，皆氣蒸之所造而化也；自微而言，皆意識之所造而化也。聖人知其故而明其理以化之，意識習氣，用歸中正，一切物理皆不足以驚之，此所以動頤而易簡也。
○ It has long been told of the pierced-chest people, the flying heads, the giants and the dwarfs. The Classic of Divine Marvels says that the people of the North Sea are two thousand li tall — like the asuras, perhaps? As for Ju Wuba in Wang Mang's time, he appears in the History of the Han. The Classic of Mountains and Seas: the land of the little people has the corpse of Liling. The Classic of Divine Marvels says: The people of the Swan country are seven inches tall and always fear being swallowed by swans; the people of the northwestern wilds are a tenth of an inch tall. The Garden of Marvels records Zhang Zhongshi as a chi and two inches tall. Dongfang Shuo's knowledge of the giant spirit is of this kind. Wang Shimao's Trivial Talks of Two You says: When Commissioner Xu was in Ganzhou and Councillor Zhang in Liangzhou, on breaking ground they found small coffins two or three chi long; opening them they saw bearded old men, many in gauze caps and red robes; the crowd made an uproar, so they stopped, and covered them over with a sacrificial address. Both gentlemen saw it with their own eyes and are trustworthy men. "Acknowledged but not discussed" — is that only for what is beyond the six directions? Ma Guan said: In the country of Champa there are women whose eyes have no pupils, whose heads fly off at night as they sleep to eat the children of other households. So the story of flying heads can be inferred by analogy. The Record of Places Outside says: There is a kind [of people] with beards and eyebrows complete, only their fingers are webbed like ducks' feet. Also, Holland once caught a woman in the sea who ate when given food and was willing to work for people, but could not speak; she had a flap of skin and flesh hanging from her body to the ground like a robe. Zhongtong says: As for the changes between male and female, different species, the sentient and insentient, the "Man" section of the Compendium covers them fully. Speaking of the manifest, all are created and transformed by steaming qi; speaking of the subtle, all are created and transformed by consciousness. The sages, knowing the reasons and understanding the principles, transformed them, turning consciousness and habitual tendencies to centrality and uprightness, so that no principles of things could startle them; this is why "moving the jaw" is easy and simple.

《人身類·積想出神 Accumulated Thought Producing an Apparition of the Spirit》

1
○破色心論曰：實無而成。智證傳言：晝夜念須曼那等，便夢與之從事，因悟一切諸法皆如是也。專氣致柔，久亦有出神事，不可不一看破。中通曰：心體本無也，隨習專而成，聖人知之，一見永不再見，惟與萬世泯于當務而已。中履曰：心官正用曰思，徇相曰想，學以濟思，即以泯想。
○ The Treatise Refuting Form and Mind says: In reality there is nothing, yet it takes shape. The Record of Wise Testimony says: One who thought of Sumana and others day and night dreamed of having intercourse with them, and thereby realized that all dharmas are like this. Concentrating the qi and attaining softness, in time there are also cases of the spirit leaving the body; one must see through this once and for all. Zhongtong says: The substance of the mind is originally nothing, and takes shape by concentrated habit; the sages knew this, and having seen it once never saw it again, but merged with ten thousand ages in attending to what was pressing. Zhonglü says: The proper use of the mental faculty is called thinking (si), and following images is called imagining (xiang); learning is to aid thinking and thereby to dissolve imagining.

《人身類·人生通理 General Principles of Human Life》

1
○語曰：北人包頭，南人束腰泉漳人赤足著屐而包頭。冬則肚暖，夏則肚冷。聾者語闞，啞者多笑。甚驚無唾，甚愧脫頤，縱樂神耗，大恐精流。手甲自傷，忌水與露。以齒咬物，其瘡毒深。頭垢搔燒卻蜈，以解毒也；拔鬢叩齒勝鬼，以壯神也。然毒以元氣靜正而自解，神以不可知而更壯，醫悟此乎？潛草曰：強弱本乎父母，仁智擴於教學，頤養因乎窮通，色脈關乎憂喜，日度既分溫冷，山川亦別粗秀，氣化之淖，天稟人習皆然。舉所不知則駭，直告其故反疑。人者，天地之心，精義入神，利用安身，其最切也。醫貫三才，人子須知；易徵節度，此身為要。
○ A saying goes: Northerners wrap their heads and southerners bind their waists people of Quan and Zhang go barefoot in clogs and wrap their heads. In winter the belly is warm and in summer the belly is cold. The deaf shout when they talk and the dumb laugh a lot. Great fright leaves no saliva, great shame makes the jaw drop, indulgence in pleasure exhausts the spirit, and great fear makes the essence flow. When one wounds oneself with a fingernail, one must avoid water and dew. When one bites something with the teeth, the poison of the sore is deep. Scratching dirt from the head and burning it repels centipedes, to counteract poison; pulling out sideburns and clicking the teeth overcomes ghosts, to strengthen the spirit. But poison is dispelled of itself by the stillness and uprightness of the primal qi, and the spirit is strengthened all the more by the unknowable — have physicians grasped this? The Qian Drafts say: Strength and weakness come from one's parents, humaneness and wisdom are expanded by teaching and learning, nourishment depends on poverty or success, complexion and pulse are linked with worry and joy; the sun's degrees divide warm and cold, and mountains and rivers too distinguish the coarse and the refined; such is the mire of the transformations of qi, and so are Heaven's endowment and human habit. Bring up what they do not know, and they are startled; tell them the reason plainly, and they doubt it instead. Man is the heart of Heaven and Earth; refining the meaning until it enters the spirit, making use of things and settling the body is what is most pressing. Medicine runs through the three powers, which every son must know; the Changes attests the measures, and this body is what matters.

《人身類·參同契 The Cantong qi》

1
○魏伯陽以《易》衍真丹，正理玄施，黃包匡廓，轂輻紀綱，鄞鄂律歷，應符龍虎，兩弦流戊就已，兌坤得朋，享其乾元，伏逆化幾，本冒如斯者也。陳抱一發揮一堅密身，名曰金丹，築基煉已，緣熟本忘，彼黃冶容成竊以炫媚耳。朱子曾注之。宓山愚者曰：兩折三番之火候，泯中用二之交輪，坎離濟化，大道固然，一鏤丸一革囊，大小皆符者也。聖人與民當務而已，守尸鬼窟，大乘呵之。漢志：老莊，道家；神仙，方技也。胡映日曰：朱子卦變合列一圖，雜卦參同，無不妙葉，仙幡老莊，養生幡仙，曷怪焉？
○ Wei Boyang used the Changes to develop the true elixir: correct principle and mysterious bestowal, yellow wrapping and frame, hub and spokes, net and cords, embryo and calendar, tallies matching dragon and tiger, the two quarters flowing from wu to ji, dui and kun gaining friends, enjoying the primal qian, hiding and reversing to transform the incipient — such is the general frame. Chen Baoyi developed it as "the one firm, secret body," called the golden elixir: laying the foundation and refining the self, when conditions ripen the root is forgotten; the yellow smelters and Rongcheng stole it to make a show of seduction. Master Zhu once annotated it. The Foolish One of Mishan says: The fire-phasing of two folds and three turns, the alternating wheel of using the two that merge in the centre, kan and li assisting and transforming — such is indeed the great Way; a carved pill or a leather bag, large or small, all tally. The sages simply attend with the people to what is pressing; guarding the corpse in the ghosts' cave is rebuked by the Great Vehicle. The bibliographic treatise of the History of the Han: Laozi and Zhuangzi are Daoists; immortals are among the techniques. Hu Yingri says: Master Zhu's hexagram changes are combined in a diagram, and the Miscellaneous Hexagrams and the Cantong are all in wonderful accord; immortals banner Laozi and Zhuangzi and nourishers of life banner the immortals — what is strange about that?
