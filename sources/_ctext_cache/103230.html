《神鬼方術類 Spirits, Ghosts and Occult Arts》

《神鬼方術類·心神自靈說 On the Mind Making Spirits Efficacious》

1
○神本靈也，而憑物觸幾，實以心顯，或獨以通之，或眾以聚之。造一像焉，專祀之，眾禱之，其效歸此，則自靈矣。彼瘞像放光而後興修、香灰撮藥而多服驗者，誰灼其故耶？神不可知，在可知中，固非人之所知也；可知之靈，以不可知而靈，尤人之所不知者也。有以信致專者，即有以疑致畏者，即有以不信致勇者，此其機一，何神乎？積想不已，能生勝氣，人心無形，其力最大，是也。故曰：有體物之鬼神，即有成能之鬼神，即有作怪之鬼神，權在自已，正已畢矣，彼如我何？聖人知之，故能轉物。
○ Spirits are inherently efficacious, but relying on things and touching off the incipient, they are in fact manifested through the mind, sometimes connecting with it singly, sometimes gathering it collectively. Make an image, worship it exclusively, let the multitude pray to it, and its efficacy returns to it; then it becomes efficacious by itself. As for buried images that emit light and are then restored, and incense ash taken as medicine that is proved effective by many who take it, who can make clear the reason? Spirits are unknowable, yet within the knowable, and certainly not what people know; the efficacy of the knowable is efficacious through the unknowable, which is even more what people do not know. Where there is concentration produced by belief, there is fear produced by doubt, and courage produced by disbelief; the mechanism here is one — what is spiritual about it? Accumulated thought without ceasing can produce a prevailing qi; the human mind has no form, yet its power is the greatest; this is it. Hence it is said: There are ghosts and spirits that embody things, there are ghosts and spirits that accomplish abilities, and there are ghosts and spirits that cause prodigies; the power lies in oneself, and when one has rectified oneself, it is done — what can they do to me? The sages knew this, and so could transform things.

《神鬼方術類·致夢法 Methods of Inducing Dreams》

1
○洞冥有懷夢草，靈廞有致夢符。王少夫言：夢是智影，法以緣因，如既絡之馬，臥則逸去，然經絡過即脫，亦馴，其神不昧，反來告形，離形之物，便通前後，因而我為汝夢，汝為我夢，不間二形，或越千里，不必相與，應在數載，受命如向，惟心所造，又何疑哉？秘術致夢，取其人之所甚耆者，如自書畫類，朱標姓名，以狐刲商陸香熏之，向月滿時呼離趾，持唵藍悉怛吽，七七日，以所欲致夢老畫圖置其位下，久則如願矣，或納牖而致其人席臥之，尤捷。郭璞赤豆刺心，即此故乎？東坡叱黃衣人，故是正眼。
○ The Dongming [Record] has the dream-cherishing herb, and the Lingqin has a talisman for inducing dreams. Wang Shaofu says: Dreams are shadows of the intellect, governed by conditions and causes; like a haltered horse, when lying down it runs off, yet once past the halter it gets free, and is also tame; its spirit is not darkened and comes back to report to the form; a thing apart from the form then communicates before and after, so that I dream for you and you dream for me, not separated by two forms, sometimes across a thousand li, without needing to be together, responding over several years, receiving commands like an echo; it is created only by the mind — what is there to doubt? The secret art of inducing dreams takes what the person greatly loves, such as his own calligraphy or painting, marks the name in vermilion, fumigates it with fox-[cut] pokeweed incense, calls Lizhi toward the full moon and recites om ram sitat hum for forty-nine days, then places the picture to be dreamt of beneath his place, and in time one gets one's wish; or putting it through the window and making the person sleep on it is quicker still. Was Guo Pu's piercing the heart with red beans for this reason? Dongpo's scolding the man in yellow was a truly correct eye.

《神鬼方術類·物靈可導 Creatures' Spirit Can Be Guided》

1
○蘇鶚言方土以丹砂養蠅虎，使分五隊舞涼州。楊千度以靈砂餌胡猻。今世教鼠、教蛙、教黃雀、畫眉者比比矣。
○ Su E says that a magician fed jumping spiders on cinnabar and made them dance the Liangzhou in five troupes. Yang Qiandu fed monkeys on spirit sand. Nowadays those who train rats, frogs, yellow sparrows and thrushes are everywhere.

《神鬼方術類·磁吸引術 The Art of Magnetic Attraction》

1
○賣丸者燒香入金蝦蟆口，人驚視之，故買其藥，蓋與木馬自走、紙人自舞同法，一處用鐵漿，一處用磁吸也。今治氣聾者，銜鐵而裹磁置耳；氣上衝者，服磁而以鐵汁塗臍；皆此理也。磁石拒棋者，取雞骨作針，磨鐵搗之，以和磁石，日塗其頭，曝乾之，置局上，即相擊不休，漢書所謂斗棋，即此術乎？
○ Pill sellers put burning incense into the mouth of a golden toad, and people watch in amazement and so buy their drugs; it is the same method as wooden horses walking by themselves and paper men dancing by themselves, using iron slurry in one place and magnetic attraction in another. Nowadays for deafness from qi, one holds iron in the mouth and puts wrapped lodestone in the ear; for qi rushing upward, one takes lodestone and smears iron juice on the navel; all on this principle. For lodestone repelling chess pieces, take chicken bone, make it into needles, grind iron and pound it, mix it with lodestone, smear the heads with it daily and dry them in the sun, and placed on the board they strike one another without stopping; is what the History of the Han calls fighting chess this art?

《神鬼方術類·解巫詛法 Methods of Undoing Witchcraft Curses》

1
○漢巫蠱言桐木人，蓋以剛桐刻其人，而以藥血詛咒魘之也。詛必夜中乘人遊房，神迷失守，然後能制人之魂。若旦夕端坐，持唵藍金剛咒八十一遍，作劍訣，出門入戶，則彼不能吸。尋常床眉畫八卦，書悉怛多般怛囉，懸軒轅鏡，則術力不能入。由此觀之，何如誠正一方更易簡乎？
○ The witchcraft of the Han speaks of tung-wood figures, carved of hard tung in the person's likeness and cursed with drugs and blood to bewitch him. The curse must be done at midnight while the person's spirit wanders from the room, when the spirit is confused and loses its guard, and then it can control the person's soul. If morning and evening one sits upright reciting the om ram Vajra spell eighty-one times and makes the sword gesture going out and coming in, they cannot draw him in. Ordinarily, if the eight trigrams are drawn on the bed lintel, sitatapatra is written, and a Xuanyuan mirror is hung, the power of the art cannot enter. Seen from this, how much simpler is the single prescription of sincerity and rectitude?

《神鬼方術類·制雌魘法 Methods of Controlling Female Bewitchment》

1
○沈周曰：教坊供白眉神，朔望以手帕針刺神而而收之，與子弟狎，則佯怒以帕擲其面，令拾之，則足以魘其魄，若辭其人，則樶物入水投火，便焦急而去。知者常佩雄精，持婆珊婆演底，然後入，則不為所魘。故曰思螺鶴草，佩之孝順，似松小草，忌置席間。一曰：取姅帕裹蝦蟆，於廁前一尺入地五寸埋之，則孝順。博物志曰：尸布在戶，婦人留連。注：月布也。陰識傳章懷太子註：臘日市豬肝塗灶，令婦孝。則當時原有此種魘法也。
○ Shen Zhou says: The Music Academy worships the White-Browed God; on the new and full moons they prick the god with needles through a handkerchief and keep it; when flirting with young men, they feign anger and throw the handkerchief in their face, making them pick it up, which is enough to bewitch their souls; and if they leave the woman, she puts things in water or fire, and they grow anxious and leave [their homes]. Those who know always wear realgar and recite Vasantavayanti before entering, and then are not bewitched. Hence it is said that the siluo crane herb worn makes one filial, and the little pine-like herb must not be put among the mats. One says: Wrap a toad in a menstrual cloth and bury it five inches deep a chi in front of the privy, and [the wife] will be filial. The Treatise on Curiosities says: If a corpse cloth is at the door, women linger. The commentary: It is a menstrual cloth. Crown Prince Zhanghuai's commentary on the biography of Yin Shi: On the La day buy pig's liver and smear the stove to make the wife filial. So such bewitchment methods existed in those times.

《神鬼方術類·入出辟邪法 Methods of Warding Off Evil Going in and Out》

1
○凡入山牽白犬、白雞與鹽，則藥寶并出。百步外口呼靈嶽，或呼林林央央，則無恙，一作林兵。又曰：入山默念儀方，即不見狼虺；默念儀黍，即不見虎。折後裙三折攝腰間，則蛇不近。得虎光入土之白石佩之。凡渡江河，朱書禹字及手書土字，除驚恐。行山慮迷，握蚃蟲一枝於手，無恙，蚃即上誦。又神龜前左肩臑骨，人穿以行，則不迷山。此出道家言。成式載婆珊婆演底，則華嚴咒也。人心有所依歸，前動他慮，則邪氣退避，此內定之力也。有以乾元亨利貞入咒者，推此可知。北齊權會乘騾夜行，忽二人引之失路，會怪之，誦易經上篇未盡，二人忽散。陳徐陵病篤，子份燒香跪誦孝經三日，陵疾豁然愈。信得及否？
○ When entering the mountains, lead a white dog and a white chicken and carry salt, and medicines and treasures will appear. Beyond a hundred paces, call out "Lingyue" or "linlin yangyang," and there will be no harm; one version writes "linbing." It is also said: On entering the mountains silently recite "yifang," and one will not see wolves or vipers; silently recite "yishu," and one will not see tigers. Fold the back of the skirt three times and tuck it into the waist, and snakes will not approach. Obtain a white stone where tiger-light has entered the earth and wear it. When crossing rivers, write the character Yu in vermilion and the character earth by hand to dispel fear. When travelling in the mountains, fearing to get lost, hold a sprig of xiang herb in the hand and there will be no harm; xiang is recited above. Also, if one wears the left humerus of the front shoulder of a divine tortoise, threaded, one does not get lost in the mountains. This comes from Daoist sayings. The Vasantavayanti recorded by Chengshi is a spell of the Avatamsaka. When the human mind has something to rely on and return to, and other thoughts stirred before are moved, evil qi retreats; this is the power of inner settledness. From this one can infer [why] some put "qian, origin, success, benefit, constancy" into spells. Quan Hui of the Northern Qi was riding a mule at night when two men suddenly led him astray; Hui found it strange and recited the first part of the Classic of Changes, and before he had finished the two men suddenly vanished. When Xu Ling of the Chen was gravely ill, his son Fen burned incense and knelt reciting the Classic of Filial Piety for three days, and Ling's illness suddenly cleared. Can one believe it or not?

《神鬼方術類·穿肉法 Methods of Piercing Flesh》

1
○穿耳者曲鉛條而夾耳之肉，久則自通，以鉛能入也。穿肩曳鐵鎖於腰而募化者，亦用此術，先飲曼陀酒，而以鉛條夾肩眼，三日即通。
○ Those who pierce ears bend a lead strip and clamp the flesh of the ear, and in time it opens by itself, because lead can penetrate. Those who pierce their shoulders and drag iron chains from their waists to collect alms also use this art, first drinking datura wine and then clamping the shoulder holes with lead strips, which open in three days.

《神鬼方術類·驅筏法 Method of Driving Rafts》

1
○江中木筏如山，百人鳴鼓牽曳，竟日行數里，夜斧柱翦牲咒之，行數十里。
○ Timber rafts on the river like mountains, hauled by a hundred men beating drums, go only a few li in a whole day; at night, when posts are axed, animals sacrificed and spells cast, they go several dozen li.

《神鬼方術類·互厭法 Methods of Mutual Suppression》

1
○妖人之能遁形者，厭以犬豕血，不復遁。能辟杖寄杖者，官印擊之，或向之伏辜。闢寄杖，桎將犯，索懸吊，餓之，能招實供。
○ Sorcerers who can make themselves invisible are suppressed with dog's or pig's blood and can no longer vanish. Those who can avoid beating by transferring it are struck with an official seal, or confess their guilt facing it. To counter transferred beating, shackle the offender, hang him with ropes and starve him, and he will confess the truth.

《神鬼方術類·憑物為怪 Prodigies Lodging in Things》

1
○黎久見江有纜精，巨纜久為怪也。遂昌雜錄言馮夢弼入蕃見馬蚪，馬蟥精也。郎瑛續已編亦載棕三舍人者，棕纜也，陳友諒敗將擲之，為眾魂所憑。
○ Li Jiu saw a cable spirit on the river, a huge cable that had long become a prodigy. The Miscellaneous Records of Suichang say that Feng Mengbi, entering foreign lands, saw a horse tadpole, a spirit of horse leeches. Lang Ying's Continued Gengsi Collection also records the Third Master of Palm, which was a palm-fibre cable that Chen Youliang's defeated general had thrown away, possessed by the souls of the multitude.

《神鬼方術類·託仙 False Immortals》

1
○方回虛谷間抄曰：石老化白鶴，太湖聖姑棺，皆誣說誑人耳，一人傳虛，眾人傳實。蜀雜記云：綿竹縣道人每歲有白雲載之而去，後乃知為蟒精所攝。按張道鳴鶴山中亦為蟒食，其徒張皇其說，今所以為神。陸游舊聞曰：蔡京禮劉混康，而林靈素以裝喑跛求醫，以符水咒之，立愈。元符三年，皂莢躍籠，黃娼鳴嗶，林為金狄亂華之說，其殆讖乎？
○ Fang Hui's Xugu Jianchao says: The old stone turning into a white crane and the coffin of the Holy Maiden of Lake Tai are all false tales to deceive people; one person transmits a fiction, and many transmit it as fact. The Miscellaneous Records of Shu say: A Daoist of Mianzhu county was carried off by a white cloud every year, and later it was learned he had been seized by a python spirit. I note that Zhang Dao on Mount Minghe was also eaten by a python, and his disciples exaggerated the story, which is why he is now a god. Lu You's Old Things Heard says: Cai Jing honoured Liu Hunkang, and Lin Lingsu treated those who feigned muteness and lameness by spells with talisman water, and they recovered at once. In the third year of Yuanfu, honey-locust pods leapt in the cage and yellow courtesans cried out, and Lin made his prophecy of the golden barbarians disordering China — was it not a prophecy?

《神鬼方術類·庚申殺尸義 The Meaning of Killing the Corpses on Gengshen》

1
○人血以庚申日灑物上為妖。道家為三彭守庚申，白香山皆信之。庚申，殺氣也。
○ Human blood sprinkled on things on a gengshen day makes prodigies. Daoists keep vigil on gengshen against the three Peng, and Bai Xiangshan believed in it too. Gengshen is killing qi.

《神鬼方術類·形家觸幾與古同征 Geomancers Touch the Incipient, Verified as in Antiquity》

1
○張續宣室志曰：李林甫宅即李衛公宅，泓師知其十九年盛，以高馬改中門而禍，謂離方不宜忽高也。張鷟朝野僉載言：定州鼓城縣魏全家為其母忽失明，問卜於王子貞，云：東來青衣人可療。果有人為持斧砍井上桑為曲轅，而母目復明，蓋曲桑蓋井所致也。故知形礙即心礙，干支加臨，皆一端幾也。此泓師即告張說以埋玉埋硫者。
○ Zhang Du's Record of the Xuan Room says: Li Linfu's house was Lord Li Wei's house; Master Hong knew it would flourish for nineteen years, and when it was altered with a high horse[-block] at the middle gate, disaster followed, because the li direction should not suddenly be raised. Zhang Zhuo's Comprehensive Records of Court and Country says: Wei Quan of Gucheng county in Dingzhou, because his mother suddenly lost her sight, consulted the diviner Wang Zizhen, who said: A man in blue coming from the east can cure her. Indeed someone came and with an axe cut the mulberry over the well to make a curved shaft, and the mother's sight returned, for the curved mulberry covering the well had caused it. Hence we know that obstruction of form is obstruction of mind, and the stems and branches impinging are all one aspect of the incipient. This Master Hong is the one who told Zhang Yue about burying jade and sulphur.

《神鬼方術類·驗針法 Method of Testing with the Needle》

1
○堪輿家驗土中有風有水，蓋有捷術。下水羅時，以雄磁旋針數回，而針搖者，其下必有翻棺倒尸之風；子午針橛移地不對者，下有尸；若針沉水者，即主有水。此理最奇。
○ Geomancers test whether there is wind or water in the ground by a quick art. When setting down the compass, turn the needle several times with male lodestone; if the needle trembles, below there must be wind that overturns coffins and upends corpses; if the north-south needle pivot shifts and does not align, there is a corpse below; if the needle sinks into the water, it means there is water. This principle is most strange.

《神鬼方術類·移洲法 Method of Moving Islands》

1
○火甄刻作雄龍，土塹刻作雌龍，埋雄於所主之洲，埋雌於所欲移之洲，則其年發大水，雌洲必交於雄洲矣。
○ Carve a male dragon from fired brick and a female dragon from earth, bury the male on the island to be the master and the female on the island to be moved, and that year there will be a great flood, and the female island will surely join the male island.

《神鬼方術類·聲能感幽 Sound Can Move the Hidden》

1
○作樂聲和，可格異類；洪鐘叩擊，鬼物帖然；要在冥心，乃能一體，而咒正所以冥心也。吳觀我太史曰：鬼神以誠為德。
○ Making music with harmonious sound can affect other kinds; when great bells are struck, ghosts become docile; the key lies in quieting the mind, and only then can one become one body with them, and spells are precisely for quieting the mind. The Grand Historian Wu Guanwo says: Ghosts and spirits take sincerity as their virtue.

《神鬼方術類·鬼幾 Signs of Ghosts》

1
○物理所曰：聲漸低者鬼，漸高者生魂。鬼叫時，童男女以甑聾耳聽之，可作占斷。凡鬼火色綠而細，人立則不見，蹲則見。甲戌年流寇未來，人見滿郊綠火，已而聞號奔聲，未幾寇至，死人如亂麻。野火固因膏光，然靈幾附物，陰氣先動，不可思議。
○ The Studio of the Principles of Things says: Voices growing fainter are ghosts, and growing louder are living souls. When ghosts cry, boys and girls listen with their ears covered by steamers, and divinations can be made. Ghost fires are green and fine, invisible when one stands and visible when one crouches. In the jiaxu year, before the roving bandits came, people saw green fires filling the countryside, then heard sounds of wailing and fleeing, and before long the bandits arrived and the dead lay like tangled hemp. Wild fires are certainly due to the glow of fat, yet the spiritual incipience attaches to things and yin qi stirs first — it is beyond conception.

《神鬼方術類·致人思術 Arts of Making People Long for One》

1
○秘抄云：野狐名紫，夜夜戴髑髏朝斗，取其心合布穀鳥腦與狗初生口中媚肉，和沉麝為香，使人聞之，則令人見思念，識破者視冷水則已。
○ The Secret Transcripts say: The wild fox called Zi wears a skull every night to worship the Dipper; take its heart, combine it with the brain of a cuckoo and the charming flesh from the mouth of a newborn puppy, mix with aloeswood and musk to make incense, and those who smell it will long to see one; those who see through it are cured by looking at cold water.

《神鬼方術類·見褢術 Arts of Seeing Monsters》

1
○萬畢曰：蛇脂為燈，置水中，即見諸怪物，或作蚖脂。贊寧曰：紫麻油以清水食之，目視精魅。鄧潛谷曰：鷹眼睛和乳研，夜注目中，三日見碧霄中物，吞梟目亦然，蝙蝠血滴目亦然。貞白曰：雲實燒之致鬼神，即今粘刺臭草也。猖術與牡荊同燒，青蛙入油鍋，即此意。
○ The Ten Thousand Infallible Arts say: Snake fat made into a lamp and set in water lets one see all kinds of monsters; or it is written lizard fat. Zanning says: Purple hemp oil taken with clear water lets the eyes see goblins. Deng Qiangu says: Hawk's eyes ground with milk and dripped into the eyes at night let one see things in the blue sky after three days; swallowing owl's eyes does the same, and so does dripping bat's blood into the eyes. Zhenbai says: Caesalpinia seeds burnt bring ghosts and spirits; it is the present sticky-thorn stinkweed. The art of the chang, burning with vitex and throwing green frogs into an oil pan, has the same idea.

《神鬼方術類·桃杙制鬼 Peach Pegs Control Ghosts》

1

《神鬼方術類·畜老辟邪 Keeping Animals to Ward Off Evil》

1

○ The Zhengao says: When studying the Way in the mountains, one should keep white chickens and white dogs, which can ward off evil. The Lingqin Secret Transcripts say: On Duanwu mix pig's-head blood with realgar and keep it; it can control ghosts. At noon on Duanwu smear a dog's mouth and nose with realgar and smoke it with camphor smoke, and this dog can control ghosts; Lei Huan's making a hunting dog see a fox demon is this. The Illustrated Compendium says: On the wuchen day of the fifth month sacrifice a pig's head to the stove and bury it at the four corners of a stone room, and evil will not enter. The Records of Accumulated Proofs: When the six domestic animals fight and speak human words, it is Mars descending and fire ghosts lodging in them; one should dig a pit to the north and bury pig and dog blood. Only chickens and geese belong to the yang qi of the east, and the cock is most masculine, so chicken ghosts are most virulent. In the time of Emperors Xuan and Yuan of the Han a hen turned into a cock; in the Yongguang period a chicken grew horns; Zhou Qi's hen ran off to the eaves-gutter and after seven days crowed like a cock; Liu Xiangzhi's family chicken grew three legs; all are called chicken calamities. Shaofu says: When an old chicken speaks human words, kill it and it ends; crowing at dusk betokens heavenly favour, and is not of the untimely-crowing kind. Burn a cock, put it in wine and drink it, and what one seeks will surely be obtained. The Taiqing Outer Arts say that in households keeping gu, the chickens fly away. Cui Shi's Monthly Ordinances say: In the twelfth month dismember a white chicken's head at the east gate to compound medicines. The Baize says: A chicken with four spurs and double wings is a dragon, and whoever kills it is struck by thunder. Wang Shanchang says: In Changsha a chick grew claws on its wings.

《神鬼方術類·致勝法 Methods of Winning》

1
○與人博奕，背孤擊虛，如甲子旬中，戌為孤，亥為虛，若為日辰所克，則必輸矣。人送邀會字來，必壓之，咒曰：伊諦彌諦彌揭羅諦。念萬遍，此即段成式所載擲投之咒也。入人家亦咒之。急則撏鬚叩齒。撇藍之巧，不用鉗夾綴鉛之詭，而多半隨其所呼，以乘除之數料之，謂之盆口。可見小道一枝，用志不分，亦能致此，蓋攝心則不懆，不懆則氣勝，乘除盈虛，觸於端幾，心自本靈也。可見懆之一字，百無所用。
○ In gambling with others, turn one's back to the solitary and strike the void; for example, in the jiazi decade xu is solitary and hai void; if one is overcome by the day and hour, one is sure to lose. When someone sends a written invitation, one must press it down and recite the spell: "Iti miti mikirati." Recited ten thousand times, this is the throwing spell recorded by Duan Chengshi. On entering someone's house one also recites it. In urgency, pluck one's beard and click one's teeth. The skill of piela uses no tricks of tongs or attached lead, yet mostly follows what one calls, estimating by the numbers of multiplication and division, and is called pen-kou. One can see that a minor art, practised with undivided will, can achieve this; for when the mind is gathered one is not agitated, and when not agitated the qi prevails, and multiplication and division, fullness and emptiness touch off the incipient, for the mind is originally efficacious. One can see that the single word agitation is of no use at all.

《神鬼方術類·制氣 Controlling Qi》

1
○上世如黃帝與蚩尤驅虎豹犀象應龍以戰，又如劉累豢龍，何也？曰：夫上世物各有官，如玄冥為水官，凡水中物各因其氣以制之，故周官猶有登蛟伐黿之說。陰符經曰：禽之制在氣。即且甘帶，而蜘蛛蜒蝸又能制之；蚊嘬鱉則鱉立死，而鱉骨又能熏蚊；雞食蜈蚣，蜈蚣又專嗜雞；鯉魚乘雨能飛，而鱉守之；啄木禹步而蟲出馬。凡物皆有好惡，無制則偏，五行相制，亦互相生也。然則狎龍驅虎之官猶可復乎？上古專簡而民質，聖人偶一出奇可也，使今日猶有狎龍驅虎之術，則天人紛紛矣。
○ In high antiquity, the Yellow Emperor and Chiyou drove tigers, leopards, rhinoceroses, elephants and winged dragons into battle, and Liu Lei reared dragons — how was this? I say: In high antiquity things each had their officials, as Xuanming was the official of water, and all creatures in the water were controlled by their qi; so the Offices of Zhou still speak of taking up jiao and attacking giant turtles. The Yinfu jing says: The control of creatures lies in qi. The centipede relishes snakes, yet spiders, millipedes and snails can control it; when mosquitoes bite turtles, the turtles die at once, yet turtle bones can smoke out mosquitoes; chickens eat centipedes, and centipedes especially crave chickens; carp can fly with the rain, but the turtle guards them; the woodpecker paces the Yu step and the insects come out. All things have likes and dislikes, and without control they become one-sided; the five phases control one another and also generate one another. Then can the offices of taming dragons and driving tigers still be restored? In high antiquity matters were simple and the people plain, and it was fine for a sage occasionally to produce a marvel; if today there were still arts of taming dragons and driving tigers, heaven and men would be in confusion.

《神鬼方術類·召魔 Summoning Demons》

1
○魔崇所附，隨月盛衰，月浸盈則人苦魔亦浸劇。星亦有為魔所藉者，故凡妖術測得其星相助相制，或用一草一石一禽獸以召魔，其魔即來。蓋綠月主人腦，其光浸盈，則性情之感腦受其動，人蓄物像於腦，於時撓亂其人，動其蓄像，作諸惡想，是由魔能測月，乘機借氣，而非月有施於魔也。彼先測某時某星相助相制，又能知人肉軀情態，便於誘引，緣此設惑，使人誤信某星有命已之權，轉相禳讖，亦非星之有助於魔也。月主腦，不獨人也，魚、蛤、犀、兔之類皆然。
○ The demons that possess people wax and wane with the moon; as the moon gradually fills, people suffer and the demons become gradually more violent. There are also stars that demons make use of, so all sorcery measures the stars that assist and control, or uses a herb, a stone, a bird or beast to summon demons, and the demons come at once. For the moon governs the human brain, and as its light gradually fills, the feelings stirred in the brain are moved; people store images of things in the brain, and at such times [demons] disturb the person, stir up his stored images and make him think all kinds of evil thoughts; this is because demons can gauge the moon and seize the opportunity to borrow qi, not because the moon does anything for the demons. They first gauge at what hour which star assists or controls, and can also know the state of people's bodies and feelings, so as to lure them, and on this basis set up delusions, making people mistakenly believe that some star has power over their fate, and in turn perform exorcisms and prophecies; it is not that the stars help the demons. The moon governs the brain not only in humans, but in fish, clams, rhinoceroses and hares alike.

1

○ Chu Yong says: Making water boil by a spell is done with a pig's bladder in the sleeve, aided by sleight of hand. Making smoke rise from jujubes by a spell is done by hiding drugs in the jujube, or hiding a mirror overhead to draw down the sun's essence; when the jujube is held to the mirror, after a while it chars. Throwing incense coins into water so that they vanish at once is done by burying water chestnut, mercury and other drugs in the ground for forty-nine days; when the drug is ready, a little is thrown into the water and the coins dissolve.

《神鬼方術類·入沸法 Method of Entering Boiling [Water]》

1

○ A cauldron at full boil can be carried in the hands for a hundred paces, which many find amazing; but this is the principle: the top boils while the bottom is not hot. When warming wine, the heat goes from the top down; the body and mouth of the pot are all hot while the wine poured is still cold, and only when the bottom of the pot is hot does the wine begin to heat; this can serve as proof. The Studio of the Principles of Things says: When true sesame oil is boiled to the extreme, at its extreme boiling it suddenly congeals and is cold, and a hand put in is not burnt; Cambodia uses this to try thieves.

For when yang qi is stifled it dies; take charcoal glowing red and put it in a jar and close it, and it becomes light charcoal; paper and banana leaves that do not burn are likewise stifled below and do not put out their tongues [of flame].

In the first month of winter of the guiwei year, at the offering in the Imperial Ancestral Temple, the guards were startled at night by a black thing like an ox, several zhang high, which ran from the Meridian Gate out through the Duan Gate. This was a black portent, while red insects had never occurred since antiquity.

《異事類·鼠徙 Rat Migrations》

1
○占曰：鼠無故皆夜去，則邑有兵。唐武德元年，李密、王世充隔洛相拒，密營中鼠一夕渡水。嘉靖時有群鼠銜渡。萬歷戊午、已未，江北有方鼠千萬，銜尾渡江南，蘆麥盡為所咋，其鼠頭方尾長。天啟時，田鼠糾結如桴，蔽江，入蘆葦，根苖立盡，張養默言短尾方喙，小於鼠而足方長。崇禎壬午，鼠銜尾渡江。
○ The divination says: When rats all leave at night for no reason, there will be war in the city. In the first year of Wude of the Tang, when Li Mi and Wang Shichong faced each other across the Luo, the rats in Mi's camp crossed the water in one night. In the Jiajing period swarms of rats crossed holding each other's tails. In the wuwu and jiwei years of Wanli, north of the Yangzi, millions of square rats crossed the river south holding each other's tails, and the reeds and wheat were all eaten by them; the rats had square heads and long tails. In the Tianqi period field rats bound together like rafts covered the river, entered the reeds and ate the roots and shoots in no time; Zhang Yangmo said they had short tails and square snouts, were smaller than rats, and had square long feet. In the renwu year of Chongzhen rats crossed the river holding each other's tails.

《異事類·釜飛釜鳴 Flying and Ringing Cauldrons》

1
○物理所云：牛膽塗釜，釜鳴；婦穢衣蒙釜，釜飛。又云：爨釜不沸者，有物如豚居之，去之無他。釜鳴欲飛，夫婦男作女拜，女作男拜，則厭之。又秘書云：瓦甑之契，投梟自止。注云：取甑書契字置墻上，有梟鳴時投之，則甑鳴自止。中通曰：釜受火氣大甚，通體皆紅，既紅則一釜全成火體矣，火性向上，釜既成火，故騰空飛起耳。然釜雖全成火體，而鐵性尚存，薄鐵不耐重火，故騰空則顫顫有聲。暄曰：甑上閉下熱，氣貫卻則鳴；釡竈近地，郤氣貫亦鳴。
○ The Studio of the Principles of Things says: When a cauldron is smeared with ox gall it rings; when covered with a woman's soiled clothes it flies. It also says: When a cooking cauldron will not boil, there is something like a piglet in it; remove it and all is well. When a cauldron rings and is about to fly, if husband and wife bow, the man in the woman's manner and the woman in the man's, it is suppressed. Also, the Secret Book says: The contract of the earthenware steamer — throw it at an owl and it stops. The commentary says: Write the character contract on the steamer and put it on the wall, and when an owl calls throw it, and the steamer's ringing stops by itself. Zhongtong says: When a cauldron receives very great fiery qi, its whole body is red, and when red the whole cauldron has become a body of fire; the nature of fire is to rise, and the cauldron having become fire, it flies up into the air. But though the cauldron has wholly become a body of fire, the nature of iron remains, and thin iron cannot bear heavy fire, so when it rises it trembles and makes a sound. Xuan says: When a steamer is closed above and hot below, if the qi passes back through it rings; when a cauldron and stove are near the ground, if the qi passes back it also rings.

《異事類·鐘鳴欲飛 Bells Ringing and About to Fly》

1
○銅山崩，洛鐘應，固也。大林寺鐘啞，其甬銑璺也。地中火氣迅坪而起，如雷之出，鐘適當之則鳴，其氣重則飛耳。梧州雲葢寺有銅鐘，上鑄漢太寶元年，愚按是南漢劉鋹時年號也。辛夘夏忽有聲，久之翁翁然韽，有動狀，愚以地梨、蒜、韭汁水潑之，以溺筩濕其地，遂止。
○ When the copper mountain collapsed, the bell at Luo responded — that is certain. The bell of the Dalin temple was mute because its shank and rim were cracked. When fiery qi in the earth rises swiftly like thunder coming out, and a bell happens to be right over it, it rings, and if the qi is heavy it flies. The Yungai temple at Wuzhou has a bronze bell cast with "first year of Taibao of the Han"; the Foolish One notes that this was a reign title of Liu Chang of the Southern Han. In the summer of the xinmao year it suddenly made sounds, and after a while hummed with a muffled ring and seemed to move; the Foolish One sprinkled it with water of water chestnut, garlic and chive juice and wet the ground with a chamber-pot, and it stopped.

《異事類·太歲肉 Taisui Flesh》

1
○往見小說言太歲飛入人家，如人頭，多竅而赤色。劉餗傳載董表儀家掘得肉塊，以為太歲，棄之河，亦無恙。又按異物考載：吳戍將鄧喜殺豬祠神，治畢懸之，忽見一人頭往食肉，喜射中之，咋咋作聲，繞屋三日，近赤祥也，後人白喜謀北叛，闔室受誅。京房易妖曰：山見葆江於邑，邑有兵，狀如人頭，赤色。得非所謂太歲乎？江鄰幾以徐積所埋之物如手者，即白澤圖之封，食之多力者也。山海經敦隅之山及開明南北東西海外並有視肉，郭云：聚肉如牛肝，有兩目，食之無盡。此皆封類可食者，升庵引作垹。霏雪錄：洪武乙亥，山陰天樂瀛掘得一物，如小兒臂，紅潤如生。蕭靜之食肉芝，如人手，肥紅。九真守陶潢土穴中得白物，如蛹，長數丈，割肉如豚，三軍盡食。
○ I once saw in fiction that Taisui flew into a person's house, like a human head, with many holes and red in colour. Liu Su's biographies record that Dong Biaoyi's family dug up a lump of flesh, took it for Taisui and threw it in the river, and no harm came. Also, the Examination of Strange Things records: Deng Xi, a garrison general of Wu, killed a pig to sacrifice to the gods and hung it up when done; suddenly a human head was seen coming to eat the meat; Xi shot and hit it, and it made a zaza sound and circled the house for three days — close to a red omen; later someone reported that Xi was plotting to defect to the north, and his whole household was executed. Jing Fang's Prodigies of the Changes says: When the mountain shows a baojiang in a city, the city will have war; it is shaped like a human head and red. Is this not what is called Taisui? Jiang Linji took the hand-like thing buried by Xu Ji to be the feng of the White Marsh Chart, which gives great strength to those who eat it. The Classic of Mountains and Seas says that on Mount Dunyu and beyond the seas south, north, east and west of Kaiming there are all shirou; Guo says: Gathered flesh like ox liver with two eyes, which never runs out however much is eaten. These are all of the edible feng kind; Sheng'an quotes it as feng with another character. The Records of Falling Snow: In the yihai year of Hongwu, Tianle Ying of Shanyin dug up a thing like a child's arm, red and moist as if alive. Xiao Jingzhi ate a flesh fungus like a human hand, plump and red. Tao Huang, prefect of Jiuzhen, found in an earth cave a white thing like a pupa, several zhang long, whose flesh when cut was like pork, and the whole army ate it.

《異事類·異像 Strange Images》

1
○崇禎辛巳，曾同姜如須過後湖，入一庵，後殿封鐍具施，乃開，皆裸佛交構形，凡數百尊，守者曰天地父母，前年大內發出者，其像皆女坐男身，有三頭六臂者，足下皆踏裸男女，累人背而疊之。考元成宗大德九年，天寧寺有秘密佛，即言此像，圓殿茜帽，已兆演揲。鄭所南久久書亦言塑裸佛與妖女合，是也，其言邪教淫殺尤甚。今聞外有刺馬僧，猶有食肉近女如此等者，但不取童男女血、生刺孕乳血以點佛唇為供養耳。然往往有異術，如斷肢複續之事，無乃如北史之押不盧乎？蓋自漢時即有西域幻人易牛馬首、指端出浮圖者矣。香山隩為舶口，呂宋人常往來，有三巴寺，隩婦至寺得內，謂之懺悔，又何疑真臘之陣毯乎？若葛蘭國浮屠有濁肌者，亦娶，如寄褐。唐房千里投荒雜錄曰：南方蠻以女配僧，曰師郎。愚者親見天雷苖中有師娘者，方許住庵，令人摩阿濕毗之腹祈子，則其俗然也。
○ In the xinsi year of Chongzhen I once passed Houhu with Jiang Rushu and entered a hermitage; the rear hall was sealed with locks, and when it was opened there were naked Buddhas in sexual union, several hundred in all; the keeper said they were "Father and Mother of Heaven and Earth," sent out from the palace the year before; all the images had female figures seated on male bodies, some with three heads and six arms, all treading on naked men and women piled on people's backs. I examine that in the ninth year of Dade of Emperor Chengzong of the Yuan the Tianning temple had secret Buddhas, meaning such images, and the round halls and red caps already presaged yandieer. Zheng Suonan's Jiujiu shu also says naked Buddhas were moulded in union with demon women, which is this; he says this heretical teaching is especially lewd and murderous. Now I hear that outside there are lama monks who still eat meat and approach women like this, only they do not take the blood of boys and girls or the milk-blood pierced from pregnant women to dot the Buddha's lips as offerings. But they often have strange arts, such as rejoining severed limbs — is this not like the yabulu of the History of the Northern Dynasties? For since the Han there have been conjurors of the Western Regions who changed the heads of oxen and horses and produced stupas from their fingertips. Xiangshan'ao is a harbour where people of Luzon constantly come and go; there is the São Paulo church, and women of the harbour go into the church and [are received] inside, which is called confession — why then doubt the zhentan of Cambodia? As for the monks of the country of Gelan who have zhuoji, they also marry, like jihe. Fang Qianli of the Tang, in his Miscellaneous Records of Banishment, says: The southern barbarians give their women to monks, and they are called master-husbands. The Foolish One saw for himself among the Tianlei Miao that only women called master-wives were allowed to live in the hermitages, and people had them rub the belly of Asvaghosa to pray for sons; such is their custom.

《異事類·奇牙發 Strange Teeth and Hair》

1
○外國嘗以貘齒充佛牙。按陳章對齊桓，胡充折齒三尺。仙鑒張氳言海蝦蟆齒方圓二尺。武夷志：魏王顱骨如鬥。智少隨家君嘉定州，州前凌雲寺有佛牙，長尺餘，以朱印之布施者，則印回供養。南史扶南傳：迎佛發，長一丈二尺，青紺色。劉敬升亦云月支國佛發盛以琉璃罌。此皆異物之尾也，如外國自有桃大如斗，而乃以王母蟠桃聞，猶不悟耶？佛以心宗為正法眼藏，此外示怪，皆毛道也。
○ Foreign countries have passed off tapir teeth as Buddha's teeth. I note that Chen Zhang, answering Duke Huan of Qi, said Hu Chong broke off a tooth three chi long. Zhang Yun in the Mirror of Immortals speaks of the teeth of the sea toad two chi square. The Gazetteer of Wuyi: The skull of the King of Wei was as large as a dou. When I, Zhi, was young I accompanied my father to Jiading prefecture, and in front of the prefecture the Lingyun temple had a Buddha's tooth over a chi long, which was stamped in vermilion for donors, who took the stamp home to worship. The History of the Southern Dynasties, in the biography of Funan, speaks of receiving a Buddha's hair twelve chi long, dark blue in colour. Liu Jingsheng also says that in the country of the Yuezhi the Buddha's hair was kept in a glass jar. These are all the hairs of strange creatures; just as foreign countries have peaches as large as a dou, yet are spoken of as the Queen Mother's immortal peaches — do people still not realize? The Buddha took the mind-school as the treasury of the true dharma eye; beyond this, displays of the strange are all the way of the hairy.

《異事類·北塞穹廬東西夷皆屋 Felt Tents beyond the Northern Frontier, Houses among the Eastern and Western Barbarians》

1 ○古稱毳帳，謂其氈也，或皮為之，北塞以外大抵如是。高句麗、日本、台灣皆有宮室。外紀諸國建屋各異：泰西則以山牆為正面；百爾西亞國一苑造於空際；墨是可都城於湖中，椿堅木而創起；拂即察有藍石，鋸板作瓦覆屋；弗沙國一殿開三十門；把都亞國有堂不立一柱，梁如人字相倚；好奇甚矣。東台僧孤朗言曾居西韃靻帳逾年，帳大數丈，往時封為吐麻憨，統其諸部，今封為吐失兔，各以皇姑嫁之，婿居別帳，有信設宴，則沐浴更衣而入，叩首隅坐，翼日天明悄然而出。其穹廬依河而居，不產五穀，食乳酪牛羊，其酒以馬乳為之。信佛，以方寸紙書百二圈，持唵嘛呢叭咪吽，見僧不受牛羊錢物，則愈敬，其所受咒書坐恆列於右方，貴右也。
○ What the ancients called felt tents means they are of felt, or made of hides, and beyond the northern frontier it is generally so. Goguryeo, Japan and Taiwan all have palaces and houses. The Record of the Outer Regions: The countries build houses differently: the Far West takes the gable wall as the front; in the country of Persia a garden is built in the air; Mexico has its capital in a lake, built on piles of hard wood; Flanders has blue stone, sawn into slabs to make tiles to roof houses; in the country of Fusha one hall has thirty doors; in the country of Padua there is a hall without a single column, its beams leaning on each other like the character ren — how fond of the strange they are! The monk Gulang of Dongtai said he once lived in the tents of the Western Tatars for over a year; the tents were several zhang across; formerly the chief was enfeoffed as Tumahan, ruling the tribes, and now as Tushitu; each is married to an imperial aunt, and the husband lives in a separate tent; when there is news and a banquet, he bathes, changes his clothes and enters, kowtows and sits in the corner, and at dawn the next day quietly leaves. Their felt tents are pitched along rivers; they produce no grain, but eat milk, curds, cattle and sheep, and make their wine from mare's milk. They believe in Buddhism, writing a hundred and two circles on paper an inch square and reciting om mani padme hum; when a monk does not accept cattle, sheep, money or goods, they revere him all the more, and the spell-writings they receive are always placed on the right of their seats, because the right is honoured.
