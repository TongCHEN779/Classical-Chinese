《金石類 Metals and Minerals》

《金石類·金 Gold》

1
○金生丹砂穴者上，瓜子、麥麩金生沙中。山金穴丈許，先見伴石，褐色，一頭如燒黑者，馬蹄塊金也。蔡絛云：政和間，益陽溪流出生金一塊，重四十九斤，故曰山赤於水。雲南葉子、大食國、加西蠟、西番回回錢，熟金也。葉子精於錠，細絲不足以盡之。赤金有紫霞，甚耎，無聲，而燒不黑。雜赤銅曰氣子，張公即有聲矣。制成者，有水銀金、雄黃金、硫金、曾青石綠金、石膽金、鍋鉛金。藥點者，有銅鐵鍮石金。熟無毒，生有毒，中毒者，鷓鴣肉解之。葛洪言：用豕負革、苦酒煉金百遍，即柔，或牡荊酒、石消之為水。其實驢馬脂皆能柔金。本草久服長生，妄也。塞竅留口，與灌汞同。
○ Gold that grows in cinnabar caves is the best; melon-seed and wheat-bran gold grow in sand. In a mountain gold mine, after about a zhang one first sees the companion stone, brown, with one end like something burnt black; this is horse-hoof lump gold. Cai Tao says: In the Zhenghe period a lump of raw gold weighing forty-nine jin came out of a stream at Yiyang, hence the saying that mountain [gold] is redder than water [gold]. The leaf gold of Yunnan, and that of the Arabs, of Castile and of the Muslim coins of the Western regions, is refined gold. Leaf gold is finer than ingots, and "fine silk" does not fully describe it. Red gold has a purple haze, is very soft, makes no sound, and does not turn black when heated. Gold mixed with red copper is called qizi, and when struck it rings. Of the manufactured kinds there are mercury gold, realgar gold, sulphur gold, azurite-malachite gold, chalcanthite gold and pot-lead gold. Of those produced by drug-touching there is copper, iron and brass gold. Refined gold is not poisonous, raw gold is; those poisoned are cured with partridge flesh. Ge Hong says: Gold refined a hundred times with pig's back-skin and bitter wine becomes soft, or it can be dissolved into water with vitex wine and saltpetre. In fact donkey and horse fat can both soften gold. The Materia Medica's claim that taking it long gives long life is nonsense. Blocking the orifices and leaving the mouth open is the same as pouring in mercury.

《金石類·土皆生金 All Earth Produces Gold》

1
○淘沙于水取黃金者，猶問其地；埽泥于衢，則無處不可，以衢中萬人行處有肥膩焉，日月蒸入，盝之澄之，重者在下，鉛硝煎之，白金出矣。中通曰：太陽所照之地皆生金，煖故也。通衢人足所磨，更煖，故生金較多。暄曰：黃本日精，白本月華，故近赤道之地多金銀。
○ Those who pan sand in water for gold still ask about the place; but sweep the mud of a thoroughfare and there is nowhere it will not do, because where ten thousand people walk in the thoroughfare there is grease, steamed in by sun and moon; wash it and settle it, the heavy part lies at the bottom, smelt it with lead and saltpetre, and white metal comes out. Zhongtong says: All land the sun shines on produces gold, because of the warmth. The thoroughfare, rubbed by people's feet, is warmer still, so produces more gold. Xuan says: Yellow is based on the essence of the sun and white on the splendour of the moon, so the lands near the equator have much gold and silver.

《金石類·分金爐 The Gold-Parting Furnace》

1
○重者在下，浮土在上，以次分焉，入鉛煮洗，所以分之也。其傾銷之罐，取旋窩細泥煎熬；大罐則兼鋼砂、瓦屑，萬杵成之，雖紅不裂。爐底與罐積而末之，加鉛洗下，然後分之，必有所得，不虛也。暄曰：分之有紅銅、蜜陀僧，其精者為銀。
○ The heavy part is at the bottom and the floating earth on top, separated in order; boiling and washing with lead is the way to part them. The crucibles for smelting are made of fine mud from the potter's wheel, boiled; large crucibles also include steel sand and potsherds, pounded ten thousand times, so they do not crack even when red-hot. The furnace bottoms and crucibles are accumulated and powdered, washed down with lead, and then parted, and something is surely obtained, not in vain. Xuan says: Parting yields red copper and litharge, the refined part of which is silver.

《金石類·金中出銀法 Method of Extracting Silver from Gold》

1
○金入銀者，甘鍋鑠化，每一兩投倭硫三錢觸之，俟冷，破甘鍋，金在底，銀則黑浮于面，入灰池煎。
○ Gold with silver in it is melted in a crucible, and to each tael three qian of Japanese sulphur is added to touch it; when cool the crucible is broken, the gold is at the bottom and the silver floats black on top, and it is smelted in the ash-pit.

《金石類·淡金變赤法 Method of Making Pale Gold Red》

1
○龍川志略曰：扶風開元寺僧傳兄子瞻以化金方，曰：得此方有死喪失官者，公若不為，正當傳矣。每淡金分數不足一分，以丹砂一錢益之，雜諸藥入甘鍋中煆之，鎔即傾出，其色斑斑，當再烹之，色勻乃止。子瞻後語陳希亮，陳試之，驗。坡曰：負此僧矣。後問陳公子慥，曰：吾父歸作此，買宅，病指癰而沒。餘謫筠，有僧儀價知之，為聰禪師言然，不私自無患。苕溪漁隱亦載葉生得乾銀法而受禍。又金曾經在塚及為釵釧，陶隱居謂之辱金，不可合煉。愚按：本淡者，可以朱砂變；其和銀者，以礬鹽之，則銀自白，金自赤；其有氣子者，別一法出之，色混有聲，即知為氣。中通曰：葉金以沙鋪之，每一葉一層沙，用鐵線縳定，以火煉之，則金微輕而色愈赤，蓋金本沙所生，故沙能遏金也。
○ The Records of Longchuan say: A monk of the Kaiyuan temple at Fufeng passed to my elder brother Zizhan a method of transforming gold, saying: Those who obtained this method have died or lost office; if you will not practise it, you are just the one to receive it. For pale gold short of fineness by a fen, add a qian of cinnabar, mix it with various drugs and fire it in a crucible, pour it out when molten, and if its colour is mottled, cook it again until the colour is even. Zizhan later told Chen Xiliang, and Chen tried it and it worked. Po said: I have betrayed that monk. Later I asked Chen's son Zao, who said: My father on his return made it and bought a house, and then died of an abscess on his finger. When I was banished to Yun, there was a monk Yijia who knew of this, and said to Chan Master Cong that it is so, but that if one does not use it for oneself there is no harm. Tiaoxi Yuyin also records that Master Ye obtained a method of drying silver and met with disaster. Also, gold that has been in a tomb or made into hairpins and bracelets Tao Yinju calls disgraced gold, which must not be used in compounding. The Foolish One notes: What is pale by nature can be transformed with cinnabar; that alloyed with silver is salted with alum, and then the silver whitens and the gold reddens of itself; that with qizi is brought out by another method; if the colour is mixed and it rings, one knows it has qi. Zhongtong says: Spread leaf gold with sand, a layer of sand to each leaf, bind it with iron wire and refine it in fire, and the gold becomes slightly lighter and redder, for gold is originally produced from sand, so sand can check gold.

《金石類·鍍金法 Method of Gilding》

1
○以汞和金塗銀器上，成白色，入火則汞去而金存，數次即黃。其鐵鍍金者，先用赤熟銅于鐵上畫花，以飛箔梅鍋內炸白，然後以金箔貼之。
○ Mix gold with mercury and paint it on silverware, which becomes white; put it in the fire and the mercury goes and the gold remains, and after several times it is yellow. For gilding iron, first draw patterns on the iron with red refined copper, fry it white in a plum pot with flying foil, and then apply gold leaf.

《金石類·金箔 Gold Leaf》

1
○隔碎金以藥紙，揮巨斧捶之，金已箔而紙無損。紙初褐色，久則烏金色。扇面篩金，箔管中隔紗數重，出金指大，可征金為至耎，以其粹也。
○ Fragments of gold are separated with drug-paper and beaten with a huge hammer, and the gold becomes leaf while the paper is undamaged. The paper is brown at first and in time turns black-gold. For gold-sprinkled fan surfaces, the leaf is put in a tube with several layers of gauze, and the gold comes out the size of a finger, which shows that gold is supremely soft because it is pure.

《金石類·洗試金石上金法 Method of Washing Gold off a Touchstone》

1
○以鹽洗，置濕地，胡桃油摩之，去。
○ Wash it with salt, put it on damp ground, rub it with walnut oil, and it comes off.

《金石類·罩金法 Method of Gold Glazing》

1
○炭燒黃金，再以鹽水調黃土塗燒之，從而滌之，及用焰硝、綠礬等分，水調傳金，置火上炙，色改即止，急入淨水洗刷而煏乾之，不黃再上，然能加外色而已，俗謂之炸金。
○ Heat the gold with charcoal, then coat it with yellow earth mixed with salt water and heat it, and then wash it; or mix equal parts of saltpetre and green vitriol with water, apply it to the gold and roast it over the fire, stopping when the colour changes, plunge it at once into clean water, scrub it and dry it over fire, and if not yellow, apply again; but this only adds outer colour, and is commonly called fried gold.

《金石類·銀 Silver》

1
○丱紅亂絲曰老翁鬚，丹房所貴銀筍也。有白如草根，有銜黑石者。外紀：曷噩刺銀丱至多。金加西蠟有四坑，金銀至賤。伯西爾國有銀河，潮溢而退，布地銀沙。
○ Ore with red tangled threads is called old man's beard, the silver shoots prized by alchemists. Some are white like grass roots, and some hold black stones. The Record of the Outer Regions: Hungary has a great deal of silver ore. Castile has four mines, and gold and silver are extremely cheap. The country of Brazil has a river of silver, which when the tide overflows and recedes leaves the ground covered with silver sand.

《金石類·銀異 Marvels of Silver》

1
○虛丹曰：銀積久，蟲食之，煎糞亦成銀。又有飛去者，徐鉉稽神錄言桐城雙港風吹錢過市。中履曰：張雛隱集有飛鏹行，言庚子歲穗城眾見鏹數萬錠飛于空中，從南而北，有方將軍焚香拜之，飛下近簷，兒童群笑，竟復飛去。
○ Xudan says: When silver accumulates long, insects eat it, and when their dung is smelted it also becomes silver. There is also silver that flies away; Xu Xuan's Records of Investigating Spirits says that at Shuanggang in Tongcheng the wind blew coins across the market. Zhonglü says: The collected works of Zhang Chuyin have a "Song of the Flying Ingots," which says that in the gengzi year the people of Suicheng saw tens of thousands of silver ingots flying in the air from south to north; a General Fang burned incense and bowed to them, and they flew down near the eaves, but when the children all laughed they flew away again.

《金石類·識銀法 Method of Assaying Silver》

1
○今之錠，古所謂一金也。二十四銖為一兩，今三倍之。京槽錠每四兩，南京山銀錠每二兩，瓜洲錠每三兩，餘杭亦然，但無邊耳。錠面有鐵，名曰洒沙，此燕京槽也。山銀自九三至七成。高者中有白孔，翦之，其口白，與紋者同。白孔有掩者，中有五分鋻鉛鋻音慳。白絲者九五，高白絲九六，面醜而心美，然亦紅根，其紅有光，以此為驗。閩中絲粗，多是九八。瓜洲為上，良工推焉。粵東如碗，圓而高邊者也。匠之烙印在邊者佳，絲不及心，以印蓋之。凡紋者翦之，其夾口滑而至底；偽者燒熱而翦之，亦滑至底，但偽口太光，紋口若有毛路者然。或視其腳，如瓦灰者，七八成也；如馬牙者，九成也；九六以上，其邊獨白，名曰粉邊。此外有領絲，有敲絲，有畫絲，有吹絲。紋者絲旋及心，十二成者旋絲之上又有小旋。其以鉛煎灰池為餅者，面有布心，或名菊花，底有圓珠者，紋也。大錠色高，小錠雖紋不及也。元寶改小錠，五十可以出一，仍至足也。小錠十成，傾大錠止得九八。視其夾口，名芋頭色。其底之孔，有如蜂窠碎小而深者佳，淺而大者低；有埂脊者佳，光平者低。傾銷取面好則底醜，底好則面醜，取夾口則底與面皆不可觀，惟足則備。其最巧偽者曰鑽鋻，曰釣角。鑽鋻者，從孔而鑽空之，入鉛而滴珠，封其穴。釣角者，釣銅于四角，中分翦之，亦不見也。試之如旋駝螺焉，轉而平者佳，左右敧仄，中必有故。有峱銀焉，白銅而藥煮之。貼錫，其最拙者也。澒銀作書帕式，白與紋等，一傾之則十得六耳。大抵紋則光活，偽則光死。新傾色青，埋入土者色黃。以礬澒點白，或煤白者，杉木急磨之，即紅見矣。凡傾必以硼砂吹之，入錫則銀飛入爐吹藥之中，補之。中通曰：銀簪、銀杯以試金石試之，其色自別。金簪久戴即有輕少，銀不耗也。聽聲亦辨。暄曰：炒之，黑白辨。
○ The present-day ingot is what antiquity called "one metal." Twenty-four zhu made a tael, and now it is three times that. The capital trough ingot is four taels each, the Nanjing mountain-silver ingot two taels, and the Guazhou ingot three taels, and Yuhang the same, only without a rim. Iron on the face of the ingot is called sprinkled sand; this is the trough of Yanjing. Mountain silver ranges from ninety-three to seventy percent. In high-grade pieces there are white holes inside, and when cut the cut is white, the same as fine silver. When the white holes are covered over, there is five fen of jian lead jian is pronounced qian inside. White-thread silver is ninety-five, high white-thread ninety-six, with ugly face and fine heart, yet also with red roots, the red having a gloss, which is the test. In Min the threads are coarse, mostly ninety-eight. Guazhou's is the best, esteemed by good craftsmen. That of eastern Yue is like a bowl, round with a high rim. Those whose craftsman's stamp is on the rim are good; where the threads do not reach the heart, they cover it with a stamp. When fine silver is cut, the cut is smooth to the bottom; counterfeits cut while hot are also smooth to the bottom, but the counterfeit cut is too glossy, while a true cut looks as if it had hairlines. Or look at its foot: if like tile ash it is seventy or eighty percent; if like horse teeth, ninety percent; above ninety-six the rim alone is white, which is called powder rim. Besides these there are collar threads, knocked threads, drawn threads and blown threads. In fine silver the threads spiral to the heart, and in the hundred-and-twenty-percent kind there are small spirals above the spiral threads. Of cakes made by smelting with lead in the ash-pit, those with a cloth-heart face, also called chrysanthemum, and round beads on the bottom are fine. Large ingots are of high grade, and small ingots, even if fine, do not match them. When a yuanbao is recast into small ingots, one in fifty can be gained and still be full weight. Small ingots of a hundred percent recast into large ingots give only ninety-eight. Looking at the cut, it is called taro colour. As for the holes in the bottom, those like a honeycomb, small and deep, are good, and shallow and large ones low; those with ridges are good, and smooth flat ones low. In casting, if the face is good the bottom is ugly, if the bottom is good the face is ugly, and if one aims at the cut, neither bottom nor face is presentable; only full fineness has everything. The most ingenious counterfeits are called drilled jian and hooked corners. Drilled jian is drilled hollow through a hole, filled with lead dripped in beads, and the hole sealed. Hooked corners has copper hooked into the four corners, and even when it is cut in half it does not show. Test it like spinning a top: if it spins level it is good; if it tilts left and right, there must be something inside. There is nao silver, which is white copper boiled with drugs. Tin plating is the clumsiest. Mercury silver is made in the shape of a letter-handkerchief, white like fine silver, but when melted down one gets only six in ten. In general fine silver has a living lustre and counterfeit a dead lustre. Newly cast it is bluish, and buried in the earth it is yellow. Silver whitened with alum and mercury, or with coal, rubbed quickly with fir wood shows red at once. All casting must be blown with borax; if tin gets in, the silver flies into the blowing drugs of the furnace, and must be made up. Zhongtong says: Test silver hairpins and cups on the touchstone and the colour itself tells. Gold hairpins worn long become lighter, but silver does not wear away. One can also tell by listening to the sound. Xuan says: Roast it, and black and white are distinguished.

《金石類·銅 Copper》

1
○銅有白赤，加倭鉛與盧甘石者皆黃，以錫則響，以砒汞而點白者，又非原白也。黃香不詳所出，飛煉為霜，能令銅白如銀。銅耎為線，若甘草煮銅則硬。
○ Copper is white or red; with zinc or calamine added it is all yellow, with tin it rings, and that whitened with arsenic and mercury is not originally white. Yellow fragrance — its origin is unknown — sublimed into frost can make copper white as silver. Copper is soft enough to draw into wire, but boiled with licorice it becomes hard.

《金石類·洗舊銅法 Method of Washing Old Copper》

1
○銅器舊黑，用香餅末醋塗，過夜，明日水草一洗，則新。
○ When copperware is old and black, smear it with powdered incense cake and vinegar, leave it overnight, wash it with water and grass the next day, and it is new.

《金石類·啞銅 Muting Copper》

1
○欲使銅無聲者，以蔥、蒜、韭實煆伏南番乳香成汁，合硼砂以淬銅，則啞。
○ To make copper soundless, calcine southern frankincense with scallion, garlic and chive seeds into a liquid, combine with borax and quench the copper in it, and it is mute.

《金石類·碎銅法 Methods of Breaking Copper》

1
○荸薺嚼錢，可使成泥；以胡桃嚼之，亦碎。河北人言地梨碎銅盆。
○ Chewing a coin with water chestnut can turn it to mud; chewed with walnut it also breaks. People of Hebei say that ground pears break copper basins.

《金石類·銅錫鑄劍 Swords Cast of Copper and Tin》

1
○江淹言古以銅錫為兵器，引昆吾之劍為銅，歐冶合赤墐之錫、若邪之銅而鑄純鉤，汲塚中得銅劍，淹為吳興令，鑿池得銅箭鏑，又有人得銅斧，其徵也。愚者曰：鉛錫入赤銅而熏煉之，其利鋒乃出。今又有作銅刀者，入錫則響。凡響銅磨之，即可剔頭。贊寧曰：煉時童男女以水灌銅，銅自分兩段，凸起者牡，凹者牝也。干將、莫邪以此分乎？
○ Jiang Yan said that in antiquity weapons were made of copper and tin, citing the sword of Kunwu as copper; Ouye combined the tin of Chijin and the copper of Ruoye to cast the Chungou; a copper sword was found in the Ji tomb; when Yan was magistrate of Wuxing a copper arrowhead was found in digging a pond, and someone else found a copper axe — these are the proofs. The Foolish One says: Only when lead and tin are put into red copper and smoked and refined does its sharp edge come out. Nowadays there are also those who make copper knives; with tin added they ring. All ringing bronze, when ground, can shave the head. Zanning says: When refining, boys and girls pour water on the copper, and it divides of itself into two parts, the convex one male and the concave one female. Were Ganjiang and Moye divided in this way?

《金石類·鐵 Iron》

1
○王延德高昌行記言礪石中寶鐵。哈密衛志云：礪石謂之吃鐵石，剖之得鑌鐵。今有旋螺花者，有芝麻雪花者。凡刀劍礱明，以金絲礬礬之，其花即見；偽者則是黑花。甘肅有錠鐵，青黑色，能刻銅石，可煆作繞指劍。舊傳貘糞者，以其食銅鐵也。沈存中至磁州鍛坊，識百煉真鋼，非世間之灌鋼也。中通曰：南方以閩鐵為上，廣鐵次之，楚鐵止可作鋤。燒淬刀口，色白，再烘之為喜鵲青，乃剛。
○ Wang Yande's Record of a Journey to Qocho speaks of precious iron in whetstones. The Gazetteer of Hami Garrison says: The whetstone is called iron-eating stone, and when split it yields bin iron. Nowadays there are kinds with spiral-snail patterns and kinds with sesame or snowflake patterns. When knives and swords are polished bright and treated with gold-thread alum, the pattern shows at once; in counterfeits it is a black pattern. 《金石類·錫 Tin》

1

The Treatise on Curiosities: grass accumulated for three years and then burnt has its juices flow down to form lead and tin; tried, it worked.

《金石類·制汞法 Methods of Controlling Mercury》

1
○淮南曰：青澒五百年為白澒，此言連鉛久而生汞也。礦砂殺之，膽礬結之，鉛凝之，紫河車伏之，川椒、茶葉收之，唾嚼棗亦斂之。灌尸如生。沈括曰：汞得硫則赤如丹，得礬則白如雪。虛丹子曰：本草言金銀銅鐵置汞上則浮，此非也，銅鐵則浮，金銀則沈。金銀取出必輕耗，以其蝕也。走馬吸水銀與走馬射阿魏，皆奇其說耳。或言瓦楞帽可盛汞，未試。
○ The Huainanzi says: Green mercury after five hundred years becomes white mercury; this means that when joined with lead for a long time it produces mercury. Ore sand kills it, chalcanthite binds it, lead congeals it, placenta subdues it, Sichuan pepper and tea leaves gather it, and saliva-chewed jujubes also contract it. Poured into a corpse, it keeps it as if alive. Shen Kuo says: Mercury with sulphur turns red like cinnabar, and with alum white like snow. Xudanzi says: The Materia Medica says that gold, silver, copper and iron put on mercury float; this is wrong, for copper and iron float but gold and silver sink. Gold and silver taken out are surely lighter, because it corrodes them. "A galloping horse sucking up mercury" and "a galloping horse shooting asafoetida" are both strange sayings. Some say a tile-ridged hat can hold mercury; this has not been tried.

《金石類·汞成銀朱輕粉法 Methods of Turning Mercury into Vermilion and Calomel》

1
○胡演秘訣：用石亭脂二升，新鍋鎔化，以汞一斤炒作青砂頭，不見星，研末，罐盛，石版蓋之，鐵線縳之，鹽泥固濟，大火煆之，取出，貼罐為銀朱，貼口為丹砂。又見一法：用白鉛二兩，汞五兩，硫黃二兩，火硝兩半，伏龍肝三錢，共研細末，入罐封固，升五炷香，冷定取出，擂碎，即水花朱。其用汞、鹽、白礬，礬倍之者，升為輕粉。今法以皂礬四兩，鹽一兩，焰硝五錢為麴，復以麴倍汞，加礬十之一，升之。銀朱還原法：不拘舊朱漆器皆可還，但入罐升之，自然飛如粉，以羽拂下，即成水銀。于湖銀朱用汞升，樟樹銀朱乃碌碎丹砂近赭黑者所為也，升以聶公廟前水則多四兩。又碾蚌蛤粉而以蘇木屢染之，曰里度珠；其入鐵沙以重之，曰鐵沙珠。
○ Hu Yan's Secret Formulas: Melt two sheng of sulphur in a new pan, fry a jin of mercury in it into green sand-heads until no specks are seen, grind it to powder, put it in a jar covered with a stone slab, bind it with iron wire, seal it with salt-mud, fire it strongly and take it out; what sticks to the jar is vermilion and what sticks to the mouth is cinnabar. Another method is seen: two taels of white lead, five taels of mercury, two taels of sulphur, a tael and a half of saltpetre and three qian of stove-earth, ground together to a fine powder, sealed in a jar and sublimed for the time of five incense sticks; when cool, take it out and pound it, and it is water-flower vermilion. That using mercury, salt and alum, with twice as much alum, sublimes into calomel. The present method makes a "ferment" of four taels of green vitriol, a tael of salt and five qian of saltpetre, takes twice as much ferment as mercury, adds a tenth part of alum, and sublimes it. Method of restoring vermilion: any old vermilion-lacquered ware can be restored; just put it in a jar and sublime it, and it naturally flies up like powder, which, brushed down with a feather, becomes mercury. The vermilion of Yuhu is sublimed from mercury; the vermilion of Zhangshu is made by grinding cinnabar that is nearly ochre-black, and sublimed with water from in front of the Nie Gong temple it gains four taels. Also, clam-shell powder ground and repeatedly dyed with sappanwood is called lidu vermilion, and that with iron sand added to make it heavy is called iron-sand vermilion.

《金石類·鉛成黃丹胡粉法 Methods of Turning Lead into Red Lead and White Lead》

1
○獨狐滔丹房鏡源炒丹法：鉛一斤，土硫黃十兩，消石一兩，鎔鉛粉，下醋點之，沸時下硫黃，少頃下消，沸定再點醋，依前下少許消黃，待為末，則成黃丹矣。依何子元所說，則作粉不盡者，以消石、礬石炒成丹者也。還鉛粉法：用連須蔥白汁拌丹，慢煎，傾出，即還鉛。蘇恭、朱震亨謂炒錫作用粉，誤矣。取鉛之處，人多受其毒，或成黃腫，本草言鉛無毒，何耶？潛老夫曰：凡鉛銅中皆有銀。從雲責來赤銅有光，乃未取煉者也。石灰十六兩赤銅從舶來，廣者石不過九兩，以取出也。赤鍋一石可取白銀四兩，鉛每斤可取銀澒五分。有此二物，其鉛乃可取霜；若為人取去此物者，則取霜不出，負版鐵鉛亦取不出。辰州志曰：安鉛于甑內，以醋瓶封，化粉。墨子言禹作粉。張華曰紂。創物之智，在古為難，後因而加捷耳。范成大曰：桂州粉以黑鉛著糟甕中罨化之。何孟春曰：嵩陽以鉛塊縣酒缸內，七七日開之，化粉。韶州粉甕蒸之不白，以蘿卜甕上蒸之，即白。
○ Dugu Tao's Mirror Source of the Alchemist's Chamber, method of frying red lead: a jin of lead, ten taels of native sulphur and a tael of saltpetre; melt the lead powder, drip vinegar into it, add the sulphur when it boils, after a moment add the saltpetre, and when the boiling stops drip in vinegar again, adding a little saltpetre and sulphur as before; when it turns to powder it is red lead. According to He Ziyuan, what does not fully become powder is fried into red lead with saltpetre and alum stone. Method of restoring lead powder: mix the red lead with juice of scallion whites with the roots, simmer and pour out, and it returns to lead. Su Gong and Zhu Zhenheng say that powder is made by frying tin, which is wrong. Where lead is mined, people often suffer from its poison and some get yellow swelling; why then does the Materia Medica say lead is not poisonous? Old Man Qian says: All lead and copper contain silver. Red copper brought from Yunze that is glossy has not had [its silver] extracted. Sixteen taels of lime[-like] red copper come by ship, while that of Guang has no more than nine taels of stone, because [the silver] has been extracted. A shi of red pot [metal] can yield four taels of white silver, and each jin of lead five fen of silver-mercury. With these two things the lead can yield frost; if someone has extracted these things, no frost can be got, and the lead of fubanzhi iron cannot be got either. The Gazetteer of Chenzhou says: Put lead in a steamer, seal it with a bottle of vinegar, and it turns to powder. Mozi says that Yu made powder. Zhang Hua says it was Zhòu. The wisdom of creating things was difficult in antiquity, and later people merely followed and made it quicker. Fan Chengda says: The powder of Guizhou is made by putting black lead in a jar of lees and covering it until it transforms. He Mengchun says: At Songyang lumps of lead are hung in a wine vat, opened after forty-nine days, and turned to powder. The powder of Shaozhou steamed in a jar does not turn white, but steamed with radish on top of the jar it turns white at once.

《金石類·珍珠 Pearls》

1
○西珠出西洋，北珠出肅慎，東珠豆青白者出東海，南珠則今洛浦、潿川蚌珠也。袁無華曰：自雷廉接交趾千里，六池出，斷望者上，次竹林，次楊梅，次平山，至汙泥下矣。南越志分大品、璫珠、走珠、滑珠、磊珂珠。其云馬價珠，則甸子也乘雅以馬價作珠，非。青珠、琅玕如珊瑚，瑟瑟碧珠，有藥成者。照乘、辟寒記事，非盡蚌出。陸佃曰：龍珠在頷，蛇珠在口，鮫珠在皮，鱉珠在甲，蛛珠在腹。如意寶珠即魚眼睛也。中通曰：通見珠紅色，名曰血珠，殆張說所云紺珠乎？東坡言其祖光祿云：見大蜂觸網，與蜘蛛相螫，墮地化石，蠻賈曰：是破露珠。

Lu Dian says: The dragon's pearl is under its chin, the snake's pearl in its mouth, the shark's pearl in its skin, the turtle's pearl in its shell, and the spider's pearl in its belly. The wish-fulfilling jewel is the eye of a fish. Zhongtong says: The red tongjian pearl, called blood pearl — is it the gan pearl spoken of by Zhang Yue? Dongpo said that his grandfather, the Grand Master for Splendid Happiness, said he saw a large bee hit a web and sting it out with the spider, and they fell to the ground and turned to stone, and a foreign merchant said: This is a broken-dew pearl.

《金石類·養珠法 Method of Cultivating Pearls》

1
○意蘭國珠最上，土人取海蚌置日中曬之，其口自開，則珠光瑩；其剖蚌出珠，則黯黯矣。宋陳襄文昌雜錄曰：以蚌質作圓珠，復取大蚌，清水盆浸，俟大蚌口開而投之，頻換清水，夜置月中，蚌蛤采玩月華，數月即成真珠。近日廣有洋珠，大如豆，竟似夜光，但易碎，又輕耳。又名玻璃珠，其中空。
○ The pearls of Ceylon are the best; the natives take sea mussels and dry them in the sun, and they open their mouths of themselves, and then the pearls are lustrous; if the mussels are cut open to take out the pearls, they are dull. Lately in Guang there are foreign pearls, as large as beans, quite like night-shining pearls, but easily broken and light. They are also called glass pearls, and are hollow inside.

《金石類·水晶 Rock Crystal》

1
○倭水晶有青、紅、白三色。信州水晶色濁。北水晶緇色。閩中多產銀晶，有黃紫者。水晶筍，其起者也。蔡絛云：政和間，伊陽山崩，出水晶。餘賡之老師言：水晶所在，夜有火光，日從而掘之。水晶埋土中久則化。中通曰：紅者日火晶，可取火；白者曰水晶，可取水，亦可取火。宋劉跂睱日記：杜知叔言故案牘暗者，取水晶承日照之，即見。暄曰：以砒、磽硝、米皮、茄兜埋晶，燒面刻之，甚軟，刻玉同。中德曰：餘賡之太老師諱颺，崇禎丁丑進士，老父以已卯出其門。

Zhongde says: My great-teacher Yu Gengzhi, personal name Yang, became a metropolitan graduate in the dingchou year of Chongzhen, and my father passed under him in the jimao year.

《金石類·玻璃琉璃 Glass and Glazes》

1

《金石類·寶石不一 Gems Are of Many Kinds》

1
○古里、三佛齊、安南、錫蘭山寶石藏璞中，有生水中者。中含活光一縷，煮酒色者，曰貓睛，大者虎睛，光焰閃鑠，亦有豆青、湖水色、黑色。其無活光曰裸子、蜻蜓頭、走水石。其紅璃即靺鞨也，亦曰紅鴉琥，薄曰別者，反其紫鴉琥，即錦鱗翅，其嫩者曰刺兒撇阿劑，紅黑黃曰孔木璃。黃鴉琥即木難，升庵以木難為徂馬琭。畷耕錄以明綠色為助木刺，今訛呼祖母綠者也，一名蛇見怕。淡綠曰撒浡泥，其性軟。青鴉琥之淡者曰未藍，屋撲你藍。白鴉琥曰水精。混白曰羊精，即金剛石之類。外紀曰：則意蘭島，其河生貓精、昔泥紅、金剛石。偽者番燒也，然磨之久則有漿出，寶璃磨不出漿。滇亦有之。又有回回甸子、河西甸子、襄陽甸子、五色璜石、金坑石、銅坑石、硝子石。寶石井中氣衝人，易病。
○ The gems of Calicut, Srivijaya, Annam and Ceylon are hidden in the matrix, and some grow in water. Those holding a thread of living light within, the colour of boiled wine, are called cat's eyes, and the large ones tiger's eyes, flashing and sparkling; some are bean-green, lake-water colour or black. Those without living light are called luozi, dragonfly-head and running-water stone. The red li is the mohe, also called red yahu; the thin kind called bie is the reverse; the purple yahu is brocade-scale wing, its tender kind called la'erpiejiji, and the red, black and yellow called kongmuli. The yellow yahu is the munan; Sheng'an took the munan to be zumalu. The Records Made while Resting from the Plough call the bright green kind zhumula, which is now miscalled "grandmother's green," also known as "snake-sees-and-fears." The pale green is called sabuni, and is soft by nature. The pale kind of blue yahu is called weilan, or wupuni blue. The white yahu is called rock crystal. The cloudy white is called yangjing, of the diamond kind. The Record of the Outer Regions says: In the island of Ceylon the rivers produce cat's eyes, xini red and diamonds. Counterfeits are fired by foreigners, but if ground long a slurry comes out, while true gems give no slurry when ground. Dian has them too. There are also Muslim turquoise, Hexi turquoise, Xiangyang turquoise, five-coloured huang stone, gold-pit stone, copper-pit stone and saltpetre stone. The vapour in gem wells strikes people and easily makes them ill.

《金石類·玉 Jade》

1
○于闐玉河有白、綠、烏玉。永樂時打魯哇貢玉璞，即于闐也。西夏、五台亦出玉。宋宣和殿有玉等子。白玉如酥，所云羊脂也；餐色、油色、雪花次之；有飯糝、漿水玉、沙子玉，乃子兒玉也；有甘黃、甘青、菜墨，則賤。屑金之翡翠，碧玉也，曰洋碧，有墨點，雲南碧不及也。大中十二玉，有如意寶珠，琅玕玉也。別寶經云：以璞映燈，內有紅光，即知為玉。石似玉者，夷陵碔砆，有驚星；其紅者克西瑪瑙、茅山石、保定石、易州似玉石、太白無脂。罐子玉則藥燒成者矣。古玉有血沁、尸沁，有墨古、渠古、甄古、土古，以包漿為貴。有舊料新作者，有以汞礦藥塗而鬼白竹葉熏者。
○ The Jade River of Khotan has white, green and black jade. In the Yongle period Dalu'a sent jade matrix as tribute; this is Khotan. Xixia and Wutai also produce jade. The Xuanhe Hall of the Song had jade scales. White jade like butter is what is called mutton fat; meal-coloured, oil-coloured and snowflake jade are next; there are rice-grain, starch-water and sand jade, which are pebble jade; sweet yellow, sweet green and vegetable-black are cheap. Gold-flecked kingfisher [jade] is green jade, called foreign green, with black spots; the green of Yunnan does not equal it. Of the twelve jades of the Dazhong period, the wish-fulfilling jewel was langgan jade. The Classic of Distinguishing Treasures says: Hold the matrix to a lamp, and if there is red light inside, one knows it is jade. Stones resembling jade are the wufu of Yiling, with startled stars; the red kinds are kexi agate, Maoshan stone, Baoding stone, the jade-like stone of Yizhou, and Taibai fatless. Jar jade is made by firing with drugs. Ancient jade has blood-seep and corpse-seep, and there are ink-old, channel-old, pot-old and earth-old, the patina being most valued. There are new pieces made from old material, and pieces smeared with mercury-ore drugs and smoked ghost-white with bamboo leaves.

《金石類·硝皆地出 All Saltpetre Comes out of the Earth》

1
○東壁分水火二種。愚者曰：大溲蝕土物久者，可煉取硫；小溲蝕土物久者，可煉取硝；則硝乃鹼氣所成者也。凡五更掃潔地，皆可取硝，而偃牆邊為易取耳，是同出而二用也。中通曰：黃嘉卿云：客買皮硝與硝石不同價，見可云：虔州以馬溺、冬瓜皮煮火硝作皮硝用。皮硝可升為玄明粉，不可作火藥，乃河南、山東所出平地自生者也。火硝是煎牆廁土而成者也。以萊菔提硝，則為馬牙白硝。
○ Dongbi divides it into two kinds, water and fire. The Foolish One says: Earth long eaten by faeces can be refined to take sulphur, and earth long eaten by urine can be refined to take saltpetre, so saltpetre is formed by alkaline qi. Whenever clean ground is swept at the fifth watch saltpetre can be taken, but it is easiest to take beside low walls; thus they come from the same source with two uses. Zhongtong says: Huang Jiaqing says that when traders buy pixiao it is not the same price as saltpetre; Jianke says that in Qianzhou they boil fire saltpetre with horse urine and wax-gourd skin to use as pixiao. Pixiao can be sublimed into xuanming powder but cannot make gunpowder; it is what forms of itself on the flat ground of Henan and Shandong. Fire saltpetre is made by boiling the earth of walls and latrines. If saltpetre is purified with radish, it becomes horse-tooth white saltpetre.

1
○生西南番，西者白如明礬，南者黃如桃膠，皆煉結成，同砒煆過，有變化，能制汞啞銅，結砂子，銀工所必用也。
○ It comes from the southwestern foreigners; the western kind is white like alum and the southern yellow like peach gum; both are formed by refining, and when calcined with arsenic they undergo transformations, can control mercury and mute copper and bind sand-silver, and are indispensable to silversmiths.

《金石類·焊藥 Solder》

1
○以硼砂合銅為之，若以胡桐汁合銀，堅如石。今玉石刀柄之類，焊藥加銀一分其中，則永堅不脫。試以圓盒口點焊藥于一隅，其藥自走，周而環之，亦一奇也。
○ It is made by combining borax with copper; if combined with silver with euphrates-poplar juice, it is as hard as stone. Nowadays for such things as jade and stone knife handles, if one part of silver is added to the solder, they hold fast for ever and never come loose. Try dotting solder on one spot of the mouth of a round box, and the solder runs of itself all the way round — also a marvel.

《金石類·硫黃 Sulphur》

1
○博物志云：西域使王畼說硫黃出且彌山，去高昌八百里，晝視孔中，上狀如煙，夜如燈，言時氣不和，皆往保此山。西國布那如山皆硫，近硫之洞，可以治病。忽魯謨斯多鹽與硫。日本土多硫黃，不可作灶，必取別島土。魏志云：盤盤國有火山，山旁皆焦，溶流數十里乃凝，即石硫黃。則硫有石、有土、有水結者。益部方物記：水硫黃出資榮州山澗中，秋潦已收，里入布茅水上，流沫擁聚，取而熬之，復投于水而成，號真珠黃。赤即石亭脂，即箕山巢許所服之石流芝也。青即冬結石。藏器載毗南硫黃香。又三佛齊有摩挲石，有光焰，燒作硫氣。
○ The Treatise on Curiosities says: Wang Chang, an envoy to the Western Regions, said that sulphur comes from Mount Qiemi, eight hundred li from Qocho; by day what is seen above the holes is like smoke, and by night like lamps; it is said that when the seasonal qi is out of harmony, all go to take refuge on this mountain. In the West the mountains of Bunaru are all sulphur, and caves near the sulphur can cure illnesses. Hormuz has much salt and sulphur. In Japan the earth has much sulphur and cannot be used for stoves, so earth must be fetched from other islands. The Record of Wei says: The country of Panpan has a volcano, all scorched around it, which flows molten for several dozen li before congealing; this is rock sulphur. Thus sulphur may be of stone, of earth, or formed from water. The Record of the Products of Yi Province: Water sulphur comes from mountain streams in Zi and Rong prefectures; when the autumn floods have subsided, the villagers spread thatch on the water, where the floating foam gathers, take it and boil it, then throw it into water again and it forms, called pearl yellow. The red kind is shitingzhi, the stone-flowing fungus taken by Chao and Xu of Mount Ji. The blue kind is winter-bound stone. Cangqi records the sulphur of Pinan as fragrant. Also, Srivijaya has the mosha stone, which has a flame and when burnt gives off sulphur fumes.

《金石類·礜砒 Yu Stone and Arsenic》

1
○陸放翁言：城固縣礜石數十百石，可立取。千金方以礜、乾姜、烏頭名匈奴露宿丹，今入水者服信。說文：礜出漢中，正與放翁城固合。蠶食之肥，鼠食之死。智以礜即砒，但有白、黃、紫色數種。山海、說文言毒鼠，而開寶本草始有砒，今人不敢合耳。砒出廣信府，曰信，李時珍不載今地名，固一缺也。飛雲云：漢中今不見有礜，果地產有盡而變耶？抑人不知用耶？
○ Lu Fangweng says: Yu stone of Chenggu county can be taken immediately in dozens or hundreds of shi. The Thousand Gold Prescriptions call [a compound of] yu stone, dried ginger and aconite the Xiongnu's sleeping-in-the-open elixir, and nowadays those who go into water take arsenic. The Shuowen: Yu stone comes from Hanzhong, which agrees exactly with Fangweng's Chenggu. Silkworms that eat it grow fat, and rats that eat it die. I, Zhi, take yu stone to be arsenic, of which there are several kinds, white, yellow and purple. The Classic of Mountains and Seas and the Shuowen speak of it poisoning rats, and arsenic first appears in the Kaibao Materia Medica; people today dare not compound it. Arsenic comes from Guangxin prefecture and is called xin; Li Shizhen does not record the present place name, which is certainly an omission. Feiyun says: Yu stone is no longer found in Hanzhong — has the produce of the land really been exhausted and changed? Or is it that people do not know how to use it?

《金石類·礬 Alum》

1
○涅石燒煉而成。桐城過黃泥河十里，即廬江礬地也，久燒廠人無疥。其飛出者胡蝶礬，光明如水晶，山峰者定水解毒，土人貴之。炙地，酒醋布礬，以桑盤覆之，灰壅一日夜，其精飛于盤上者，礬霜也。青礬乃煤石所煉者，本草綱目未載也。樟樹礬場取煤炭之石，石有銅色，謂之銅石，即以細煤燒而圍之，以礬場水淋其咸，入釜重煮，冷則成礬，他處水不能成，或以皂角水淋之。又升為礬紅，曰罐中紅。安福出銅炭，即煤炭中有銅星者也。洋船貴青礬，以能制龍也。青礬廠氣熏人，衣服當之易爛，栽木不茂，惟烏桕樹不畏其氣。
○ It is formed by burning and refining alunite. Ten li past the Huangni River from Tongcheng is the alum ground of Lujiang, and people at the works, after long burning, have no scabies. That which flies out is butterfly alum, bright as rock crystal, and the mountain-peak kind settles water and cures poisons; the locals value it. Heat the ground, spread alum with wine and vinegar, cover it with a mulberry tray, bank it with ash for a day and night, and the essence that flies up onto the tray is alum frost. Green vitriol is refined from coal-stone, and is not recorded in the Compendium of Materia Medica. The alum works of Zhangshu take the stone of coal, which has a copper colour and is called copper stone; they burn it surrounded with fine coal, leach its salts with the water of the alum works, boil it again in a cauldron, and when cool it becomes alum; water from elsewhere will not form it, or it is leached with honey-locust water. It is also sublimed into alum red, called red-in-the-jar. Anfu produces copper charcoal, which is coal with copper specks in it. Foreign ships value green vitriol because it can control dragons. The vapours of green vitriol works smoke people, clothes exposed to them easily rot, and trees planted do not flourish; only the tallow tree does not fear the vapour.

《金石類·鹽 Salt》

1
○咸者，生于火也。地中得火，遇水成鹽，如火然薪木，既已成灰，水淋灌之，即成灰鹵，蓋燥乾之極，遇水即鹹，此其驗也。鹼性下墜。五味辛甘酸苦皆寄草木，獨是鹹味寄于海水，足徵四味浮輕，鹼性沉重矣。蜀中鹽井，先鑿得淡水，深乃得鹽。三晉鹽池，雨多水亦淡，洩去淡水，下乃得鹽。海于地中為最卑，諸鹹就之，積咸既多，淡入亦化，故海中無處非鹽。且火因于日，日遍大地，地中亦無不有鹽也。鹵積則能載物，故淘土為巨器，泛中流，必無沉溺。姚寬曰：煮海試鹵者，杓鹵而置蓮子數枚，三浮三沈者淡，七八浮則淳鹵矣，取其浮而直。閩人以雞子、桃仁試之，飯豆亦可試也。以解池、邛井為上。消物剝煉金銀，宜戎鹽大顆鹽，而洗眼宜青者。梁沭公驗遠貢鹽云：若南燒羊山鹽，文理粗；北燒羊山鹽，文理密，月望收之，其徹如冰，以氈橐煮之。交河出磧下有紫鹽，鮮而甘。李時珍曰：今醫但知青鹽，而不知青赤皆戎鹽也。北戶錄言張掖桃花鹽與傑公紫鹽，即赤鹽也。本草戎鹽南海青、北海赤，謂西海之南北耳。抱朴子作赤鹽法，此嶺南之染成紅鹽也。智按：張畼傳魏餉九種鹽，言黑者療腹脹，胡鹽療目痛，柔鹽療馬瘠，赤鹽、駁、臭鹽、馬齒鹽不中食，則赤黑之性有分矣。又按：元史寧夏有韋紅二鹽，陝西環州紅鹽味甘而價賤，豈南史之訛記，抑古之稱謂通借耶？在魏以極遠者曰胡鹽，在中國則九種鹽皆戎鹽也。光明鹽出鹽州五原鹽池，正方徹明。蘇頌曰：階州出石鹽，生山石中，不假煎煉，甚明瑩。吳錄曰：天竺有新淘水石鹽，如水晶。金幼孜北錄：北有鹽海，出白鹽如水晶。忽魯謨斯在赤道北二十七度，地悉是鹽，否則硫也，有山五色，皆鹽，土人鑿石旋以為器。高昌、鄯善二國平磧，掘七天，見紫鹽，味甜美，其色鮮，食之止痛。泑澤即蒲昌海，龍城千里，其鹽堅剛，能深金之色。昆吾鹽，陸地產，十餘里無水自生，月滿如積雪。又清鹽池，其鹽正方，廣半寸，似石，人耕池旁地，取池水沃種之，去勿回，即生。又張掖池百步，其鹽多少隨月增減。襄沔記云：龍巢山有鹽池，水色白，上有青紫棱，四邊皆青泥，約二十里，冬月草上如霜，嘗之是鹽，世謂鹽花山。又臨邛一火井、一鹽井，以火井火煮鹽井水，斛得鹽四石，若家火則一二斗矣。高州、夔州鹽白，井研鹽黑。山西解池，則南風結成者。天津、花馬池、惠安堡池，皆曬成者，畏雨不畏風。山東煎成，淮北曬成。淮南灶戶取地上草燒灰，敷鹵地，過宿，掠上鐵鐅鐵片而以牛糞為堤，煎出鹵，再煎，加皂角汁，乃成鹽，其地以雨而晴為潤也。廣東稻稈鹽田草燒海燒灰淋煎。浙則編竹塗石灰而煎之，其竹不焦，鹽火同類也。濱州土鹽，煎土草而成。樹鹽則結五嬛鹽敷樹。
○ Saltiness is produced by fire. When the earth contains fire and meets water it forms salt, just as when fire burns firewood and it has turned to ash, water poured through it makes lye, because the utterly dry becomes salty on meeting water; this is the proof. Alkalinity by nature sinks. Of the five tastes, pungent, sweet, sour and bitter are all lodged in plants, while saltiness alone is lodged in sea water, which is sufficient proof that the four tastes are light and floating and alkalinity heavy and sinking. In the salt wells of Shu, fresh water is struck first, and only deeper is salt obtained. In the salt lakes of Shanxi, when there is much rain the water too is fresh; the fresh water is drained off and salt is found below. The sea is the lowest place on earth and all saltiness goes to it; since accumulated saltiness is so great, fresh water entering is also transformed, so there is no place in the sea that is not salty. Moreover, fire derives from the sun, and the sun covers the whole earth, so there is no place in the earth without salt. Accumulated brine can bear things, so a great vessel moulded of earth floating in midstream will never sink. Yao Kuan says: Those who boil sea water test the brine by ladling some out and putting several lotus seeds in it; if three float and three sink it is weak, and if seven or eight float it is strong brine, taking those that float upright. The people of Min test it with eggs and peach kernels, and cooked beans can also be used to test it. The Xie lake and the Qiong wells are the best. For dissolving things and stripping and refining gold and silver, rong salt and large-grain salt are suitable, and for washing the eyes the blue kind. Liang Shugong, examining salt sent as tribute from afar, says: The salt of Southern Shaoyang Mountain is coarse in texture, and that of Northern Shaoyang Mountain fine; collected at the full moon it is clear as ice, and it is boiled in felt bags. Jiaohe produces purple salt from beneath the gravel desert, fresh and sweet. Li Shizhen says: Present-day physicians know only blue salt and not that blue and red are both rong salt. The Record of the Northern Doors says that the peach-blossom salt of Zhangye and the purple salt of Master Jie are red salt. The Materia Medica's rong salt being blue at the South Sea and red at the North Sea refers to the south and north of the Western Sea. Baopuzi's method of making red salt is the salt dyed red in Lingnan. I, Zhi, note: The biography of Zhang Chang says that the Wei presented nine kinds of salt, saying that the black kind treats abdominal distension, Hu salt treats eye pain, soft salt treats emaciation in horses, and red salt, piebald, stinking salt and horse-tooth salt are not fit to eat; so the natures of red and black differ. I also note: The History of the Yuan says that Ningxia has two salts, Wei and red, and that the red salt of Huanzhou in Shaanxi is sweet and cheap; is this a misrecording in the History of the Southern Dynasties, or were ancient names used loosely? In Wei the most distant was called Hu salt, while in China all nine kinds of salt were rong salt. Bright salt comes from the Wuyuan salt lake in Yanzhou, perfectly square and clear. Su Song says: Jiezhou produces rock salt, which grows in mountain rock, needs no boiling or refining, and is very bright and lustrous. The Record of Wu says: India has newly washed water-rock salt, like rock crystal. Jin Youzi's Northern Record: In the north there is a salt sea which produces white salt like rock crystal. Hormuz is twenty-seven degrees north of the equator, and its land is all salt, or else sulphur; there are mountains of five colours, all salt, and the natives carve the rock and turn it into vessels. In the flat deserts of the two countries of Qocho and Shanshan, digging for seven days one finds purple salt, sweet and delicious, fresh in colour, which stops pain when eaten. Youze is Lake Puchang, a thousand li from Longcheng; its salt is hard and can deepen the colour of gold. Kunwu salt is produced on land; for over ten li, without water, it grows of itself, and at the full moon it is like piled snow. Also, at the Qing salt lake the salt is perfectly square, half an inch wide, like stone; people till the land beside the lake and water what they plant with the lake water, and if they leave without turning back, it grows. Also, the Zhangye lake is a hundred paces across, and its salt increases and decreases with the moon. The Record of Xiang and Mian says: Mount Longchao has a salt lake whose water is white, with blue-purple ridges on top and blue mud all around, about twenty li; in winter the grass is covered as if with frost, and tasted it is salt; it is called Salt Flower Mountain. Also, Linqiong has a fire well and a salt well; when the water of the salt well is boiled with the fire of the fire well, a hu yields four shi of salt, while with household fire it is only one or two dou. The salt of Gaozhou and Kuizhou is white, and that of Jingyan black. The Xie lake of Shanxi is formed by the south wind. Tianjin, Huama lake and Hui'anbao lake are all formed by sun-drying, and fear rain but not wind. Shandong's is boiled and Huaibei's sun-dried. The salt households of Huainan take grass from the ground, burn it to ash, spread it on the brine ground, and after a night scrape it up into iron pans iron sheets with dikes of cow dung, boil out the brine, boil it again with honey-locust juice added, and it becomes salt; their land is moist when it clears after rain. Guangdong has rice-straw salt field straw is burnt with sea [water], and the ash leached and boiled. In Zhe they weave bamboo, coat it with lime and boil in it, and the bamboo does not scorch, because salt and fire are of the same kind. The earth salt of Binzhou is made by boiling earth and grass. As for tree salt, the salt forms on the tree in five rings.

《金石類·煮成消石法 Method of Boiling to Make Saltpetre》

1
○皇甫謚言石脾與消石煮之如雪，貞白不知石脾為何物。王右軍云：石脾入冰則乾，出水則濕。陳臥子嘗問：入水則干，何自知之？智按：舶來鹽石、消石，以入水而取出則無水滴，久置之，其上自潤耳。蛾眉山有石脾，人無識者。造者以苦參水煮蝴蝶礬、戎鹽，熬乾為石脾；又以苦參煮石脾、朴硝，去滓，以冷水漬一夜，即成消石，可化諸石為水。此與焰消之消石不同。
○ Huangfu Mi said that stone spleen boiled with saltpetre is like snow, and Zhenbai did not know what stone spleen was. Wang Youjun says: Stone spleen becomes dry when put in ice and damp when taken out of water. Chen Wozi once asked: How does one know that it becomes dry when put in water? I, Zhi, note: When the salt stone and saltpetre brought by ship are put in water and taken out, they have no drops of water, but when left long their surface becomes moist of itself. Mount Emei has stone spleen, which no one recognizes. Makers boil butterfly alum and rong salt in sophora water and dry it into stone spleen; then boil stone spleen and mirabilite with sophora, remove the dregs and steep it overnight in cold water, and it becomes saltpetre, which can dissolve all stones into water. This is different from the saltpetre of gunpowder.

《金石類·煉鍮石法 Method of Refining Brass》

1
○崔昉曰：銅一斤，爐甘石一斤，煉之即成鍮石。其真鍮生波斯，如黃金，燒之赤而不黑。然鍮非一種，或曰黃銀，即雄黃銀，通雅載之詳矣。格古要論言：鍮石性高麗者，可磨下石汁塗笙簧上，不匿氣，水庶得不鋪，此亦一種也。唐制有青鍮石帶，遼元皆用之。凡銅與鍮石貼金，欲取下者，以肥皂塗之，燒火中，則金圈在肥皂上。
○ Cui Fang says: A jin of copper and a jin of calamine refined together become brass. True brass grows in Persia, like gold, and when heated turns red and not black. But brass is not of one kind; some call it yellow silver, that is realgar silver, recorded in detail in the Comprehensive Elegance. The Essential Criteria of Antiquities says: Brass of Korean nature can be ground and the stone juice smeared on the reeds of a sheng, so that they do not swallow the breath, and with water they may not need to be spread [with wax]; this is another kind. The Tang regulations had blue brass belts, and the Liao and Yuan used them. Whenever gilding on copper or brass is to be removed, smear it with soap and put it in the fire, and the gold rings off onto the soap.

《金石類·燒石易鑿法 Method of Heating Stone to Make It Easy to Chisel》

1
○萬安張振山開河，夢神與之方，曰：以桐油、石灰與黑豆末之，燒石，則鑿之甚易。因用之，驗。智按：以硫燒之，其石亦易碎。
○ When Zhang Zhenshan of Wan'an was opening a river, a god gave him a method in a dream, saying: Powder tung oil, lime and black beans together and heat the stone with them, and it will be very easy to chisel. He used it and it worked. I, Zhi, note: If it is heated with sulphur the stone also breaks easily.

《金石類·補斷石法 Methods of Mending Broken Stone》

1
○黑娥小錄云：鎔黃白蠟、松香末，同色石粘之。一曰：研生羊肝和麵綴之。一曰：鎔青和石屑補。若玉、瑪瑙、珊瑚等物損拆，研石膏、明礬、磨芨調塗損處，以綿紙井綸絲護束，置湯釜中煮透，俟冷。
○ The Small Record of Mo'e says: Melt yellow and white wax with powdered rosin of the same colour as the stone, and stick it. One says: Grind raw sheep's liver mixed with flour and join it. One says: Melt [qing] and mix it with stone dust to mend it. If jade, agate, coral and the like are broken, grind gypsum, alum and bletilla and mix them to smear the broken part, bind it with cotton paper and well-rope silk, boil it thoroughly in a pot of water, and wait for it to cool.

《金石類·石樂以堅耎膏澀燥鎮為用 Stone Medicines Are Used for Hardness and Softness, Paste and Astringency, Dryness and Settling》

1
○自陶、蘇皆以硬為石膏，耎為寒水石，丹溪乃斷取耎為膏，確矣。愚在平西有一洞山石膏，視之乃方解石也。石體凝止而膏用解釋，故能解肌發汗。今以膏收豆漿，非以其釋而止乎？滑石亦耎，可畫，然在土中耎如泥，見風乃結，虞衡志詳載之。蓬萊縣桂府村亦出滑石，可刻圖書，中有光明黃子為石腦芝，其根為不灰木。醫取利竅，非惟通小水也。瓊萃曰：硬者化之亦自有膏，石無不可化者，鐘乳津汁非膏乎？愚曰：下垂凝澀，與上舍蘊之膏殊矣。石皆寒重，惟硫、礦、礜、砒熱，丹砂、磁、礬則不可以寒熱論也。雄磁虛連數十片鐵，醫以色黑入腎，亦取雄吸；丹砂色赤入心，本取鎮氣養神。孫思邈加神麴為磁朱丸，誰知其理耶？如花蕊澀能止血，青礞鹹墜下痰，礓礰袪腫發逐皮節，空青明目取應膽汁，固各有其類用也。中通曰：阿房宮砌磁門，則御刀不出入耶？萬畢：慈石縣井，亡人自歸，亦吸之意云爾。空青治目，為袪肝火，而以東方甲乙色類之。寒墜，燥化，膏滑，通解，此猶易知也；丹砂則玄矣，蓋善入而引藥靈化也。中履曰：通推載火山不燼木，徐無不灰木，而滑石根亦為不灰木，其同名乎？燕京白爐甚輕，火燒不壞，但畏水耳，是不灰木與天皮同坑，其筋點燈耐久，人所不知。
○ From Tao and Su on, all took the hard kind for gypsum and the soft for calcite, and Danxi decisively took the soft as gypsum, which is right. At Pingxi I had a cave-mountain gypsum, which on inspection was calcite. The body of stone congeals and stops, but gypsum works by releasing, so it can release the flesh and induce sweating. Nowadays gypsum is used to set soy milk — is that not because it releases and then stops? Talc too is soft and can be drawn with, but in the earth it is soft as mud and hardens only when exposed to the wind; the Treatise of the Supervisor of Forests and Waters records it in detail. Guifu village in Penglai county also produces talc, which can be carved into seals; inside it there are bright yellow seeds which are stone-brain fungus, and its root is unburnable wood. Physicians use it to free the orifices, not only to pass urine. Qiongcui says: The hard kind, when dissolved, also has a paste; there is no stone that cannot be dissolved; are not the drippings of stalactites a paste? The Foolish One says: Hanging down and congealing astringently is different from the paste held within above. Stones are all cold and heavy; only sulphur, ore, yu stone and arsenic are hot, and cinnabar, lodestone and alum cannot be discussed in terms of cold and heat. Male lodestone can draw up several dozen pieces of iron in a row; physicians use it because its black colour enters the kidneys, and also for its male attraction; cinnabar's red colour enters the heart, originally used to settle the qi and nourish the spirit. Sun Simiao added medicated leaven to make the lodestone and cinnabar pill — who knows its principle? Thus ophicalcite, being astringent, can stop bleeding, chlorite schist, being salty, brings down phlegm, jiangli reduces swelling and drives out [wind] in the skin and joints, and azurite brightens the eyes by corresponding to the bile — each certainly has its use according to its kind. Zhongtong says: If the Afang palace had gates built of lodestone, then would sword-bearers be unable to go in and out? The Ten Thousand Infallible Arts: hang lodestone in a well and a runaway will come back by himself; this too is the idea of attraction. Azurite treats the eyes by removing liver fire, and is matched with it by the colour of the east, jia and yi. Cold settles, dryness transforms, paste lubricates and penetration releases — these are still easy to know; cinnabar is mysterious, for it penetrates well and leads drugs to transform wondrously. Zhonglü says: The Tongtui records the unconsumed wood of the fire mountain, and Xu Wu the unburnable wood, while the root of talc is also unburnable wood — do they share the name? The white stove of Yanjing is very light, not damaged by fire, fearing only water; this unburnable wood comes from the same pit as heaven-skin, and its fibres used as lamp wicks last long, which people do not know.

《金石類·煎礦 Smelting Ore》

1
○浙福坑場，或採或閉，饒、信、瑞坑從未開也。楚出辰州，貴出銅仁，中州則宜陽趙保山、永寧秋樹坡、盧氏高觜、嵩縣馬槽山，蜀會川密勒山，甘肅大黃山，山東、江北皆有。凡有礦處，山童望氣知之，有深淺耳。開采妨爭，或費工不償，惟滇常行，楚雄、永昌、大理最盛，次曲靖、姚安，次鎮沅。外域多有，皆黃道腰輪下國也。礦砂山見磊砢小褐石，自有脈絡，穴土十丈或倍之，支洞尋苗，或黃碎山，或石縫亂絲，則鐎砂近矣，形如煤炭，下疊石，不甚黑。出土以斗量，高者六七兩一斗，下者一二兩。其鐎砂放光甚者，精華洩漏，得銀偏少。爐築五尺，砂光淘洗，每鐎砂二石，用栗木炭二百斤，牆背鼓鞁，火力既合，鐎鎔成團，然銀猶隱鉛中砂二石鎔團約百斤。冷定，入分金蝦蟆爐，鉛沉下者已類佗僧，柳枝燃照，鉛氣淨盡，則生銀也。傾無絲紋，或現圓星，滇號茶經，入銅少許，乃入槽成絲耳。楚雄所出硐砂百斤，又生鉛二百斤于爐內，然後煽煉成團，其再入蟆爐，沈鉛結銀，同也。爐底佗僧樣者，別入爐煉，又成扁擔鉛。雜銀者，紅銅與鉛也，高爐坩鍋煉之，灑硝而銅鉛就鍋底，曰銀鏽；灰池落者曰爐底。二者填火土甑，鉛就低流，銅黏餘銀，仍可分取。
○ The mines of Zhe and Fu are sometimes worked and sometimes closed, and the mines of Rao, Xin and Rui have never been opened. In Chu it comes from Chenzhou, in Gui from Tongren, in the Central Province from Mount Zhaobao in Yiyang, Qiushupo in Yongning, Gaozui in Lushi and Mount Macao in Song county; in Shu from Mount Mile in Huichuan; in Gansu from Mount Dahuang; and Shandong and north of the Yangzi have it too. Wherever there is ore, the mountain boys know it by watching the qi, only it is deep or shallow. Mining causes disputes, or the labour is not repaid; only Dian works it regularly, Chuxiong, Yongchang and Dali most of all, then Qujing and Yao'an, then Zhenyuan. Foreign lands have much, all countries beneath the waist-wheel of the ecliptic. On ore-sand mountains one sees heaped small brown stones, which have their own veins; one digs ten zhang or twice that into the earth, with side tunnels following the shoots, and where there are yellow broken hills or tangled threads in rock cracks, the jiao sand is near; it is shaped like coal, with layered stone below, not very black. Brought out of the earth, it is measured by the dou: high-grade gives six or seven taels a dou, low-grade one or two taels. Jiao sand that gives off much light has leaked its essence, and yields less silver. The furnace is built five chi high, and the sand is washed till it shines; for every two shi of jiao sand two hundred jin of chestnut-wood charcoal are used, with bellows behind the wall, and when the fire is full the jiao melts into a mass, but the silver is still hidden in the lead two shi of sand melt into a mass of about a hundred jin. When it has cooled and set, it is put into the toad furnace for parting gold, and the lead that sinks is already like litharge; when lit with burning willow twigs and the lead vapour is all gone, there is raw silver. When cast without thread patterns, or showing round stars, it is called tea classic in Dian, and only after a little copper is added and it is put in the trough does it form threads. For the cave sand of Chuxiong, a hundred jin, with another two hundred jin of raw lead, are fanned and smelted into a mass in the furnace, and then put again into the toad furnace, sinking the lead and binding the silver, in the same way. The litharge-like part at the bottom of the furnace is smelted separately in another furnace and becomes carrying-pole lead. What is mixed with silver is red copper and lead; when refined in a tall furnace with a crucible and sprinkled with saltpetre, the copper and lead go to the bottom of the crucible, which is called silver rust, and what falls into the ash-pit is called furnace bottom. These two are put into a fireclay steamer, the lead flows to the low part, and the copper retains the remaining silver, which can still be separated.

《金石類·銅礦 Copper Ore》

1
○銅託體鉛中，銀中亦有不雜銀鉛者。爐須傍通高低二孔，鉛先化，從上流；銅後化，從下出。煉託銀者，銀結于面，銅沈于下。日本方長板銅，漳人再煉取銀而傾餅轉售，是也。蒙山銅最下。宋奉新曰：亦銅以爐甘或倭鉛參和為黃銅銅十斤，爐甘石六斤；用倭鉛四，則紅銅六，以袁郡自風煤炭煉，以砒霜等藥制煉為白銅，礬硝等藥制煉為青銅，廣錫參和為響銅銅八，廣錫二，初質則紅而已。智按：建申初，判度支趙贊采連州白銅鑄大錢；慶歷中，知商州皮仲容采青水青銅鑄錢；今萬歷錢有青銅白沙者，色終不變；宋公何云烏有耶？銅礦如姜如鍮，有銅星，入爐傍溢者為自然銅，亦名石髓鉛。鑄錢加倭鉛，甚至鉛六銅四，則鑄色黑而墮即碎矣。
○ Copper lodges in lead, and there is also copper in silver not mixed with silver or lead. The furnace must have two side holes, high and low; the lead melts first and flows from the upper, and the copper melts later and comes out the lower. In refining copper that holds silver, the silver forms on top and the copper sinks below. The square long plate copper of Japan, which the people of Zhang refine again to take the silver and then cast into cakes for resale, is this. The copper of Mengshan is the poorest. Song of Fengxin says: Copper is also mixed with calamine or zinc to make brass ten jin of copper to six jin of calamine; with four of zinc, six of red copper, refined with the coal of Yuan commandery's Zifeng, refined with arsenic and other drugs to make white copper, refined with alum, saltpetre and other drugs to make bronze, and mixed with Guang tin to make bell metal eight of copper to two of Guang tin; the original substance is merely red. I, Zhi, note: At the beginning of Jianzhong, Zhao Zan as Supervisor of Public Revenue mined the white copper of Lianzhou to cast large coins; in the Qingli period, Pi Zhongrong as prefect of Shangzhou mined the bronze of Qingshui to cast coins; and the Wanli coins of today include bronze white-sand ones, whose colour never changes — how can Master Song call them nonexistent? Copper ore is like ginger or brass, with copper specks; what overflows at the side of the furnace is native copper, also called stone-marrow lead. When coins are cast with zinc added, even six parts lead to four of copper, the casting is black and breaks when dropped.

《金石類·鐵 Iron》

1
○土錠鐵浮者可拾，或耕起而取數寸者，耕後其塊日生，西北甘肅、東南尤溪是錠鐵也。夔京、遵化、山西平陽，則砂鐵也。徼外鑌鐵，則礪石中得者。凡鐵爐用鹽和泥造成，出爐未炒為生鐵，既炒則熟，生熟相煉則鋼。尤溪毛鐵，生也；豆腐鐵，熟也。鎔流時又作方塘留之，灑幹潮泥灰，而持柳棍疾攪，則熟矣。灌鋼以熟片加生鐵，用破草鞋蓋之，泥塗其下，火力鎔滲，取煆再三。又有廣南墮子生鋼，沈括所見其鋼是其類也。淬地溲百煉，又一法也。
○ Ingot iron in the soil can be picked up from the surface, or a few inches of it is turned up by ploughing, and after ploughing the lumps grow daily; that of Gansu in the northwest and of Youxi in the southeast is ingot iron. That of Kuijing, Zunhua and Pingyang in Shanxi is sand iron. The bin iron from beyond the borders is obtained from whetstones. All iron furnaces are built of mud mixed with salt; what comes out of the furnace unpuddled is pig iron, once puddled it is wrought, and pig and wrought refined together make steel. The hairy iron of Youxi is raw, and bean-curd iron wrought. When it flows molten, a square pond is made to hold it, dried damp-mud ash is sprinkled on, and it is stirred quickly with willow poles, and then it is wrought. For poured steel, wrought sheets are added to pig iron, covered with broken straw sandals, smeared below with mud, and the force of the fire melts it through; it is taken out and forged repeatedly. There is also the duozi raw steel of Guangnan; the steel Shen Kuo saw was of this kind. Quenching in earth-urine and refining a hundred times is another method.

《金石類·錫 Tin》

1
○臨賀產錫，方書呼錫為賀。南丹、河池二州最盛，衡、永次，滇錫則遠不致矣。山錫、水錫，又分瓜砂。南丹河內黑色粉粹，淘取煎煉，日可一斤。其爐沙不即鎔，用鉛少許勾引，或以剩灰勾引。其流出潔白，而錘之即裂，必入鉛制，方充造器。其入鉛太多，則鎔化入醋淬八九度，鉛盡化灰而去。嘉興黃錫最精，必以青布濾，費事矣。
○ Linhe produces tin, and the medical books call tin he. Nandan and Hechi prefectures have most, Heng and Yong are next, and the tin of Dian is too far to bring. There is mountain tin and water tin, further divided into melon sand. At Nandan and Henei it is black and powdery, and when panned and smelted yields about a jin a day. When its furnace sand does not melt at once, a little lead is used to draw it, or leftover ash is used to draw it. What flows out is pure white but cracks when hammered, so lead must be added to control it before it is fit for making vessels. If too much lead is added, it is melted and quenched in vinegar eight or nine times, and the lead all turns to ash and goes. The yellow tin of Jiaxing is the finest, but must be filtered through blue cloth, which is laborious.

《金石類·鉛 Lead》

1
○有銀礦鉛滇中多、銅山鉛貴州多、軍鉛，曰草節鉛嘉利州出。雅州出釣腳鉛，如皂廁科斗，生涉中。上饒、樂平出雜銅鉛。劍州出陰平鉛。難更僕也。其曰倭鉛，非礦鉛也，乃爐甘石泥罐火煉而成者，與銅收伏，入火即成煙飛去。
○ There is silver-ore lead much in Dian, copper-mountain lead much in Guizhou, and army lead, called grass-joint lead produced in Jiali prefecture. Yazhou produces hook-foot lead, like the tadpoles of the soap-pod privy, growing in [rivers]. Shangrao and Leping produce mixed copper lead. Jianzhou produces Yinping lead. They are too many to enumerate. What is called Japanese lead is not ore lead, but is made by firing calamine in mud crucibles; combined with copper it is subdued, but in the fire it turns to smoke and flies away.

《金石類·冶鑄 Smelting and Casting》

1
○朝鐘用響銅四萬七千斤，錫四千斤，高丈五尺，徑八尺。埏模既燥，厚附牛油、黃蠟，蠟二油八。凡油蠟一斤虛位，填銅十斤。鑄鐘不可接，像則可以數接為之，銃則內磨為要也。生鐵鑄釜，補綻甚多，惟廢破釜鐵鎔鑄，則無復隙漏。釜成後以輕梜敲之，如木者佳。然惟福山鑄鍋為最，以其薄而光，鎔鐵既精，工法又熟。他處皆厚，必用黃泥、豕油煉之乃可用。刀以建陽平水鋼健。中德曰：青州出鐵，而顏神鎮穿珠燈必資山西鐵絲。
○ The court bell used forty-seven thousand jin of bell bronze and four thousand jin of tin, and was fifteen chi high and eight chi in diameter. When the clay mould has dried, it is thickly coated with ox tallow and yellow wax, two parts wax to eight of tallow. For every jin of tallow and wax in the hollow, ten jin of copper are filled in. A bell cannot be cast in joined sections, but an image can be made in several joined parts, and for a gun the important thing is to grind the inside. Pig-iron cauldrons need much patching; only those cast from melted old broken cauldrons have no cracks or leaks. When a cauldron is made, tap it with a light stick, and if it sounds like wood it is good. But Fushan's cast pans are the best, because they are thin and glossy, the molten iron being fine and the workmanship skilled. Elsewhere they are all thick and must be seasoned with yellow mud and lard before they can be used. For knives, the Pingshui steel of Jianyang is strongest. Zhongde says: Qingzhou produces iron, yet the bead-strung lamps of Yanshen town must depend on Shanxi iron wire.

《金石類·鍜縫 Forge Welding》

1
○鐵性逐節黏合，塗黃泥于接口，入火揮錘，泥滓成枵而去，取其神氣媒合也。大器以細陳壁土撒接口，自合。煤用鐵炭，無媒則用火墨，俗名火矢，揚燒不閒穴火者也。成樂器者，必圓成無焊，其餘用焊藥炙合，以錫末為小焊，響銅末為大焊。焊銀器則用紅銅末，皆兼硼砂。巧焊金玉用銀末，如玉柄鐵刀之類。水銀、鉛、鍚三合，亦成焊藥。
○ Iron by nature joins piece by piece; smear yellow mud on the joint, put it in the fire and swing the hammer, and the mud dross turns hollow and goes, taking its spirit qi as the go-between. For large objects, sprinkle fine old wall earth on the joint and it joins of itself. For coal use iron charcoal; without coal use fire-ink, commonly called fire-dung, which is burnt with bellows and not left idle in the fire-pit. Musical instruments must be made whole without solder; the rest are joined by heating with solder, tin powder for small solder and bell-bronze powder for large solder. For soldering silverware, red copper powder is used, all with borax. For fine soldering of gold and jade, silver powder is used, as for jade-handled iron knives. Mercury, lead and tin combined in three also make solder.

《金石類·健鐵健鋼 Hardening Iron and Hardening Steel》

1
○已經爐錘，出火入清水淬之，曰健；未淬則鋼之弱性猶存也。鎈用純鋼，以羊角末和鹽醋先塗，乃握已健之鋼鏩畫斜文，重燒紅，退冷，入水健之。針條穿鼻，入釜慢火炒之，炒後以土末入松木火矢與豆豉三者罨蓋，下用火蒸，留二三針插外以試火候，外針入手成粉，則蒸針足矣，乃開封入水健之。
○ After the furnace and hammer, taken from the fire and quenched in clear water, it is called hardened; unquenched, the weakness of the steel still remains. Files use pure steel; first smear it with powdered sheep's horn mixed with salt and vinegar, then hold a hardened steel chisel to cut oblique lines, heat it red again, let it cool, and harden it in water. For needles, after the eyes are pierced, put them in a pot and roast them over a slow fire; after roasting, cover them with powdered earth, pine-wood fire-dung and fermented beans, and steam them with fire below, leaving two or three needles stuck outside to test the heat; when the outside needles crumble to powder in the hand, the steaming of the needles is sufficient, and they are unsealed and hardened in water.

《金石類·冷錘 Cold Hammering》

1
○黃銅用爐甘石者，不退火性，受錘；用倭鉛者，出爐退火性，以受冷錘。樂器聲有雌雄，雄者重數錘，俱從冷錘點發。銅經錘後，色成啞白，受鎈複現黃光。凡折鐵十分，銅耗一分，加冷錘者，其質更堅。
○ Brass made with calamine does not lose its fire-nature and can take the hammer; that made with zinc loses its fire-nature on leaving the furnace and so takes cold hammering. The sounds of musical instruments are female and male; the male ones are hammered heavily several times, all brought out by cold hammering. After hammering, copper becomes dull white in colour, and when filed shows yellow lustre again. Whenever iron is folded ten times, copper loses a tenth; with cold hammering its substance is all the harder.

《金石類·羊頭 Sheep's Head》

1
○斧鑿柄受木之孔，先以冷鐵作骨，後以熱鐵包之，冷者不黏，自成空管。
○ For the holes in axes and chisels that take the wooden handles, first make a core of cold iron, then wrap it in hot iron; the cold does not stick, and a hollow tube is formed by itself.
